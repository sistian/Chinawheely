Name Origin

Baicheng means "white mountain" in Chinese, referring to the snow-capped Changbai Mountain that lies on the border with North Korea. The city was formerly known as Hunjiang.

Education & Universities

Baishan Normal University is the main local higher-education institution.

Getting Around

Baishan has no commercial airport; the nearest is Changbaishan Airport near the mountain. Baishan Railway Station and Baishan West Station connect to Shenyang and other cities. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Baishan is rich in forests and mineral resources, including coal and iron. It borders the western slopes of Changbai Mountain and has many rivers fed by mountain snowmelt.

Baishan's Best Photo Spots

Capture the city from the most iconic and photogenic angles.

⛰️ 🌅 Jingshan Park Pavilion

Best at sunrise

Climb to the Wanchun Pavilion for the classic panoramic view over the golden rooftops of the Baishan old town. On clear days, you can see the CCTV tower and the western mountains in the distance. Arrive early to beat the crowds.

🏯 Baishan old town Corner Tower

Best at dusk

The northwest corner tower reflected in the moat is one of Baishan's most photographed scenes. The sunset glow turns the water gold and the tower silhouette dramatic. Your driver can drop you at the northeast or northwest corner for the perfect shot.

🛶 the old quarter old-quarter lanes

Best in late afternoon

The lakeside old-quarter lanes around the lakeside lanes offer a charming mix of old Baishan life, willow trees, and red lanterns. Take a rickshaw ride or wander the narrow alleys, then capture the golden hour light on the water.

🦅 🏙️ CCTV Headquarters

Best at blue hour

Also known as the "Big Pants," this modern architectural icon in the CBD looks best from the plaza below or from across the Third Ring Road. At night, the building is illuminated and reflected in the surrounding towers.

⛰️ 🧱 Baishan local attraction

Best in early morning mist

For photographers seeking a dramatic, less-crowded Baishan old town shot, Baishan old town is unmatched. The wild, unrestored sections and watchtowers winding over mountain ridges create unforgettable sunrise and fog images.

🏯 🎨 798 Art District

Best on a sunny afternoon

Colorful murals, industrial brick buildings, and quirky sculptures make 798 a favorite for creative portraits. Every corner offers a different backdrop, from Soviet-era slogans to contemporary street art.

Baishan Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Baishan like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

The main sight of Baishan rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Baishan

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Baishan

🍵 Tea Houses of Baishan

Tea culture runs through the day in Baishan: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
