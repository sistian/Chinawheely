Northeast China Food Tour Guide

From Harbin's Russian-influenced dumplings to Shenyang's famous Guo Bao Rou -- discover the bold, hearty flavours of China's Dongbei region.

Explore Dongbei Food

Iconic Dongbei Dishes

Dongbei cuisine is defined by cold winters, fertile farmland, and a culture of communal eating. The food is generous, the portions are large, and the flavours are honest and satisfying.

🥩

Guo Bao Rou (锅包肉)

Thick-sliced pork loin coated in potato starch, deep-fried until shatteringly crisp, then tossed in a sweet-sour vinegar sauce. The dish that defines Shenyang -- invented in the Qing dynasty by a Homesick Shandong chef.

🥟

Dumplings (饺子)

Dongbei dumplings are a religion. Hand-made wrappers, generous fillings of pork and cabbage, boiled or pan-fried. In Harbin, try the Russian-style pelmeni (Пельмени) -- larger, with a thicker wrapper.

🍖

Shaobai (烧白)

Pork belly sliced thin, layered with preserved vegetables, steamed until the fat melts into the vegetables. A staple of Shenyang and Dalian comfort food -- rich, salty, and deeply satisfying.

🥗

Cold Dishes (凉菜)

No Dongbei meal begins without cold dishes -- pickled vegetables, shredded potato, cold cucumber in garlic sauce. The fermented sour cabbage (酸菜) is the region's signature flavour.

🍺

Russian-Chinese Fusion

Harbin's unique food heritage -- Russian sausage (红肠), Russian black bread, pelmeni, and cabbage soup. Influenced by 50 years of Russian settlement and trade in the early 20th century.

🍜

Lanzhou-style Hand-Pulled Noodles

Though originally from Gansu, hand-pulled beef noodles have become a Dongbei staple -- the rich bone broth, chewy noodles, and generous serving of beef make it perfect winter food.

🦐

Dalian Seafood

Dalian's location on the Yellow Sea produces exceptional seafood -- sea urchin (海胆), clam, abalone, sea cucumber. Best eaten simply -- steamed, cold-served with soy and sesame oil.

🥬

Pickled Vegetables (酸菜)

The flavour backbone of Dongbei cooking -- Chinese cabbage fermented in salt and spices. Used in hot pot, dumpling fillings, noodle soups, and as a side. No Dongbei kitchen is complete without a clay jar of pickles.

❄️ Harbin: Where Russia Meets China

Harbin's food is unlike anywhere else in China -- a unique Russian-Chinese fusion shaped by 50 years of colonial history, cold winters, and a vibrant food culture that celebrates the best of both worlds.

🥟

Da Long Fang (大龙凤饺子馆)

Established: 1958 (over 65 years)

Specialty: Harbin's most famous dumpling house -- famous for its oversized pelmeni (Russian-style dumplings) and the signature "three-delight" dumplings. An institution that has served generations of Harbin families.

Address: 78 Zhongyang Avenue, Daoli District, Harbin (哈尔滨市道里区中央大街78号)

Nearest Metro: 中央大街站 (Central Avenue Station), Line 2

Must Order: Pelmeni, Three-Delight Dumplings, Cabbage and Pork Dumplings

🍺

Harbin Brewery (哈尔滨啤酒厂)

Established: 1900 (over 124 years)

Specialty: The birthplace of Chinese beer -- Harbin Brewery was founded by Russian brewers in the colonial era. The original factory still produces the premium "Milan" label. Brewery tours available.

Address: 55 Ganghua Street, Daoli District, Harbin (哈尔滨市道里区光华街55号)

Nearest Metro: 工程大学站 (Engineering University Station), Line 1

Must Order: Harbin Premium Beer, Fresh Unpasteurised Beer

🍖

Old Ma's Sausage Shop (老妈妈红肠)

Established: 1973 (over 51 years)

Specialty: Harbin Russian sausage (红肠) -- smoked pork sausage with a garlicky, paprika-kissed casing. A beloved snack and souvenir. Made to a recipe that traces back to Russian sausage-makers of the 1920s.

Address: 12 Zhongyang Avenue, Daoli District, Harbin (哈尔滨市道里区中央大街12号)

Nearest Metro: 中央大街站 (Central Avenue Station), Line 2

Must Order: Russian Sausage (Red sausage), Smoked Bacon

🥟

Fu Jiu Man (福九满)

Established: 1988 (over 36 years)

Specialty: Upscale Harbin cuisine with a focus on local game -- venison, wild boar, and river fish. The restaurant is famous for its "ice and snow" themed dishes using local ingredients in creative ways.

Address: 88 Youyi Road, Nangang District, Harbin (哈尔滨市南岗区友谊路88号)

Nearest Metro: 铁路局站 (Railway Bureau Station), Line 1

Must Order: Steamed Venison Meatballs, Frozen Fish in Ice

🏯 Shenyang: Capital of Liaoning Cuisine

Shenyang (Mukden) was the capital of the Qing dynasty before Beijing. The food reflects that imperial heritage -- rich, elaborate, and deeply satisfying. Guo Bao Rou was born here.

🥩

Old Guan Li (老关里)

Established: 1946 (over 78 years)

Specialty: Shenyang's most famous Guo Bao Rou restaurant -- serving the dish for over 75 years with the original family recipe. The Guo Bao Rou here is perfectly balanced -- crisp exterior, tender interior, sweet-sour sauce.

Address: 88 Zhongjie Road, Shenhe District, Shenyang (沈阳市沈河区中街路88号)

Nearest Metro: 中街站 (Zhongjie Station), Line 1/2

Must Order: Guo Bao Rou, Sliced Pork with Sauerkraut, Steamed Buns

🥟

Shen Jiao Wang (沈饺王)

Established: 1972 (over 52 years)

Specialty: Handmade dumplings in the old Shenyang style -- larger than average, with thick wrappers and generous pork-cabbage filling. Open kitchen where you can watch the dumplings being made.

Address: 23 Sanhao Street, Heping District, Shenyang (沈阳市和平区三好街23号)

Nearest Metro: 三好街站 (Sanhao Street Station), Line 2

Must Order: Pork & Cabbage Dumplings, Pan-Fried Dumplings

🍖

Xinghua Yuan (兴华园)

Established: 1956 (over 68 years)

Specialty: Traditional Manchu-style hot pot with lamb. The broth is based on a centuries-old recipe, served with thin-sliced lamb, sesame sauce, and fermented cabbage. A favourite of Shenyang locals for generations.

Address: 88 Huanghe Street, Huanggu District, Shenyang (沈阳市皇姑区黄河大街88号)

Nearest Metro: 黄河大街站 (Huanghe Street Station), Line 2

Must Order: Manchu Lamb Hot Pot, Lamb Slices, Fermented Cabbage

🌊 Dalian: Fresh From the Yellow Sea

Dalian's location on the Yellow Sea means exceptional seafood is the star -- sea urchin, abalone, clam, sea cucumber, and the famous Dalian crab. Simply prepared to honour the freshness.

🦐

Yuan J Seafood (袁记海鲜)

Established: 1965 (over 59 years)

Specialty: Dalian's most famous seafood restaurant -- famous for its steamed sea urchin (海胆), salt & pepper mantis shrimp, and live crab prepared three ways (steamed, stir-fried, and in congee).

Address: 88 Victory Road,中山区, Dalian (大连市中山区胜利路88号)

Nearest Metro: 港湾街站 (Gangwan Street Station), Line 2

Must Order: Steamed Sea Urchin, Salt & Pepper Mantis Shrimp, Steamed Dalian Crab

🥟

Jin Guo (金锅饺子)

Established: 1979 (over 45 years)

Specialty: Dalian-style dumplings with a seafood twist -- shrimp dumplings, crab meat dumplings, and the signature squid-ink dumplings. A local institution with a loyal following.

Address: 33 Jiefang Road, Xigang District, Dalian (大连市西岗区解放路33号)

Nearest Metro: 人民广场站 (People's Square Station), Line 2

Must Order: Shrimp Dumplings, Crab Meat Dumplings, Squid Ink Dumplings

🍲

Wang's House (王家大院)

Established: 1988 (over 36 years)

Specialty: Dalian home-style cooking with a focus on seafood -- stir-fried clam with black bean sauce, steamed sea bass with ginger, and the restaurant's signature crab roe scrambled eggs.

Address: 58 Xinghai Bay, Ganjingzi District, Dalian (大连市甘井子区星海湾58号)

Nearest Metro: 星海广场站 (Xinghai Square Station), Line 1

Must Order: Crab Roe Scrambled Eggs, Clam with Black Bean, Steamed Sea Bass
