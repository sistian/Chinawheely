The 4 dishes below are the ones Nanchang is known for, each of them listed in the city's own encyclopaedia entry. Stopping at a market or a food street is easier with a car and a driver who knows where the stalls are, because the good ones are rarely on the main road.

What to Eat in Nanchang

🍜 clay-pot soup

Local dish

clay-pot soup is on the local menu in Nanchang. Ask for it by name — it is the dish regulars order, and the one worth stopping for while you are in town.

Where to Shop in Nanchang

The specialties of Nanchang are sold from the market and food streets of the old commercial quarters; a driver who works here every day will take you to the stalls the locals use rather than to the tourist-facing shops near the main sights.
