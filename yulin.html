Name Origin

Yulin (玉林) was written 鬱林 in the Ming and Qing dynasties, a name taken from the dense stands of yulan trees that once covered the local hills; it was simplified to 郁林 and then changed to 玉林 in 1956. Today it is a prefecture-level city in the southeast of Guangxi, the largest city in the autonomous region and a major gateway between the Pearl River Delta and the border with Vietnam.

Education & Universities

Yulin Normal University, the Yulin campus of Guangxi Medical University and Yuchai Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Yulin Fumian Airport (YLX) has domestic flights. Yulin Railway Station on the Lizhou–Zhanjiang line and Yulin North Station on the Nanzhu high-speed railway connect the city with Nanning, Guangzhou and the Pearl River Delta. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Yulin has a humid subtropical monsoon climate with hot, rainy summers and mild winters. The city sits in the hilly country of southeastern Guangxi, where the Shaping, Yujiang and Beiliu rivers drain into the Xijiang, and the Darong Mountains in the north carry one of the region's largest tracts of evergreen forest.

Rong County is the heritage anchor of Yulin. Its Jinglue Terrace Zhenwu Pavilion, first built in the Ming dynasty, and the painted timberwork of the old guild halls are the finest heritage in the city, with the Goulou Cave karst site at Beiliu drawing visitors from across the country.

Yulin Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Yulin like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

Zhenwu Pavilion is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Yulin

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Yulin

🍵 Tea Houses of Yulin

Tea culture runs through the day in Yulin: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
