China's Auto City

Changchun is famously China's Auto City (汽车城). The FAW Group (First Automobile Works, 中国一汽), founded in 1953, is headquartered here and operates the massive FAW-Volkswagen and FAW Toyota joint ventures. The city produces some of China's most iconic vehicles. Car enthusiasts will appreciate the FAW Car Museum, and visitors can tour the FAW heritage factory.

Metro & Transportation

Changchun's metro system has 5 lines and 100+ km of track, with Line 3 being China's first light rail line. The city is served by Changchun Longjia International Airport (CGQ), located about 31 km north of the city center. The airport express train connects to the city in about 35 minutes. Changchun Railway Station and Changchun West Station offer high-speed rail connections to Harbin, Shenyang, Dalian, and Beijing.

Natural Environment

Changchun sits in the heart of the Songnen Plain, surrounded by gentle hills. The Northeast Forest Park and Jingyuetan National Forest Park (54 Lakes) give the city its nickname "Forest City." The climate is humid continental — long, freezing winters and warm, humid summers. Winter temperatures drop to -20°C, making Changchun one of China's coldest major cities.

Top Changchun Attractions

From film studios to forest lakes, explore Northeast China's most distinctive city with a private driver.

🎬

Changchun Film Theme Park

Film Culture · China's Oldest Film Studio

Known as the "Hollywood of the East," Changchun Film Studio is the birthplace of Chinese cinema. The Film Theme Park offers studio tours, props exhibits, and film production experiences. The iconic red brick studios have produced over a thousand films since 1946.

🏛️

Puppet Emperor's Palace

Manchukuo History · UNESCO-associated Site

The former palace of Puyi, China's last emperor, during the Japanese puppet state of Manchukuo (1932–1945). Now a museum with preserved rooms, imperial artifacts, and exhibits on this fascinating and tragic chapter of Chinese history.

🌲

Jingyuetan National Forest Park

54 Lakes · 80 km² Forest Park

Known as "54 Lakes," this vast forest park features pristine lakes, pine forests, and cycling paths. The Changchun Film Festival Avenue runs along the lake — especially stunning during autumn foliage and winter snow.

🚗

FAW Car Museum

Auto Industry · FAW Heritage Factory

China's national car museum showcasing the history of FAW and Chinese automotive development. See the first Hongqi limousines that carried Chinese leaders, vintage models, and cutting-edge NEV concepts. A must for car enthusiasts.

🗼

Stalin Street (Moscow Grand Theatre)

Colonial Architecture · Changchun's "Moscow Street"

A grand tree-lined boulevard flanked by European-style buildings from the Manchukuo era. The Changchun People's Stadium and the historic Grand Theatre sit along this avenue, evoking the city's complex international history.

🏞️

Northeast Forest Park

Urban Forest · Largest City Park in Northeast China

One of Asia's largest urban forest parks, covering 891 hectares. Features man-made lakes, pavilions, and dense pine forests. Popular for morning tai chi, cycling, and summer boat rides. The park transforms into a snowy wonderland in winter.

Getting to Changchun: Airports & Railway Stations

Getting to Changchun by air, rail, or road

✈️ Airport

Changchun Longjia International Airport (CGQ)

North of city center · 31 km to downtown

CGQ is Jilin Province's main international gateway, serving domestic flights and international routes to Japan, Korea, and Russia. The airport has two terminals: Terminal 1 for domestic flights and Terminal 2 for international and some domestic flights. The Airport Express train runs to Changchun Railway Station in about 35 minutes, but with luggage, a private car is far more comfortable. By road, expect 40–50 minutes to central Changchun hotels, depending on traffic.

🚄 Railway Stations

Changchun Station (长春站)

City center · Oldest station

The historic main station in the heart of the city. Handles conventional trains and some high-speed services. Easy taxi or metro connections to downtown hotels. Long-distance routes include Harbin, Shenyang, Dalian, and Beijing.

Changchun West Station (长春西站)

High-speed hub · West Changchun

The city's primary high-speed rail station, handling G/D trains to Beijing (3.5h), Harbin (1h), Shenyang (1.5h), Dalian (2h), and Shanghai (8h). A private car pickup here is recommended as taxi queues can be long during peak hours.

Changchun South Station (长春南站)

South district · Mixed traffic

Smaller station serving regional high-speed and conventional trains. Convenient for FAW factory visits and the southern districts. Pre-booking a car is advisable for smooth connections.

Changchun's Best Photo Spots

Capture the city from the most iconic and photogenic angles.

🌅 Film Festival Avenue, Jingyuetan

Best at sunrise or dusk

The grand avenue lined with film-themed sculptures reflects beautifully in the lake at dawn and dusk. The Changchun Film Festival's golden awards statues are iconic. Arrive early morning for misty lake shots with the pine forest backdrop.

🏛️ Puppet Emperor's Palace

Best in afternoon

The ornate Japanese-colonial architecture is most photogenic in soft afternoon light. The main hall with its chrysanthemum throne and the tranquil palace gardens offer rich visual detail. Drone shots capture the whole compound beautifully.

🌲 Jingyuetan 54 Lakes

Best in autumn foliage season

The string of 54 connected lakes surrounded by pine forests is at its most spectacular in September–October when the larch and maple trees turn gold and crimson. Cycling along the lakeside path offers continuous photo opportunities.

🏙️ Stalin Street European Architecture

Best in blue hour or snow

The grand colonial boulevard with its European-style buildings, tree-lined median, and ornate facades looks like a scene from Moscow or Paris — especially magical in winter snow or illuminated at night.

🚗 FAW Car Museum Entrance

Best for car enthusiasts

The iconic red vintage Hongqi cars parked at the museum entrance make for dramatic shots. The sleek modern NEV concepts and the factory facade also photograph well. Early morning or late afternoon light is best for exterior shots.

🏞️ Changchun Snow Scene (Winter)

Best after fresh snowfall

Changchun's heavy winter snowfall transforms the city into a beautiful snowscape. The ice-covered trees in Jingyuetan, the snow-dusted colonial buildings on Stalin Street, and the frost-covered lake surfaces create extraordinary winter photography.

Food & Shopping in Changchun

🍽️ Must-Try Changchun Cuisine

Chuànr (Northeast Grilled Skewers)

The signature street food of Northeast China. Lamb or beef skewers grilled over charcoal, brushed with cumin and chili. Found at night markets across the city. Your driver can take you to the most authentic night market stalls.

Guobac (Northeast Corn Pancake)

A crispy corn pancake unique to Northeast China. Made from fermented corn batter, pan-fried until golden. Savory or sweet, it's a beloved local breakfast. Try it at the morning market near Changchun Station.

Wangsan Christmas Chicken (旺仔圣诞鸡)

A uniquely Changchun dish — a whole chicken marinated in spices and slow-roasted until the skin is lacquered and the meat falls off the bone. A local institution since the 1980s, found near the Film Studio area.

Shaobing (Flaky Meat Pie)

Flaky, layered flatbread filled with minced pork and scallions, pan-fried until crispy. A popular breakfast item sold at street carts. Savory, filling, and cheap — perfect with a cup of soy milk.

Northeast Hotpot & Korean BBQ

Changchun's large Korean and Manchu population means excellent Korean BBQ and rich, spicy Northeast-style hotpot. The cold winter makes hotpot a year-round favorite. Ask your driver for the best Korean BBQ near the university district.

🛍️ Shopping Destinations

Hongqi Street (红旗街)

Changchun's main shopping and entertainment district. Features department stores, electronics retailers, and the Changchun Film Theme Park gift shop. The street comes alive in the evening with food stalls and performers.

Chongqing Road Pedestrian Street

A lively pedestrian shopping street lined with international brands, local boutiques, and the famous Chongqing Road book market. A great place to find Changchun-brand souvenirs and northeastern specialties.

Euromall (欧亚商城)

One of Northeast China's largest shopping complexes, operated by the Euromall Group. Features duty-free international brands, a large supermarket, and a food court. Popular with locals and visitors alike.

Shengbaling (盛百龄) Night Market

The city's most famous night market, featuring hundreds of food stalls. Try grilled skewers, fried rice cakes, and sweet fermented rice drinks. Open from 5 PM until late. A private car can drop you right at the entrance.

🎁 What to Buy in Changchun

Changchun Film Festival Memorabilia

Official merchandise from China's oldest film festival — posters, film reels, statuettes, and director's chairs. Available at the Film Theme Park gift shop and official online store.

Northeast Ginseng & Medicinal Herbs

Jilin Province is famous for wild ginseng, deer antler velvet, and cordyceps. The Tonghua pharmaceutical district and specialty medicine markets offer quality products. Your driver can recommend reputable shops.

Manchu-style Embroidery

Intricate embroidery with Manchu cultural motifs — dragons, phoenixes, and peonies — on silk and cotton. Traditional craft shops near the Puppet Emperor's Palace sell authentic pieces.

Korean-style Cosmetics & Skincare

Due to Changchun's proximity to the Korean border and large Korean community, Korean cosmetics and skincare products are widely available and popular. Find them at Euromall or the Korean shopping district.

Intangible Cultural Heritage & Living Crafts of Changchun

Meet the traditional arts that have shaped Northeast China — and the artisans keeping them alive.

🎭 Erhuang Opera (二黄)

Jilin Province intangible cultural heritage. Erhuang is one of the two main melodies of Jilin opera, characterized by深沉悲壮 (deep and heroic) tones. It blends singing, martial arts, and elaborate costumes. Changchun's provincial opera troupe performs regularly at the Changchun People's Art Theatre.

Where to experience it: Changchun People's Art Theatre, Jilin Provincial Opera House. Ask your driver to book tickets in advance.

🪡 Manchu Embroidery (满族刺绣)

Manchu embroidery is distinct from Han Chinese styles, featuring bold geometric patterns, vivid colors, and symmetrical designs depicting dragons, phoenixes, and peonies. Traditionally used for clothing, banners, and temple decorations.

Where to find it: Manchu Folk Museum, specialty craft shops near the Puppet Emperor's Palace, and the annual Changchun Folk Art Festival.

❄️ Ice and Snow Sculpture

Changchun is internationally famous for its Ice and Snow World (冰雪大世界), a winter wonderland of enormous ice sculptures illuminated in brilliant colors. The art form is a source of great local pride, with master sculptors training for years to create these monumental works.

Best time: December to February. The main venue is at Changchun Jingyuetan Ice and Snow World.

🎋 Northeast Woodcut Print (东北木版画)

A distinctive printmaking tradition originating in the 1930s Lu Xun Academy of Fine Arts in Shanghai but developed extensively in the Northeast. Bold black-and-white compositions depicting rural life, landscapes, and revolutionary themes. Jilin's printmaking tradition is especially strong.

Where to find it: Jilin Provincial Art Museum, Jilin Academy of Fine Arts gallery.

🥟 Northeast Dumpling Making (包饺子)

Dumpling making is a beloved Northeast tradition. Families gather to make jiaozi (饺子) — folded, stuffed dumplings — during festivals. The Northeast style features thick, generous skins and large portions. Many cooking classes in Changchun offer dumpling-making experiences.

Where to experience: Jilin International Studies University cultural program, private cooking classes. Your driver can help arrange a local experience.

🎺 Northeast Folk Music & Erhu

The erhu (二胡), a two-stringed bowed instrument, is central to Northeast folk music. The haunting, expressive melodies of pieces like "Erquan Yingyue" (Moonlight on the Second Spring) are an essential part of the region's cultural soul. Traditional folk performances feature erhu solos, suona trumpet, and folk singing.

Where to experience: Jilin Conservatory of Music concerts, Changchun Folk Music Festival.

Changchun Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Changchun like a local.

🎬

Visit the Film Studio at Golden Hour

The Film Theme Park is less crowded in the late afternoon. The iconic red brick buildings with Soviet-inspired architecture look stunning in golden hour light. The outdoor sets — traditional streets, old Shanghai alleyways — photograph beautifully without crowds.

❄️

Come in Winter for the Ice Sculptures

Changchun's Ice and Snow World is one of the world's top three ice sculpture festivals alongside Harbin and Sapporo. Book a private car for the 30-minute drive to Jingyuetan at night — the illuminated ice sculptures are absolutely magical at -20°C.

🚗

Visit a Car Factory on Weekend Tours

FAW Group offers weekend factory tours (book via their official website). See the assembly lines where Volkswagen and Toyota models are built. The FAW Car Museum adjacent is free and fascinating for car enthusiasts.

🌲

Cycling Jingyuetan in Autumn

Rent a bike at the park entrance and cycle the 20 km lakeside loop. In September–October, the golden larches and crimson maples reflected in the 54 lakes create one of Northeast China's most beautiful autumn scenes. Start early to avoid tour buses.

🍜

Eat Like a Local at Night Markets

Skip the tourist restaurants and head to the Shengbaling Night Market or the Tongyuan Street Night Market. Try grilled skewers, corn pancakes, rice cakes in sweet soy sauce, and the local favorite — fermented millet drink (格瓦斯). Open from 5 PM until midnight.

🏛️

Book Puppet Emperor's Palace Early

The museum is popular with Chinese tour groups. Book tickets online and arrive at opening time (9 AM) to explore the preserved rooms, imperial artifacts, and Manchukuo history exhibits with fewer crowds.

Best Time to Visit Changchun

Each season offers a different side of China's Auto City and Film Capital.

🌸

Spring

Apr–May · 5–18°C

Short but pleasant. Trees bud and flowers bloom in Jingyuetan. Occasional sandstorms from Mongolia. Good for film studio tours and Puppet Emperor's Palace visits.

Highlights: Jingyuetan spring blossoms, Film Studio tours

☀️

Summer

Jun–Aug · 18–28°C

Warm and humid with long daylight hours. The best time for outdoor activities in Jingyuetan and Northeast Forest Park. July and August see the most rain but also the most vibrant greenery.

Highlights: Cycling at 54 Lakes, night markets

🍂

Autumn

Sep–Nov · 0–18°C

The most beautiful season. The larch and maple forests of Jingyuetan turn gold and crimson. Clear skies and crisp air. Peak photography season. Hotel prices rise in October.

Highlights: Jingyuetan autumn foliage, FAW Museum

❄️

Winter

Dec–Feb · -18 to -5°C

Freezing and spectacular. Changchun Ice and Snow World at Jingyuetan is one of the world's three great ice sculpture festivals. The city is transformed into a magical winter wonderland. Heated indoor attractions and hot pot make winter travel deeply cozy.

Highlights: Ice and Snow World, hot pot, Stalin Street in snow
