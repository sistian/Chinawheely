Good to Know Before You Book

Three things travellers ask us about most - here is the honest answer.

🌐 Language - you will never be stuck

Our drivers are local professionals and most of them do not speak English; they use translation apps to understand your instructions. Our online customer service team speaks English and is available 7 days a week (8AM-12AM Beijing time) on WhatsApp and WeChat - we pass your requests to the driver, confirm the route and the price, and translate anything you need. You can book, change and pay entirely in English.

🍼 Baby & Child Seats - extra charge

Baby and child seats are not included in the quoted fare. They are available at an extra charge. Tell us the child's age and weight in the form or on WhatsApp and we will send the exact price before you pay, so nothing is added without your approval.

📶 WiFi - tell us in advance

In-car WiFi is on request, not an automatic extra. Message us before pickup and your driver will switch on a mobile hotspot and share the network (name and password) with you on arrival. It is a shared mobile connection, so coverage and speed depend on the local network.

Frequently Asked Questions About Booking

Do I need to pay in advance?

We ask for a small deposit or full payment to confirm your booking. You can pay via PayPal or Alipay, and we'll send confirmation within 10 minutes.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight in real time. For reasonable delays, your driver will wait at no extra charge. Just make sure your flight number is correct.

Can I book a car for a full day?

Yes. Select "City / Hourly" service and choose 4, 8, or 12 hours. Your driver will follow your itinerary for the day.

Is the price per person or per car?

All prices are per car, not per person. The vehicle capacity is shown next to each option (Economy, Business, MPV).
