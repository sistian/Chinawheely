Name Origin

Liaoyuan means "source of the Liao" in Chinese, named for its location at the headwaters of the Liao River. It is a coal-mining city in Jilin Province.

Education & Universities

Liaoyuan Vocational and Technical College is the main local institution.

Getting Around

Liaoyuan has no commercial airport; Changchun Longjia Airport is used. Liaoyuan Railway Station connects to Changchun and Siping on the Beijing–Harbin railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Liaoyuan lies in the hilly Jilin region with a humid continental climate. It has coal reserves, rivers, and forests.

Liaoyuan Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Liaoyuan like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

The main sight of Liaoyuan rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Liaoyuan

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Liaoyuan

🍵 Tea Houses of Liaoyuan

Tea culture runs through the day in Liaoyuan: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
