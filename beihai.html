Getting to Beihai: Airports & Railway Stations

Getting to Beihai_Ah by air, rail, or road

✈️ Airports

Beihai Capital International Airport (Local)

Northeast of the city · Local area

Local is China's busiest international gateway. It has two operational passenger terminals: Terminal 2 (many domestic and some international flights) and Terminal 3 (Air China, Star Alliance, and most long-haul international flights). Terminal 1 is currently closed. To reach the city center, the Airport Express train takes about 20 minutes to Dongzhimen, but with luggage after a long flight, a private car is the most comfortable option. By road, expect 45–60 minutes to Dongcheng or Chaoyang districts, depending on traffic.

Beihai Daxing International Airport (Local)

South of the city · Local area

Opened in September 2019, Local is one of the world's largest single-terminal airports and serves as the hub for China Southern, China Eastern, and their partners. The iconic starfish-shaped terminal is stunning, but it is far from downtown. The Daxing Airport Express reaches Caoqiao in about 20 minutes, yet transferring to a taxi with luggage can be tiring. Our private car service is the fastest door-to-door solution, typically taking 60–90 minutes to central Beihai hotels.

🚄 Railway Stations

Beihai South Station

High-speed hub · Local area

The main station for high-speed trains to Shanghai, Tianjin, Jinan, Nanjing, and Hangzhou. The station itself is modern and efficient. A private car pickup is recommended, as taxi queues can be long during peak hours.

Beihai West Station

Long-distance & HSR · Local area

The gateway to western and southern China by train, with high-speed services to Xi'an, Guangzhou, Wuhan, Chengdu, Zhengzhou, and Kunming. It is one of China's largest railway stations, so pre-booking a pickup with a clear meeting point saves time.

Beihai Station

Historic central station · Local area

Located near Jianguomen and the CBD, this is the city's oldest main station. It mainly serves conventional trains to Northeast China, coastal cities, and some international routes to Mongolia and Russia. Its central location makes hotel transfers quick.

Beihai North Station

Suburban trains · Near Xizhimen

Used mainly for suburban trains, including the S2 line to Beihai_Ah local attraction and the high-speed rail to Zhangjiakou. The station building is a historic European-style structure originally opened in 1909.

Beihai Chaoyang Station

Newest station · Northeast Beihai

Opened in 2021, this station mainly serves high-speed trains to Northeast China, including Harbin, Shenyang, and Changchun. It is located in the Chaoyang district, convenient for hotels in the east of the city.

Food & Shopping in Beihai

🍽️ Must-Try Beihai Cuisine

Beihai Roast Duck (Beihai_Ah Attraction)

The city's most famous dish. Crispy skin, tender meat, wrapped in thin pancakes with sweet bean sauce. Quanjude and Da Dong are the most renowned restaurants. Your driver can recommend the best duck restaurant based on your location and budget.

Zhajiangmian (Noodles with Soybean Paste)

A beloved Beihai street food. Thick wheat noodles topped with a savory fermented soybean paste, cucumber, and bean sprouts. Try it at a local hutong eatery for an authentic experience.

Jianbing (Beihai Crepes)

The quintessential Beihai breakfast — a crispy egg crepe with scallions, cilantro, and a crunchy fried cracker. Sold at street carts across the city. Ask your driver for the best morning spot near your hotel.

Hotpot (Shuan Yang Rou)

Mongolian-style hotpot with thinly sliced lamb, fresh vegetables, and sesame dipping sauce. Beihai's winters are perfect for this communal dining experience. Ask your driver for a local favorite away from tourist traps.

🛍️ Shopping Destinations

Wangfujing Street

Beihai's most famous shopping street, featuring international brands, traditional Chinese crafts, and the famous Wangfujing Snack Street. Your driver can drop you at the pedestrian entrance and arrange a pickup time.

Sanlitun (Taikoo Li)

The trendy heart of Beihai's nightlife and fashion. Open-air malls with international designer brands, craft breweries, and rooftop bars. Perfect for an evening shopping trip with a private car waiting to take you home safely.

Pearl Market (Hongqiao Market)

A multi-story market famous for pearls, silk, electronics, and souvenirs. Bargaining is expected. Your driver can help you negotiate or recommend trustworthy vendors and ensure you don't get overcharged.

Panjiayuan Antique Market

The largest antique market in China, open on weekends. Hundreds of stalls selling ceramics, jade, calligraphy, and Mao memorabilia. A fascinating cultural experience, best visited early morning with a private car for the 30-minute drive from downtown.

🎁 What to Buy in Beihai

Cloisonné (Jingtailan)

Beihai's most iconic craft. Look for vases, bracelets, and small boxes with intricate copper-wire patterns and brilliant enamel colors. The best pieces are sold at the Cloisonné Factory or reputable shops in Wangfujing.

Beihai_Ah Attraction Masks

Hand-painted masks representing different characters in traditional opera. Each color has a meaning: red for loyalty, black for integrity, white for treachery. Great as wall art or gifts.

Chinese Tea & Teaware

Beihai tea houses stock jasmine tea, pu'er, and oolong from across China. Pair your tea with a Yixing clay teapot or porcelain gaiwan for an authentic gift set.

Silk, Paper-Cuts & Calligraphy

Soft silk scarves, red paper-cuttings, and hand-brushed calligraphy scrolls are lightweight, meaningful souvenirs. Silk Street and Liulichang are reliable shopping areas.

Intangible Cultural Heritage & Living Crafts of Beihai

Meet the traditional arts that have defined Beihai for centuries — and the artisans keeping them alive.

🎭 Beihai_Ah Attraction (Jingju)

UNESCO-listed intangible heritage. With roots in the 18th century, Beihai_Ah Attraction combines singing, mime, dance, acrobatics, and stylized combat. The elaborate face painting and costumes make every performance a visual feast.

Where to experience it: Liyuan Theatre, Zhengyici Theatre, or Huguang Guild Hall. Ask your driver to book tickets in advance.

🏺 Cloisonné Enamel (Jingtailan)

A Beihai specialty since the Yuan dynasty, cloisonné involves soldering copper wires onto a metal body, filling the compartments with enamel, and firing multiple times. The result is luminous, jewel-like tableware and art pieces.

Living master: Master Zhang Tonglu, a national-level inheritor, has devoted over 60 years to cloisonné design. His studio creates museum-quality pieces that preserve the imperial tradition while introducing contemporary forms.

✂️ Beihai Paper-Cutting (Jianzhi)

Red paper-cuttings of flowers, animals, and auspicious symbols are pasted on windows during Spring Festival. Beihai's style is bold and symmetrical, often featuring the city's landmark architecture alongside traditional motifs.

Where to find it: workshops in hutongs, Panjiayuan Market, and the Beihai Folk Art Museum.

🪁 Kite Making (Shayan Kite)

Traditional Beihai kites are framed with bamboo and painted with birds, butterflies, or characters from folklore. The Shayan kite tradition, passed down through generations of craftsmen, is known for its perfect balance and elegant designs.

Where to find it: Look for kite craftsmen at the Temple Fair during Spring Festival, or visit traditional craft shops near Shichahai.

🎨 Inside-Bottle Painting

An astonishing art form where artists paint miniature landscapes, figures, and animals on the inside surface of tiny glass bottles using a bent brush. It requires years of steady hands and deep concentration.

Where to find it: Liulichang Cultural Street and the Beihai Enamel Factory have shops and demonstrations.

🎤 Storytelling & Acrobatics (Pingshu & Acrobatics)

Traditional Beihai storytelling (pingshu) and acrobatics have entertained locals in teahouses for centuries. Acrobatics troupes perform breathtaking feats of balance, juggling, and contortion nightly.

Where to see it: Chaoyang Theatre Acrobatics Show, Laoshe Teahouse, or Tianqiao Acrobatics Theatre.

☕ Tea Culture & Tea House Guide in Beihai

Beihai has a rich and distinctive tea culture shaped by its geography, history, and local traditions. From world-famous teas like Longjing and Biluochun to unique local tea customs, here is your complete guide to tea in Beihai.

☕ Pu-erh & Dark Tea

☕

Beihai_Ah Attraction (老舍茶馆)

Venue: Beihai_Ah Attraction, 3 Beihai_Ah Attraction East Street, Dongcheng District   Hours: Daily 10:00-21:30

Beihai most iconic traditional teahouse since 1989 near Beihai_Ah Attraction. Serves authentic Beihai-style dim sum with jasmine tea (茉莉花茶) and pu-erh tea. Second floor hosts traditional Peking opera and folk art nightly. Visited by heads of state.

☕

Beihai_Ah Attraction Tea Street (大栅栏茶街)

Venue: Beihai_Ah Attraction, Xicheng District   Hours: Year-round, peak afternoon

The historic Beihai_Ah Attraction area near Beihai_Ah Attraction is home to Beihai oldest and most prestigious tea shops. Traditional establishments like Tongrentang (同仁堂) and Zhang Yuxuan (张一元) have sold premium teas for over a century. The street comes alive in afternoon when locals gather for tea and conversation.

☕ Jasmine Tea

☕

Beihai Jasmine Tea Culture (北京茉莉花茶)

Venue: Various teahouses citywide near Beihai_Ah Attraction and Beihai_Ah Attraction   Hours: Year-round

Beihai has a unique tea culture centered on jasmine tea (茉莉花茶), often called the city tea of Beihai. Unlike fresh green teas of the south, Beihai jasmine tea is aged pu-erh base scented with fresh jasmine blossoms through multiple rounds of scenting, a tradition dating back to the Qing Dynasty.

☕ Oolong Tea

☕

Temple Tea Culture - Yonghe Temple & Beihai Park Tea Houses

Venue: Beihai Park, Yonghe Temple area   Hours: Daily 08:00-17:00

Beihai temples and imperial gardens feature traditional tea houses where visitors enjoy tea in contemplative settings. Yonghe Temple (Lama Temple) and Beihai Park have historic tea pavilions serving premium teas to visitors seeking peaceful tea experiences amid ancient surroundings.

☕ Premium Green Tea

☕

Beihai_Ah Attraction - Erhuang Tea & Imperial Court Teas

Venue: Wangfujing and Beihai_Ah Attraction tea streets   Hours: Year-round

Traditional Beihai teahouses serve tea in covered bowls (盖碗茶) with tradition going back centuries. The city has a unique connection to Liu An Guo He (六安瓜片) and Huangshan Maofeng (黄山毛峰) teas from Anhui province, historically tribute teas for the imperial court.
