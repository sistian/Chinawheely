Overview: Food & Shopping in Shenzhen

What to eat in Shenzhen and where the shopping actually happens.

The 3 dishes below are the ones Shenzhen is known for, each of them listed in the city's own encyclopaedia entry. Stopping at a market or a food street is easier with a car and a driver who knows where the stalls are, because the good ones are rarely on the main road.

What to Eat in Shenzhen

🍜 沙井蚝

Local dish

沙井蚝 is on the local menu in Shenzhen. Ask for it by name — it is the dish regulars order, and it is the one to try while you are in town.

🍜 乳鸽

Local dish

乳鸽 is on the local menu in Shenzhen. Ask for it by name — it is the dish regulars order, and it is the one to try while you are in town.

Local dish

Where to Shop in Shenzhen

The specialties of Shenzhen are sold from the market and food streets of the old commercial quarters; a driver who works here every day will take you to the stalls the locals use rather than to the tourist-facing shops near the main sights.
