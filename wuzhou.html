Name Origin

Wuzhou means "five hills" in Chinese, named for the hills near the city. It is located in eastern Guangxi at the border with Guangdong on the Xijiang River.

Education & Universities

Wuzhou University and Wuzhou Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Wuzhou Xijiang Airport (WUZ) has domestic flights. Wuzhou Railway Station connects to Guangzhou and Nanning on the Nanning–Guangzhou railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Wuzhou lies on the Xijiang River (West River) with a subtropical monsoon climate. It has rivers, hills, and is an important river port and trade city.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Wuzhou

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Wuzhou

🍵 Tea Houses of Wuzhou

Tea culture runs through the day in Wuzhou: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
