Name Origin

Zhuzhou means "islet of the Zhu" in Chinese. It is located in eastern Hunan and is an important industrial city, especially for rail transit and high-speed train manufacturing.

Education & Universities

Hunan University of Technology and Zhuzhou Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Zhuzhou has no commercial airport; Changsha Huanghua Airport is the nearest. Zhuzhou Railway Station and Zhuzhou West Station connect to Changsha and other cities on the Beijing–Guangzhou railway. The city is connected to Changsha metro via Zhuzhou's rail transit.

Natural Resources & Environment

Zhuzhou lies in the Xiang River valley in eastern Hunan with a humid subtropical climate. It has rivers, hills, and is an important industrial and transportation hub.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Zhuzhou

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Zhuzhou

🍵 Tea Houses of Zhuzhou

Tea culture runs through the day in Zhuzhou: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
