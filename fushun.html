Name Origin of Fushun

Fushun means "pacify the frontier" in Chinese. It was an important frontier garrison during the Ming dynasty and later became an industrial coal and oil shale city in Liaoning.

Fushun Education & Universities

Liaoning University of Petroleum and Chemical Technology (Fushun campus) and Fushun Normal University are the main institutions.

Getting Around Fushun

Fushun has no commercial airport; Shenyang Taoxian International Airport is used. Fushun Railway Station and Fushun North Station connect to Shenyang and other Northeast cities. Buses and taxis are available.

Fushun Natural Resources & Environment

Fushun lies in the eastern Liaoning hills near the Hun River. It has coal, oil shale, and forest resources. The region has a humid continental climate.

Food & Shopping in Fushun

What to eat and what to bring home from Fushun

🍽️ Must-Try Fushun Cuisine

Try Northeastern pork stew, Fushun spicy mix (malatang), grilled fish, sour cabbage dishes, and Manchu-influenced snacks.

🛍️ Shopping in Fushun

Coal and oil shale-themed souvenirs, Northeastern mushrooms, and Hetuala crafts are popular. Main shopping is in Xinfu District and Wanda Plaza.

🍵 Tea Culture in Fushun

Traditional Fushun teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

🚗 Private Car for a Food Crawl

A private car and driver lets you start at the morning market, cross the city for lunch, and finish at an evening teahouse without worrying about parking or the last bus back to your hotel.

Fushun Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Fushun like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

Manchu ancestral city is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Fushun

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Fushun

🍵 Tea Houses of Fushun

Tea culture runs through the day in Fushun: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
