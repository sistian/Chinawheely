Name Origin

Datong means "great unity" in Chinese. It was the capital of the Northern Wei dynasty and later a strategic northern garrison city on the Great Wall. It is famous for its Buddhist cave art.

Education & Universities

Shanxi Datong University is the main local higher-education institution.

Getting Around

Datong Yungang Airport (DAT) offers domestic flights. Datong Railway Station and Datong South Station are on the Beijing–Baotou and Datong–Xi'an railways. City buses and taxis are available.

Natural Resources & Environment

Datong lies in the Loess Plateau and Inner Mongolian plateau transition zone. It is a major coal-producing city with a dry continental climate.

🏯 Nine Dragon Wall

A masterpiece of a replica of the imperial Nine Dragon Wall from the Ming dynasty in Beijing, but actually older and more detailed than the Beijing version. Datong's Best Photo Spots

Capture the city from the most iconic and photogenic angles.

⛰️ 🌅 Jingshan Park Pavilion

Best at sunrise

Climb to the Wanchun Pavilion for the classic panoramic view over the golden rooftops of the Yungang. On clear days, you can see the CCTV tower and the western mountains in the distance. Arrive early to beat the crowds.

🏯 Yungang Corner Tower

Best at dusk

The northwest corner tower reflected in the moat is one of Datong's most photographed scenes. The sunset glow turns the water gold and the tower silhouette dramatic. Your driver can drop you at the northeast or northwest corner for the perfect shot.

🛶 the old quarter old-quarter lanes

Best in late afternoon

The lakeside old-quarter lanes around the lakeside lanes offer a charming mix of old Datong life, willow trees, and red lanterns. Take a rickshaw ride or wander the narrow alleys, then capture the golden hour light on the water.

🦅 🏙️ CCTV Headquarters

Best at blue hour

Also known as the "Big Pants," this modern architectural icon in the CBD looks best from the plaza below or from across the Third Ring Road. At night, the building is illuminated and reflected in the surrounding towers.

⛰️ 🧱 Yungang at UNESCO World Heritage Site

Best in early morning mist

For photographers seeking a dramatic, less-crowded Yungang shot, UNESCO World Heritage Site is unmatched. The wild, unrestored sections and watchtowers winding over mountain ridges create unforgettable sunrise and fog images.

🎨 798 Art District

Best on a sunny afternoon

Colorful murals, industrial brick buildings, and quirky sculptures make 798 a favorite for creative portraits. Every corner offers a different backdrop, from Soviet-era slogans to contemporary street art.

Datong Local Secrets

🎭 Start at the landmark

UNESCO World Heritage Site is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Best Time to Visit Datong

Each season shows a different side of Datong.

🌸

Spring

Mar–May · 10–22°C

Mild and pleasant with occasional dust storms. Cherry blossoms at the parkland around UNESCO World Heritage Site. Moderate crowds.

Highlights: Yungang gardens, Yungang park

☀️

Summer

Jun–Aug · 22–32°C

Hot and humid with occasional heavy rain. Air conditioning is essential. Peak domestic tourist season.

Highlights: Yungang lotus, evening old-quarter walks

🍂

Autumn

Sep–Nov · 8–20°C

Clear skies, crisp air, and golden ginkgo trees. The most popular and comfortable season to visit.

Highlights: Yungang hiking, Ditan Park ginkgo

❄️

Winter

Dec–Feb · -6–5°C

Cold and dry with occasional snow. Fewer tourists and lower hotel prices. Indoor heating is excellent.

Highlights: Frozen the old quarter skating, hot pot dinners

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Datong

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Datong

🍵 Tea Houses of Datong

Tea culture runs through the day in Datong: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
