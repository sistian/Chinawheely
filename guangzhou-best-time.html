Seasons Overview

The climate record of Guangzhou, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 22.8 °C; annual rainfall averages 1,926 mm; July is the hottest month at 29.4 °C; January the coldest at 14.1 °C; the climate pattern includes typhoons, the monsoon, maritime influence, humid heat, the rainy season.
