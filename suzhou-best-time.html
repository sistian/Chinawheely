Suzhou Best Time to Visit | When to Go to Suzhou

Complete seasonal guide for planning your Suzhou trip

Overview: Best Time to Visit Suzhou

The best time to visit Suzhou is during March to May and September to November. Suzhou experiences distinct seasons, each offering unique travel experiences for sightseeing tours, photography expeditions, and cultural exploration. Classical gardens, silk production, canals, Pingjiang Road.

Quick Facts About Suzhou Weather

Spring: 10-20C - Mild and comfortable for outdoor activities

Summer: 25-33C - Warm, great for indoor attractions and evening tours

Autumn: 14-24C - Cool and pleasant, peak travel season

Winter: 2-10C - Cold but unique winter scenery

Spring in Suzhou (10-20C)

Best time to see classical gardens in full bloom. Comfortable temperatures perfect for walking tours through the Humble Administrator Garden and Lingering Garden.

Summer in Suzhou (25-33C)

Hot and rainy with temperatures reaching 33C. Indoor garden tours and air-conditioned museums recommended. Summer provides longer daylight for evening canal walks.

Autumn in Suzhou (14-24C)

Golden leaves create stunning photography opportunities. Comfortable temperatures make this peak season for garden tours and Pingjiang Road walks.

Winter in Suzhou (2-10C)

Quiet season with unique winter charm. Gardens are less crowded, and the winter scenery offers different beauty.
