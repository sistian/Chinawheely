Name Origin

Xinzhou means "new prefecture" in Chinese. It is located in north-central Shanxi and is known for the Buddhist holy site of Mount Wutai.

Education & Universities

Xinzhou Normal University and Xinzhou Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Xinzhou has no commercial airport; Taiyuan Wusu Airport is the nearest. Xinzhou Railway Station connects to Taiyuan and other cities. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Xinzhou lies on the Loess Plateau with a continental climate. It has mountains, including the famous Mount Wutai, and the Yellow River.

Xinzhou Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Xinzhou like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

UNESCO, one of the four sacred Buddhist mountains is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Xinzhou

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Xinzhou

🍵 Tea Houses of Xinzhou

Tea culture runs through the day in Xinzhou: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
