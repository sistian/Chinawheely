Name Origin

Guyuan means "stable and plain" in Chinese. It is located in southern Ningxia on the Liupan Mountains and is an important Hui Muslim cultural center.

Education & Universities

Ningxia Normal University is the main local higher-education institution in Guyuan.

Getting Around

Guyuan Liupanshan Airport (GYU) offers domestic flights. Guyuan Railway Station connects to Yinchuan and Xi'an on the Baoji–Zhongwei railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Guyuan lies on the Liupan Mountains with a semi-arid continental climate. It has loess hills, forests, and is known for its potatoes, beef, and goji berries.

Food & Shopping in Guyuan

What to eat and what to bring home from Guyuan

🍽️ Must-Try Guyuan Cuisine

Try Guyuan mutton, halal noodles, Ningxia goji berries, Liupan mountain mushrooms, and Hui-style baked bread.

🛍️ Shopping in Guyuan

Guyuan goji berries, potato products, halal snacks, and Hui crafts are popular. Main shopping is in Yuanzhou District.

🍵 Tea Culture

Traditional Guyuan teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

🚗 Private Car for a Food Crawl

A private car and driver lets you start at the morning market, cross the city for lunch, and finish at an evening teahouse without worrying about parking or the last bus back to your hotel.

Guyuan Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Guyuan like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

The main sight of Guyuan rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Guyuan

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Guyuan

🍵 Tea Houses of Guyuan

Tea culture runs through the day in Guyuan: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
