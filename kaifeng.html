Name Origin

Kaifeng means "opening the frontier" in Chinese. It was the capital of the Northern Song dynasty and one of the world's largest cities around 1000 CE.

Education & Universities

Henan University and Kaifeng University are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Kaifeng has no commercial airport; Zhengzhou Xinzheng Airport is used. Kaifeng Railway Station connects to Zhengzhou and other cities on the Longhai railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Kaifeng lies on the North China Plain with the Yellow River to the north. It has a temperate continental climate and is known for the Kaifeng section of the Yellow River.

Kaifeng Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Kaifeng like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

The main sight of Kaifeng rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Kaifeng

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Kaifeng

🍵 Tea Houses of Kaifeng

Tea culture runs through the day in Kaifeng: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
