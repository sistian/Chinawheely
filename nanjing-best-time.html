Seasons Overview

The climate record of Nanjing, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 16.4 °C; annual rainfall averages 1,144 mm; July is the hottest month at 28.4 °C; January the coldest at 3.1 °C; the climate pattern includes The midsummer drought, the monsoon, the plum-rain season, the flood season, maritime influence.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 10.1–21.5 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 246 mm.

Highlights: Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, Confucius Temple and the Qinhuai River

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 25–28.4 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 579 mm.

Highlights: Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, Confucius Temple and the Qinhuai River

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 11.5–23.7 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 181 mm.

Highlights: Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, Confucius Temple and the Qinhuai River

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run 3.1–5.6 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 139 mm.

Highlights: Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, Confucius Temple and the Qinhuai River
