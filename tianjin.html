Key Fact
About Tianjin

Name Origin

The name Tianjin (天津) means 'Emperor's Ford' — where the Yongle Emperor crossed the river. It has been a major port since the Yuan Dynasty and became a treaty port after the Second Opium War.

Education & Universities

Tianjin hosts Nankai University and Tianjin University, two of China's oldest and most prestigious universities, plus Tianjin Medical University.

Metro & Transportation

Tianjin Metro has 10 lines covering the city and connecting to Beijing via intercity rail. A private car is convenient for visiting the spread-out historic concession area and riverfront.

Natural Resources & Environment

Tianjin sits at the confluence of the Haihe River and the Bohai Sea. The flat city has hot summers and cold winters, with the riverfront and former concessions defining the urban landscape.

6 Things to Do in Tianjin

Make the most of your visit to Tianjin with these handpicked experiences — all easily accessible with a private car and English-speaking driver.

🏛️

Explore the Ancient Culture Street (Gulou)

Tianjin's most vibrant traditional street, featuring a drum tower, bell tower, and hundreds of shops selling folk art, calligraphy, and local snacks. The nearby Tianjin Eye ferris wheel provides spectacular city views.

🏫

Walk the Five Great Streets European Architecture Quarter

A remarkably well-preserved area of early 20th-century European architecture, including neoclassical, baroque, and art deco buildings. Free to walk — one of northern China's most underrated urban walking areas.

🍜

Eat at the Tianjin Food Street Underground City

An underground gastronomic adventure featuring 100+ stalls of authentic Tianjin cuisine: mahua fried dough twists, Goubuli steamed buns, and the famous Erguotou baijiu. Open until midnight daily.

🏯

Visit the Temple of the Great Upset (Dabeiyuan)

One of Tianjin's oldest and most atmospheric Buddhist temples, less frequented by tourists but with beautiful architecture and a peaceful incense-filled courtyard.

🎭

Catch a Traditional Crosstalk Show

Tianjin is the birthplace of xiàngsheng — traditional Chinese comedy storytelling and wordplay. The Tianjin Quyuan Arts Troupe performs regularly and is a beloved local cultural experience.

🌆

Stroll Along the Hai River Waterfront

The Hai River cuts through central Tianjin and is lined with a pleasant riverside promenade. Walk from the Italian Style Street to the Jingang Bridge for some of the best city views.

Best season to travel in Tianjin

🌸 Best Season April – June / September – October — Spring (April–June) brings warm, comfortable weather and the European Architecture Quarter at its most photogenic. Autumn (September–October) offers pleasant temperatures and clear skies.

🌸
Spring April – June 12°C–30°C 🌸 Best

The best time to visit. Warm days, cool evenings, and the city's trees in full leaf. The European Architecture Quarter is at its most photogenic.

☀️
Summer July – August 24°C–36°C OK

Hot and humid with occasional rain. Indoor attractions like the Tianjin Museum are preferable during afternoon heat.

🍂
Autumn September – October 12°C–26°C 🍂 Best

One of the best seasons. Clear skies, comfortable temperatures, and the Hai River waterfront illuminated beautifully at night.

❄️
Winter November – March -5°C–8°C OK

Cold and dry with strong winds. The ancient Gulou area is quiet and atmospheric. Indoor attractions are warm and uncrowded.

Chinese Spring Festival Facts

🧧

When is Chinese New Year 2027? January 29, 2027

Chinese New Year 2027 is January 29, 2027. Tianjin follows Beijing-influenced northern Chinese Spring Festival traditions while maintaining its own unique European-influenced character.

🏮

Tianjin Eye and Haihe River Illuminations

The Tianjin Eye (天津之眼) Ferris wheel on the Haihe River is spectacularly lit during Spring Festival -- 120m above the river with city lights reflecting on the water. The best night view in Tianjin.

🥟

Chinese Spring Festival Food -- Tianjin Style

Baozi (包子) are the signature Tianjin food -- large fluffy steamed buns with various fillings. During Spring Festival, families make their own baozi. Tianjin also has a strong jiaozi tradition.

🎭

Tianjin Peking Opera -- Shuanghuai Style

Tianjin is famous for its unique Shuanghuai style of Peking opera -- a combination of Beijing and Shanghai styles. The Tianjin Peking Opera Theatre performs throughout the holiday.

Top Spring Festival Activities

🏯

Tianjin Eye Night View (天津之眼)

The 120m Tianjin Eye Ferris wheel on the Haihe River is spectacularly lit during Spring Festival. City lights reflect on the river creating one of northern Chinas most romantic night views. Open until 10pm.

🏛️

Five Great Avenues European District Walk

The European colonial Five Great Avenues (五大道) district is beautifully decorated with red lanterns during Spring Festival -- European architecture with Chinese New Year decorations creates a uniquely Tianjin atmosphere. Free.

🥁

Tianjin Peking Opera at Tianjin Grand Theatre

The Tianjin Grand Theatre (天津大剧院) presents special Peking opera performances including the unique Tianjin Shuanghuai style. Expected 2027: January 29-February 3.

Must-Try Spring Festival Food

🥟

Baozi (包子) -- Tianjin Signature

Tianjins most famous food -- large fluffy steamed buns with various fillings. During Spring Festival, families make their own baozi. The classic filling is pork and cabbage with ginger.

Tip: Best at: Goubuli (狗不理) -- Tianjin most famous baozi chain

🥟

Jiaozi -- Tianjin Style

Tianjin jiaozi have a thin wrapper and generous filling of pork and Chinese cabbage. Served with a dipping sauce of vinegar and garlic.

Tip: Available at all Tianjin wet markets from mid-January

🧋

Mahua (麻花) -- Tianjin Fried Dough Twist

Tianjins famous sweet snack -- long spirals of fried dough in sugar syrup. A popular gift during Spring Festival. Goubuli brand mahua is the most famous.

Tip: Best at: Goubuli shops or Tianjin train station gifts

🏆 Major Sports Events in Tianjin

Tianjin is northern China's historic sports city -- home to the Tianjin Jinmen Tiger FC and the famous Jin-Jing Derby (Tianjin vs Beijing) rivalry. The European-influenced heritage gives its sports culture a unique cosmopolitan character.

🏃

Tianjin Marathon (天津马拉松)

2026: April (annual)

The Tianjin Marathon (天津马拉松) passes the Haihe River, Tianjin Eye (天津之眼), the European colonial Five Great Avenues (五大道), and finishes at the Tianjin Olympic Centre. 20,000 runners.

Venue: Start: Tianjin Eye area; Finish: Tianjin Olympic Centre. Metro: Tianjin Eye (Line 3/6).

⚽

Chinese Super League Football (中超联赛 – 天津津门虎)

2026 season: March to November

Tianjin Jinmen Tiger (天津津门虎) competes in the CSL at the Tianjin Olympic Centre (天津奥林匹克中心) -- a 60,000-capacity stadium. The Jin-Jing Derby (津京德比) against Beijing Guoan is always intense.

Venue: Tianjin Olympic Centre (天津奥林匹克中心), Hedong District. Metro: Olympic Centre (Line 5).

🏀

Tianjin Dragons CBA Basketball (天津先行者)

2026-2027 season: October 2026 to May 2027

The Tianjin Dragons (天津先行者) play at the Tianjin Sports Centre (天津体育中心). Consistent CBA competitors with a dedicated fan base.

Venue: Tianjin Sports Centre (天津体育中心), Hexi District. Metro: Sports Centre (Line 5/6).

⚽

Tianjin Derby – vs Beijing Guoan (津京德比)

2026 season: select home games

The Jin-Jing Derby (津京德比) between Tianjin Jinmen Tiger and Beijing Guoan is one of China's oldest and most passionate football rivalries. Both sets of fans create an incredible atmosphere at Tianjin Olympic Centre.

Venue: Tianjin Olympic Centre. Metro: Olympic Centre.
