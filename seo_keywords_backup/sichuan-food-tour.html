Explore Iconic Dishes

Iconic Sichuan Dishes

Sichuan cuisine is famous for ma (numbing heat) and la (spicy), creating a sensation that is bold, complex, and utterly addictive.

🫘

Mapo Tofu (麻婆豆腐)

Silken tofu in a fiery, numbing sauce of doubanjiang, Sichuan peppercorns, and minced pork. A dish that defines Sichuan cuisine -- complex, spicy, and deeply savory.

🥩

Twice-Cooked Pork (回锅肉)

Thin-sliced pork belly first boiled, then wok-fried with Sichuan preserved vegetables and doubanjiang. A household staple with countless variations across generations.

🍜

Dan Dan Noodles (担担面)

Hand-pulled noodles in a spicy sauce of ground pork, preserved vegetables, Sichuan pepper, and chili oil. Street food royalty from Chengdu's oldest alleyways.

🦞

Spicy Crayfish (麻辣小龙虾)

Whole crayfish stir-fried in a mala broth of Sichuan pepper, dried chilies, and aromatics. A summer institution in Chengdu and Chongqing -- eat with your hands.

🍲

Water-Boiled Fish (水煮鱼)

Thick slices of fish poached in a broth poured over vegetables, then finished with a flood of sizzling chili oil and Sichuan peppercorns. A theatrical centerpiece dish.

🥬

Spicy Crisp Chicken (辣子鸡)

Bite-sized chicken deep-fried until crispy, then wok-tossed with dried chilies, Sichuan pepper, and garlic. Addictive snacking food -- the bones are a feature, not a bug.

🍲

Hot Pot (川渝火锅)

The ultimate communal dining experience -- a simmering broth of Sichuan spices, doubanjiang, and herbs. Dip thinly-sliced meats, tofu, and vegetables. Chengdu uses 清油 (vegetable oil); Chongqing uses 牛油 (beef tallow).

🥒

Cucumber in Chili Oil (凉拌黄瓜)

Smashed cucumber dressed in a vinaigrette of soy, vinegar, garlic, and a generous pour of crimson chili oil. Refreshing and fiery in equal measure.

🏮 Chengdu: The Heart of Sichuan Cuisine

Chengdu has been the capital of Sichuan cuisine for centuries. These time-tested institutions have been serving the same dishes for generations.

🏆

Chen Yiguang (陈麻婆豆腐)

Established: 1862 (over 160 years)

Specialty: Mapo Tofu -- the restaurant where this iconic dish was invented. The original owner, Chen Mapo, created the dish for workers at a nearby brick factory.

Address: 198 Wan Hua North Road, Qingyang District, Chengdu (成都市青羊区万华北路198号)

Nearest Metro: 人民公园站 (People's Park Station), Line 2

Must Order: Mapo Tofu, Twice-Cooked Pork, Sliced Beef in Chili Oil

🥇

Jian Chuan Zhai (笺膳传)

Established: 1945 (over 80 years)

Specialty: Refined Sichuan home cooking with a palace-style presentation. A favourite of Chengdu's literary and political circles for decades.

Address: 30 Shaocheng Street, Qingyang District, Chengdu (成都市青羊区少城路30号)

Nearest Metro: 宽窄巷子站 (Kuanzhai Alley Station), Line 4

Must Order: Sliced Beef with Pepper Salt, Stir-Fried Pea Shoots

🔥

Huang Cheng Lao Ma (皇城老妈)

Established: 1986 (over 38 years)

Specialty: Upscale Sichuan hot pot with premium ingredients. A Chengdu institution for special occasions -- elegant atmosphere with traditional music performances.

Address: 9 Zongfu Street, Jinjiang District, Chengdu (成都市锦江区总府路9号)

Nearest Metro: 春熙路站 (Chunxi Road Station), Line 2

Must Order: Sichuan Mala Hot Pot, Hand-Made Noodles

🍜

Long Chao Shou (龙抄手)

Established: 1941 (over 83 years)

Specialty: Wonton soup and Sichuan noodles. The name means "Dragon Wonton" -- this is the place for a comforting bowl of wontons in a rich, spicy broth.

Address: 15 Chunxi Road, Jinjiang District, Chengdu (成都市锦江区春熙路15号)

Nearest Metro: 春熙路站 (Chunxi Road Station), Line 2

Must Order: Red Oil Wontons, Sichuan Spicy Wontons, Plain Wonton Soup

🌶️

Kui Ming (魁名)

Established: 1963 (over 60 years)

Specialty: Traditional Sichuan cold dishes and appetizers. Famous for its tongue-and-lung salad, cucumber in chili oil, and dan dan noodles made to a secret family recipe.

Address: 5 Wenshufang Street, Qingyang District, Chengdu (成都市青羊区文殊坊街5号)

Nearest Metro: 文殊院站 (Wenshu Monastery Station), Line 1

Must Order: Dan Dan Noodles, Cold Dish Platter, Tea-Smoked Duck

🦞

Yuxin Si Mian (玉欣丝面)

Established: 1958 (over 65 years)

Specialty: Hand-pulled noodles in a rich beef bone broth, served with Sichuan pickled vegetables. A locals' favourite near the old People's Park gate.

Address: 88 People's Park Outer Road, Qingyang District, Chengdu (成都市青羊区人民公园外环路88号)

Nearest Metro: 人民公园站 (People's Park Station), Line 2

Must Order: Beef Noodle Soup, Spicy Noodles with Sesame Paste

🔥 Chongqing: Sichuan's Spicier Sister

Chongqing's cuisine is harder, hotter, and more industrial than Chengdu's. Think beef tallow hot pots, mountain-smoked meats, and the famous Chongqing noodles.

🍲

Dongnao Chongqing Hotpot (东来老火锅)

Established: 1995 (over 29 years)

Specialty: Old-school Chongqing hot pot with beef tallow (牛油) base -- richer and more intensely flavoured than Chengdu's vegetable oil version. The OG of Chongqing's modern hot pot era.

Address: 1 Jiangbei City Jiaceng, Jiangbei District, Chongqing (重庆市江北区江北城嘉融路1号)

Nearest Metro: 大剧院站 (Grand Theatre Station), Line 6

Must Order: Beef Tallow Hot Pot, Prime Beef Slices, Tripe

🍜

Hua Jia Yuan Jie Shou (花嘉捷守面庄)

Established: 1978 (over 46 years)

Specialty: Chongqing Xiao Mian -- the city's signature thin noodle soup with a fiery broth, pickled vegetables, and peanut碎. A bowl of this is the essence of Chongqing.

Address: 23 Jiaoyuan Street, Yuzhong District, Chongqing (重庆市渝中区焦元街23号)

Nearest Metro: 较场口站 (Jiaochangkou Station), Line 1/2

Must Order: Chongqing Xiao Mian, Spicy Ribs Noodles

🥩

Yi Group Meirong (伊群美蓉)

Established: 1963 (over 60 years)

Specialty: Chongqing-style cold dishes and cured meats. Famous for its smoked sausage, dried beef, and the distinctive Chongqing cured pork belly.

Address: 88 Ciqikou Old Street, Shapingba District, Chongqing (重庆市沙坪坝区磁器口老街88号)

Nearest Metro: 磁器口站 (Ciqikou Station), Line 1

Must Order: Tea-Smoked Duck, Cured Pork Belly, Spicy Tofu Skin

Beyond Chengdu & Chongqing

Sichuan cuisine spreads across western China -- these establishments carry the tradition in cities far from Chengdu.

🏮

Tian Tian Lao Zao (天天老灶) -- Xi'an

Established: 1982 (over 42 years)

Specialty: A Sichuan restaurant that has served Xi'an for over four decades -- famous for its authentic huiguorou and the spicy bean jelly that Xi'an locals crave.

Address: 6 West Street, Beilin District, Xi'an (西安市碑林区西大街6号)

Nearest Metro: 钟楼站 (Bell Tower Station), Line 2

Must Order: Twice-Cooked Pork, Sichuan Pickled Fish

🌶️

Qin Xiang Yuan (秦香苑) -- Xi'an

Established: 1991 (over 33 years)

Specialty: Sichuan hot pot and cold dishes in the heart of Xi'an's Muslim Quarter -- a beloved crossover between Sichuan and Shaanxi flavours.

Address: 88 Beiyuanmen, Lianhu District, Xi'an (西安市莲湖区北院门88号)

Nearest Metro: 钟楼站 (Bell Tower Station), Line 2

Must Order: Sichuan Mala Hot Pot, Spicy Shrimp

🍜

Jiu Zi Jiang (九子姜) -- Mianyang

Established: 1971 (over 53 years)

Specialty: Mianyang-style Sichuan noodles -- a close cousin of Chengdu's dan dan mian but with a unique fermented soybean sauce that gives it a deeper, earthier flavour.

Address: 35 Jianchuan Road, Mianyang (绵阳市涪城区剑川路35号)

Nearest Metro: Taxi from Mianyang city centre

Must Order: Mianyang Style Dan Dan Noodles, Wontons in Chili Oil

What Makes Sichuan Food Unique?

🌶️

Mala (麻辣)

The signature combination of Sichuan pepper (ma) and chili heat (la). The pepper creates a tingling, numbing sensation on the lips -- unique in the world of cuisine.

🍶

Doubanjiang (豆瓣酱)

Fermented broad bean and chili paste -- the soul of Sichuan cooking. Pixian doubanjiang from Chengdu is considered the finest and is aged for up to 3 years.

🫚

Sichuan Pepper (花椒)

Grown in Hanyuan, Sichuan -- this tiny numbing berry is the defining ingredient of Sichuan cuisine. Used whole, ground, or as an infused oil.

🔥

Chili Oil (红油)

Chili oil is used in nearly every Sichuan dish -- poured over cold dishes, mixed into noodles, served as a dip. Chengdu chili oil is famous for its deep red colour and complex flavour.
