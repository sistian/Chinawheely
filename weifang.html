Name Origin

Weifang means "weifang" or "guarding the capital" in Chinese. It is located in central Shandong and is famous as the "Capital of Kites" and for its history of craft and agriculture.

Education & Universities

Weifang University, Weifang Medical University, and Shandong University of Technology (Weifang campus) are main institutions.

Getting Around

Weifang Nanyuan Airport (WEF) has domestic flights. Weifang Railway Station and Weifang North Station connect to Jinan and Qingdao on the Qingdao–Jinan high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Weifang lies on the Shandong Peninsula with a temperate continental climate. It has the Bohai Sea coast, rivers, and is an important agricultural and manufacturing base.

Weifang Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Weifang like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

The main sight of Weifang rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Weifang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Weifang

🍵 Tea Houses of Weifang

Tea culture runs through the day in Weifang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
