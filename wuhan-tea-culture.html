The Real Tea Story in Wuhan

Heicha, or dark tea, is post-fermented tea from Hunan and Anhui. The best known examples are Anhua dark tea and the pressed Fu brick tea of Shaanxi; both are stacked and covered during a long post-fermentation that gives them their dark colour and mellow taste. The tea on the table comes from the producing areas; what is local is the way it is drunk.

Tea Houses Worth Stopping At in Wuhan

Wuhan Tea houses around the old quarter Most of Wuhan's tea trade happens in the streets around the old quarter, where the tea shops pour a pot for you at the counter rather than selling by the tin. Ask for a longjing, a shou pu-erh or a jasmine blend and they will let you taste before you buy. Morning tea and the market stalls Tea drinking in Wuhan starts early: the markets open with the first kettle of the day, and the stalls around the old commercial streets are where the city's own blend is put together.
