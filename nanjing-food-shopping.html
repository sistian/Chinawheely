Overview: Food & Shopping in Nanjing

What to eat in Nanjing and where the shopping actually happens.

The 6 dishes below are the ones Nanjing is known for, each of them listed in the city's own encyclopaedia entry. Stopping at a market or a food street is easier with a car and a driver who knows where the stalls are, because the good ones are rarely on the main road.

Where to Shop in Nanjing

The specialties of Nanjing are sold from the market and food streets of the old commercial quarters; a driver who works here every day will take you to the stalls the locals use rather than to the tourist-facing shops near the main sights.
