Name Origin

Shenyang means "north of the Shen River" in Chinese. It was the birthplace of the Qing dynasty and the former capital of Manchukuo.

Education & Universities

Northeastern University, Liaoning University, Shenyang Aerospace University, and China Medical University are major institutions.

Getting Around

Shenyang Taoxian International Airport (SHE) has domestic and international flights. Shenyang Railway Station and North Station are major rail hubs. The city has four metro lines (Lines 1, 2, 9, and 10).

Natural Resources & Environment

Shenyang lies on the Liao River plain in northeastern China with a humid continental climate. It is the largest city in Northeast China.

Insider tips to help you experience Shenyang like a local

UNESCO is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Shenyang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Shenyang

🍵 Tea Houses of Shenyang

Tea culture runs through the day in Shenyang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
