Name Origin

Beihai means "north sea" in Chinese, named for its location on the northern shore of the Beibu Gulf. It was historically an important port and treaty port in Guangxi.

Education & Universities

Beihai University of Art and Design and Beihai Vocational and Technical College are the main local higher-education institutions.

Getting Around

Beihai Fucheng Airport (BHY) offers flights to major Chinese cities. Beihai Railway Station connects to Nanning and other cities on the Nanning–Beihai high-speed line. City buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Beihai has a tropical monsoon climate, beautiful beaches, mangroves, and coral reefs. Silver Beach (Yintan) is famous for its long, flat, white-sand beach.

Food & Shopping in Beihai

What to eat and what to bring home from Beihai

🍽️ Must-Try Beihai Cuisine

Beihai is famous for fresh seafood, especially crab, shrimp, and fish. Try Hainanese-style chicken rice, seafood noodles, and tropical fruit from the south.

🛍️ Shopping in Beihai

Pearl jewelry from Beihai, dried seafood, tropical fruits, and Vietnamese-style goods are popular. Main shopping areas are around Beibuwan Square and the old port.

🍵 Tea Culture

Traditional Beihai teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

🚗 Private Car for a Food Crawl

A private car and driver lets you start at the morning market, cross the city for lunch, and finish at an evening teahouse without worrying about parking or the last bus back to your hotel.

Top Beach Resort

One of China's most beautiful beaches — 24km of silver-white sand with gentle slopes and clear turquoise water. Perfect for swimming and water sports.

🏛️

🏝️ Weizhou Island (涠洲岛)

Volcanic Island

China's largest volcanic island with coral reefs, diving spots, and dramatic volcanic rock formations. The Catholic church built from volcanic rock is unique.

⛰️

🏘️ Beihai Old Street (北海老街)

Colonial Architecture

200-year-old street with Sino-European coral stone architecture. Built in the 1880s by French and British merchants. Try local snail shells and coconut pastries.

🌸

🌿 Jinhai Bay Mangrove

Nature Reserve

UNESCO-recognized mangrove ecosystem. Take the boardwalk through ancient trees, watch fishermen in traditional boats, sample fresh oysters from local farms.

🏖️

🦐 Waishadao Seafood Market

Seafood Paradise

The famous seafood market where you pick live fish, crab, and shrimp and restaurants cook it instantly. A feast for seafood lovers.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Beihai

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Beihai

🍵 Tea Houses of Beihai

Tea culture runs through the day in Beihai: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
