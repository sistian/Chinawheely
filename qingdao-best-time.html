Seasons Overview

The climate record of Qingdao, month by month.

Book a Qingdao ride All Qingdao services Nearby: Tianjin · Jinan

The annual average temperature is 13 °C; annual rainfall averages 662 mm; August is the hottest month at 25.7 °C; January the coldest at -0.2 °C; the climate pattern includes typhoons, continental influence, the monsoon, cold waves, drought.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 5.7–16.8 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 111 mm.

Highlights: Badaguan (Eight Great Villas), Mount Laoshan, Zhanqiao Pier

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 20.8–25.7 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 377 mm.

Highlights: Badaguan (Eight Great Villas), Mount Laoshan, Zhanqiao Pier

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 9.1–22.1 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 139 mm.

Highlights: Badaguan (Eight Great Villas), Mount Laoshan, Zhanqiao Pier

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run -0.2–2.6 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 35 mm.

Highlights: Badaguan (Eight Great Villas), Mount Laoshan, Zhanqiao Pier
