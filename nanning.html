Name Origin

Nanning means "southern peace" in Chinese. It is the capital of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region and is known as the "Green City."

Education & Universities

Guangxi University, Guangxi Minzu University, and Guangxi Medical University are major institutions.

Getting Around

Nanning Wuxu International Airport (NNG) has domestic and international flights. Nanning Railway Station and East Station are major rail hubs. The city has five metro lines (Lines 1–5).

Natural Resources & Environment

Nanning lies on the Yong River in southern Guangxi with a subtropical monsoon climate. It has many parks, rivers, and is known as the Green City of China.

Top Nanning Attractions

Visit Qingxiu Mountain, Nanhu Lake Park, Guangxi Museum of Nationalities, Guangxi University, and the Yong River waterfront.

Nanning Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Nanning like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

The main sight of Nanning rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Nanning

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Nanning

🍵 Tea Houses of Nanning

Tea culture runs through the day in Nanning: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
