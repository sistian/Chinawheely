Name Origin

Kashgar (Kashi) comes from a Uyghur/Turkic word meaning "jade mountain" or "colored stone." It was a key oasis city on the Silk Road for over 2,000 years.

Education & Universities

Kashgar University is the main higher-education institution.

Getting Around

Kashgar Laining International Airport (KHG) has domestic flights. Kashgar Railway Station connects to Urumqi on the Southern Xinjiang railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Kashgar lies at the western edge of the Tarim Basin with the Taklamakan Desert to the east and the Pamir Mountains to the west. It has an arid continental climate.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Kashgar

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Kashgar

🍵 Tea Houses of Kashgar

Tea culture runs through the day in Kashgar: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
