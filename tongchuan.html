Name Origin

Tongchuan means "copper river" in Chinese, named for the copper-colored river. It is an industrial city in central Shaanxi known for coal and ceramics.

Education & Universities

Tongchuan Vocational and Technical College is the main local institution.

Getting Around

Tongchuan has no commercial airport; Xi'an Xianyang Airport is the nearest. Tongchuan Railway Station connects to Xi'an. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Tongchuan lies on the Guanzhong Plain with a continental climate. It has coal resources, the Jing River, and is famous for Yaozhou porcelain.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Tongchuan

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Tongchuan

🍵 Tea Houses of Tongchuan

Tea culture runs through the day in Tongchuan: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
