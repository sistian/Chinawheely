Overview: Food & Shopping in Beijing

What to eat in Beijing and where the shopping actually happens.

The 3 dishes below are the ones Beijing is known for, each of them listed in the city's own encyclopaedia entry. Stopping at a market or a food street is easier with a car and a driver who knows where the stalls are, because the good ones are rarely on the main road.

What to Eat in Beijing

🍜 北京烤鸭

Local dish

北京烤鸭 is on the local menu in Beijing. Ask for it by name — it is the dish regulars order, and it is the one to try while you are in town.

🍜 涮羊肉

Local dish

涮羊肉 is on the local menu in Beijing. Ask for it by name — it is the dish regulars order, and it is the one to try while you are in town.

Local dish

卤煮 is on the local menu in Beijing. Ask for it by name — it is the dish regulars order, and it is the one to try while you are in town.

Where to Shop in Beijing

The specialties of Beijing are sold from the market and food streets of the old commercial quarters; a driver who works here every day will take you to the stalls the locals use rather than to the tourist-facing shops near the main sights.
