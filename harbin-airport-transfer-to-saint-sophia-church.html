Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB)

42 km from the Harbin terminal, about 55 minutes by car.

Taiping lies about 37 km south-west of the city in Daoli's direction and was built in 1979 as Yanjiagang. The approach runs in on the G10 towards the centre and finishes on Toulong Street beside Central Street, in the old foreign quarter of Daoli District.

Why transfer to Saint Sophia Cathedral?

The cathedral exists because of the railway. The Trans-Siberian route reached Vladivostok in 1903 and connected to northeast China soon after, and the Russian Fourth Army Division that stayed on in Manchuria needed a spiritual symbol large enough to face the whole settlement. The first church was finished in timber in March 1907; when Harbin's population reached 300,000, with 100,000 of them Russian, the building was extended in brick and finally completed in 1932. It was then the largest Orthodox church in the Far East and one of the last major works of Russian Revival architecture built in China.

Its modern history is just as interesting as its construction. After 1949 it was shut, its windows bricked up and its interior used first as a warehouse for a state department store and later as a rehearsal room for the Harbin Drama Studio. Concrete apartment blocks grew up on all four sides, close enough to hide it from the street. It was listed as a national cultural heritage site in 1996, and the surrounding blocks were later demolished to open a plaza in front of it. It no longer functions as a church and instead houses the Harbin Architecture Art Museum.

The architecture rewards a closer look. The main hall is laid out as a cross with a huge green-tipped dome, the design was inspired by the Cathedral of Christ the Saviour in Birky, Ukraine, and the brickwork of the facades carries the mosaic patterns typical of Russian Revival churches. At 721 square metres of floor area and 53.3 metres to the top of the dome, it is smaller than it looks, and the interior galleries and painted ceiling reward a few minutes inside.

  • Closed from 1958 to 1976 and used as a warehouse, then a drama studio rehearsal room

What to do at Saint Sophia Cathedral once you arrive

Time the visit for the hour before dusk, when the floodlit dome against a dark sky is the image everyone comes for, and the floodlights on the plaza come on at night. In daylight the green dome and the brick colour read better from the corner of Toulong and Zhaolin Streets, looking back along the plaza with the onion domes behind. The square in front has a paving pattern and a statue of the city's founders that are worth a slow circle.

The immediate surroundings are the real attraction. Central Street begins a short walk north and is lined with the pastel and brick facades of the former foreign concessions, now bars, restaurants and shops selling Russian goods, dried milk candy and gingerbread. The Sophia Cathedral square sits at the foot of it, so you can spend an evening walking the street after the cathedral and stay for dinner in a restored concession-era building.

  • Admission is typically 20 yuan for adults and 15 yuan for minors, with the museum inside the cathedral
  • Opening hours are roughly 08:30 to 17:00, with shorter hours in the winter season
  • Central Street, the old concession block, is a five-minute walk north from the cathedral
  • Ice lanterns in the surrounding district are the reason many visitors come in February

Arriving at Harbin Taiping International Airport

Taiping is in the south-west of the city, built in 1979 as Yanjiagang Airport and upgraded to international status in 1984, when it began serving routes to Russia. It handled 24.6 million passengers in 2025 and is the 22nd busiest airport in mainland China. Two terminals operate side by side, with international flights handled in the older building a mile north of the domestic Terminal 2, so check which terminal your flight uses before setting off for the city.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get from Harbin Taiping airport to Saint Sophia Cathedral?

Taiping Airport is about 37 km south-west of the city centre and the cathedral is in Daoli District, so the road distance is roughly 42 km. The drive takes about 55 minutes in clear conditions. In winter, ice and snow on the expressway can add 15 to 20 minutes, and after heavy snow the airport access roads are the slowest part of the trip.

How much does it cost to enter Saint Sophia Cathedral?

Admission is generally 20 yuan for adults and 15 yuan for minors, and the ticket covers the Harbin Architecture Art Museum housed inside the cathedral. It is one of the cheaper major sights in Harbin, and the interior galleries, painted ceilings and stained glass are worth the small fee on their own.

Is Saint Sophia Cathedral still a working church?

No. It stopped functioning as a church after 1949 and was closed completely from 1958 through the Cultural Revolution, first serving as a warehouse for a state department store and later as a rehearsal room for the Harbin Drama Studio. It was restored as a museum in the 1990s and now houses the Harbin Architecture Art Museum.

What is the best time of day to visit?

Late afternoon into dusk. The floodlights on the dome and the onion domes behind it make the cathedral the city's best night image, and the plaza is lit from the same circuit. If you prefer the brick and mosaic detail in daylight, arrive in the morning, when the light is cleaner and the plaza is empty.

Can I combine the cathedral with Central Street?

Yes, and it is the natural pairing. Central Street, the main commercial stretch of the old Russian and foreign concessions, begins at the cathedral square and runs for about a kilometre. Between the cathedral and the street you can cover the museum, the floodlit plaza and a dinner in a converted concession building within two or three hours.
