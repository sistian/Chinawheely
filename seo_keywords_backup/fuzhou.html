Key Fact

Name Origin

Fuzhou (福州) means 'Prosperous City' or 'Blessed City.' It has been an important port and cultural center in Fujian for over 2,200 years, serving as the capital of the Minyue kingdom and later as a key maritime city during the Tang and Song dynasties.

Education & Universities

Fuzhou is a respected education center in southeast China. Fuzhou University is a key national university known for engineering and chemistry. Fujian Normal University is one of the oldest normal universities in China, and Fujian Medical University is also well-regarded.

Metro & Transportation

Fuzhou Metro has 5 lines connecting the city center, railway stations, and the airport. It is modern and efficient, but reaching Drum Mountain, the Min River area, and ancient towns is easier with a private car.

Natural Resources & Environment

Fuzhou lies on the Min River near the coast of the Taiwan Strait, surrounded by low hills and lush greenery. The city is known for its many Banyan trees, mild climate, and frequent rainfall. It has a relaxed, green atmosphere compared to busier coastal cities.

Main city center station · Gulou district

The main railway station serving conventional and high-speed trains to Xiamen, Quanzhou, Shanghai, and other cities. Connected to Metro Line 1.

Food & Shopping in Fuzhou

🍽️ Must-Try Fuzhou Cuisine

Fuzhou Fish Balls (Yu Wan)

Springy fish balls with a savory meat filling. A signature Fuzhou snack found in restaurants and street stalls.

Buddha Jumps Over the Wall (Fo Tiao Qiang)

A luxurious soup with abalone, sea cucumber, shark fin, and chicken, slow-cooked in a clay pot. Fuzhou's most famous banquet dish.

Oyster Omelette (Hao Jian)

Fresh oysters fried with eggs and sweet potato starch, served with chili sauce. A popular Fujian coastal snack.

Rice Paste and Rice Cakes

Soft rice paste noodles and sweet rice cakes are common Fuzhou breakfast and snack items.

Intangible Cultural Heritage & Living Crafts of Fuzhou

Meet the traditional arts that have shaped Fuzhou's identity for centuries.

🎭 Min Opera (Fuzhou Opera)

A traditional opera form from Fujian, known for its melodious singing, elegant movements, and elaborate costumes. It is one of the major opera styles of southeast China.

Where to find it: Fuzhou theatres and cultural festivals.

🎨 Fuzhou Bodiless Lacquerware

A unique craft where objects are made from lacquer-soaked cloth and then polished and decorated. It is one of China's three great lacquer arts.

Where to find it: Lacquerware shops and museums in Fuzhou.

🎋 Fuzhou Soft Wood Carving

Traditional wood carving using soft, lightweight wood to create intricate figures and scenes.

Where to find it: Craft markets and cultural centers in Fuzhou.

🎶 Fujian Nanyin Music

An ancient genre of Chinese chamber music, recognized by UNESCO, featuring flutes, lutes, and percussion.

Where to find it: Cultural performances and traditional tea houses in Fuzhou.

✂️ Fuzhou Paper Cutting

Delicate folk paper art with themes from Fujian opera, local legends, and auspicious symbols.

Where to find it: Folk art shops and festival markets.

🍵 Fujian Tea Ceremony Arts

The traditional arts of tea preparation, tasting, and appreciation, deeply tied to Fujian's oolong and jasmine tea cultures.

Where to find it: Tea houses and specialty tea shops across Fuzhou.

6 Things to Do in Fuzhou

Make the most of your visit to Fuzhou with these handpicked experiences — all easily accessible with a private car and English-speaking driver.

🏯

Walk the Three Lanes and Seven Alleys (三坊七巷)

Fuzhou's most famous historic district, a perfectly preserved grid of Tang and Song dynasty lanes lined with traditional Fujian architecture. The area comes alive in the evening with tea houses, craft shops, and local snack stalls.

⛩️

Visit the Lin Zexu Memorial Hall (林则徐纪念馆)

The former residence of Lin Zexu, China's most celebrated historical figure, who led the anti-opium campaign against British traders in the 19th century. A beautifully restored compound with excellent historical exhibits.

🛶

Take a Boat Ride on the Min River

The Min River flows through the heart of Fuzhou and is best appreciated from the water. Evening boat cruises pass the Fuzhou's illuminated skyline and the historic Gulang Island in the distance.

🍜

Eat Fuzhou's Famous Fish Ball Noodles (鱼丸汤)

The signature dish of Fuzhou cuisine — handmade fish balls with a filling of sweet pork, served in a delicate clear broth. The best versions are found at the old city restaurants in the Three Lanes district.

🏯

Explore the Fuzhou Pagoda (白塔)

One of Fuzhou's two iconic pagodas, offering panoramic views of the city from its hilltop location in the Yu Shan National Park. The surrounding park is also home to the Fuzhou Zoo and the famous Fuzhou Orchid Garden.

🌿

Visit the Fuzhou National Forest Park

A massive urban forest park at the foot of Robin Mountain with excellent hiking trails, a botanical garden, and views of the Fuzhou coast from the summit. A refreshing escape from the city.

Best season to travel in Fuzhou

🍂 Best Season October – December / March – April — October–December offers the most comfortable temperatures and the famous Fuzhou osmanthus in bloom across the city's parks. March–April is equally pleasant with blooming flowers for walking the Three Lanes and Seven Alleys.

🌸
Spring March – April 14°C–24°C 🌸 Best

The best time to visit. Comfortable temperatures, blooming flowers across the city, and pleasant conditions for walking the Three Lanes and Seven Alleys.

🌧️
Plum Rain May – June 20°C–30°C Avoid

The monsoon season brings frequent rain and high humidity. The old city's alleys are atmospheric in the rain. Indoor attractions are preferable during downpours.

☀️
Summer July – September 26°C–38°C Avoid

Hot, humid, and prone to typhoons. Early morning visits to the Three Lanes district are best. The coastal areas provide some relief.

🍂
Autumn October – December 14°C–26°C 🍂 Best

The second-best season. Osmanthus flowers fill the city's parks with fragrance. Comfortable temperatures for walking the old streets and Min River boat rides.

☕ Tea Culture & Tea House Guide in Fuzhou

Fuzhou has a rich and distinctive tea culture shaped by its geography, history, and local traditions. From world-famous teas like Longjing and Biluochun to unique local tea customs, here is your complete guide to tea in Fuzhou.

☕ White Tea

☕

Fuzhou Fuding White Tea (福鼎白茶) Culture

Venue: Fuzhou tea markets, Fuding (day trip)   Hours: Year-round, best in spring

Fujian is famous for white tea, particularly from Fuding (福鼎) county. Fuding White Tea (福鼎白茶) includes varieties like Bai Meigan (白牡丹) and Gongmei (贡眉), known worldwide for its delicate, sweet flavor and health benefits. Fuzhou as the provincial capital is the best place to taste and learn about Fujian white tea.

☕ Premium Green Tea

☕

Fuzhou Lianhua Tea (莲花茶) and Flower Tea Culture

Venue: Three Lanes and Seven Alleys (三坊七巷) traditional teahouses   Hours: Year-round

Fuzhou has unique tradition of flower teas, particularly lotus tea (莲花茶) made by placing tea leaves inside a lotus flower overnight so the tea absorbs lotus fragrance. This labor-intensive traditional tea is a specialty of Fuzhou served in traditional teahouses in the historic Three Lanes and Seven Alleys (三坊七巷) area.

☕ Oolong Tea

☕

Fuzhou Wuyi Rock Tea (武夷岩茶) - Da Hong Pao

Venue: Wuyi Mountains (武夷山), day trip from Fuzhou   Hours: Year-round, best in spring

Fuzhou is the closest major city to the famous Wuyi Mountains (武夷山), the origin of Da Hong Pao (大红袍) and other famous rock teas (武夷岩茶). These oolong teas are grown on rocky cliffs of the Wuyi Mountains known for their distinctive mineral flavor and orchid aroma.

☕

Fuzhou Oolong Tea Culture - Fujian Tea Capital

Venue: Fuzhou tea markets and specialty teahouses   Hours: Year-round

Fujian province produces some of Chinas most famous teas and Fuzhou as the provincial capital is the best place to experience the full range of Fujian tea culture including oolong teas from Wuyi Mountains, white teas from Fuding, and premium Tieguanyin from Anxi.

☕ Tea Culture

☕

Fuzhou Three Lanes and Seven Alleys Tea Culture (三坊七巷茶文化)

Venue: Three Lanes and Seven Alleys (三坊七巷)   Hours: Daily 09:00-21:00

The historic Three Lanes and Seven Alleys (三坊七巷) district is the heart of Fuzhou tea culture. Traditional teahouses in this area serve Fuding white tea, Wuyi rock tea, and regional Fujian teas in a setting of beautifully preserved Ming and Qing dynasty architecture.

Min Opera Performances (闽剧)

Fuzhou is the cultural centre of northern Fujian. The Fujian Provincial Opera House performs Min opera (闽剧) throughout Spring Festival -- a unique Fujian art form with distinct dialect, music, and performance style.

🥟

Chinese Spring Festival Food -- Fujian Style

Fujian cuisine is famous for its light, delicate flavours. Spring Festival foods include fengjian (封肉 -- large pork belly stew), fish balls, and the famous Fujian rice wine.

Top Spring Festival Activities

Fuzhou Sanfang Qixiang Historical District

The Sanfang Qixiang (三坊七巷) historical district -- Fuzhou most famous cultural area -- is beautifully decorated during Spring Festival. Red lanterns, traditional architecture, and street performers create a festive atmosphere. Free.

Fuzhou Fish Balls (鱼丸)

Fuzhou famous fish balls (鱼丸) -- smooth fish paste balls filled with pork, served in a delicate clear broth. A comfort food during the January holiday.

Fuzhou Nian Gao (年糕)

Fuzhou nian gao is typically sweet and soft -- made from glutinous rice flour. Steamed and sliced. Symbolises better year after year.

🏆 Major Sports Events in Fuzhou

Fuzhou is southeastern China's historic maritime sports city -- host of the Fuzhou Marathon and home to Fujian's football and basketball teams. The coastal marathon course along the Min River is spectacular.

🏃

Fuzhou Marathon (福州马拉松)

2026: December (annual)

The Fuzhou Marathon (福州马拉松) passes the Min River (闽江), the historic Three Lanes and Seven Alleys (三坊七巷), and the coastal areas. 25,000 runners. One of southeastern China's most popular marathons.

Venue: Start: Kaiyuan Park (开元公园); Finish: Minjiang Park area. Metro: Dou巷 (Line 1).

⚽

Chinese Super League Football (中超联赛 – 福建鯤舟)

2026 season: March to November

Fujian's CSL club (福建鯤舟) competes at the Fuzhou Olympic Sports Centre (福州奥体中心). The 2026 season marks an important chapter for football in Fujian province.

Venue: Fuzhou Olympic Sports Centre (福州奥体中心), Jin'an District. Metro: Fuzhou Olympic Sports Centre (Line 1).

🏀

Fujian Sturgeons CBA Basketball (福建晋江文旅)

2026-2027 season: October 2026 to May 2027

The Fujian Sturgeons (福建晋江文旅) are one of CBA's established teams. Home games in Jinjiang (near Quanzhou). Known for fast-paced offensive basketball with strong three-point shooting.

Venue: Quanzhou or Jinjiang sports venues. Occasional games in Fuzhou.

🌊

Fuzhou Open Water Swimming – Min River Crossing (闽江公开水域游泳)

2026: September (annual)

Open-water swimming event in the Min River (闽江) in Fuzhou. Multiple distance categories. Historic waterway with scenic urban views.

Venue: Min River (闽江), Fuzhou. Spectator access: riverside promenade.
