Complete seasonal guide for planning your Zhengzhou trip

Overview: Best Time to Visit Zhengzhou

The best time to visit Zhengzhou is during April to May and September to October. Zhengzhou experiences distinct seasons, each offering unique travel experiences for sightseeing tours, photography expeditions, and cultural exploration. Shaolin Temple, Yellow River, ancient capital, shaolin kung fu.

Quick Facts About Zhengzhou Weather

Spring: 10-20C - Mild and comfortable for outdoor activities

Summer: 24-34C - Warm, great for indoor attractions and evening tours

Autumn: 12-22C - Cool and pleasant, peak travel season

Winter: -2-8C - Cold but unique winter scenery

Spring in Zhengzhou (10-20C)

Mild temperatures perfect for visiting Shaolin Temple. Comfortable for outdoor activities and kung fu performances.

Summer in Zhengzhou (24-34C)

Hot and dry with temperatures reaching 34C. Early morning visits to Shaolin Temple recommended.

Autumn in Zhengzhou (12-22C)

Comfortable temperatures making it peak season for travel. Great weather for Yellow River tours and city sightseeing.

Winter in Zhengzhou (-2-8C)

Cold with temperatures dropping below freezing. Unique winter scenery. Fewer tourists, lower prices.
