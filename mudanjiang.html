Name Origin

Mudanjiang means "Mudan River" in Chinese. It is located in southeastern Heilongjiang and is famous for Jingpo Lake and the Russian border.

Education & Universities

Mudanjiang Normal University and Mudanjiang Medical University are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Mudanjiang Hailang Airport (MDG) has domestic flights. Mudanjiang Railway Station connects to Harbin and other Northeast cities. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Mudanjiang lies in mountainous eastern Heilongjiang with a humid continental climate. It has forests, rivers, Jingpo Lake, and volcanic landscapes.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Mudanjiang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Mudanjiang

🍵 Tea Houses of Mudanjiang

Tea culture runs through the day in Mudanjiang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
