Name Origin

Wulingyuan District (simplified Chinese: 武陵源区; traditional Chinese: 武陵源區; pinyin: Wǔlíngyuán Qū) is one of two urban districts in Zhangjiajie City, Hunan Province, China.

Education & Universities

Higher education in Wulingyuan is provided by the city's local colleges, vocational schools and teacher-training institutions rather than by large research universities.

Getting Around

Getting around Wulingyuan is mostly by road, with buses and taxis in the urban area and long-distance coaches to the surrounding counties.

Natural Resources & Environment

Located on the central area of Zhangjiajie, the district is surrounded by Cili County to the northeast and southeast, to the north by Sangzhi County, and to the south and southwest by Yongding District.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Wulingyuan

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Wulingyuan

🍵 Tea Houses of Wulingyuan

Tea culture runs through the day in Wulingyuan: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
