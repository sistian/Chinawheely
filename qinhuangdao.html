Name Origin

Qinhuangdao means "island of Qin Shi Huang" in Chinese, named after the first emperor who sought immortality here. It is a famous coastal city in Hebei.

Education & Universities

Yanshan University and Northeastern University at Qinhuangdao are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Qinhuangdao Beidaihe Airport (BPE) has domestic flights. Qinhuangdao Railway Station and Beidaihe Station connect to Beijing and Tianjin on the Beijing–Harbin high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Qinhuangdao lies on the Bohai Sea coast with a humid continental climate. It has beaches, mountains, and is a major summer resort and port city.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Qinhuangdao

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Qinhuangdao

🍵 Tea Houses of Qinhuangdao

Tea culture runs through the day in Qinhuangdao: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
