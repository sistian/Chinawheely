Name Origin

Baicheng means "white city" in Chinese, referring to the pale alkaline soil and salt flats of the region. It is located in northwestern Jilin on the Songnen Plain.

Education & Universities

Baicheng Normal University is the main institution of higher education in the city.

Getting Around

Baicheng Chang'an Airport is under development; the nearest operational airport is Ulanhot. Baicheng Railway Station connects the city to Changchun and other Northeast cities via conventional rail. Buses and taxis are available.

Natural Resources & Environment

Baicheng is part of the western Jilin grassland region. Nearby wetlands include Momoge National Nature Reserve, an important stopover for migratory birds including cranes.

Food & Shopping in Baicheng

What to eat and what to bring home from Baicheng

🍽️ Must-Try Baicheng Cuisine

Try Northeastern Chinese dishes such as stewed pork with vermicelli, grilled lamb skewers, pickled cabbage, and fresh fish from the wetlands.

🛍️ Shopping in Baicheng

Local specialties include grassland mushrooms, sunflower seeds, millet, and Mongolian-style dairy products. Wetland-related souvenirs are also available.

🍵 Tea Culture

Traditional Baicheng teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

🚗 Private Car for a Food Crawl

A private car and driver lets you start at the morning market, cross the city for lunch, and finish at an evening teahouse without worrying about parking or the last bus back to your hotel.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Baicheng

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Baicheng

🍵 Tea Houses of Baicheng

Tea culture runs through the day in Baicheng: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
