Seasons Overview

The climate record of Jinan, month by month.

Book a Jinan ride All Jinan services Nearby: Qingdao · Beijing · Zhengzhou

The annual average temperature is 14.7 °C; annual rainfall averages 673 mm; July is the hottest month at 27.5 °C; January the coldest at -0.4 °C; the climate pattern includes continental influence, the monsoon, drought, humid heat.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 8.2–21.8 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 89 mm.

Highlights: Daming Lake, Baotu Spring, Thousand Buddha Mountain

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 26.3–27.5 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 450 mm.

Highlights: Daming Lake, Baotu Spring, Thousand Buddha Mountain

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 8.3–22 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 111 mm.

Highlights: Daming Lake, Baotu Spring, Thousand Buddha Mountain

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run -0.4–2.2 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 22 mm.

Highlights: Daming Lake, Baotu Spring, Thousand Buddha Mountain
