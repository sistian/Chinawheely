Overview: Day Trips from Chongqing

Chongqing is a municipality of 80,000 square kilometres with a city of thirty million in the middle of it, and its day trips are all about getting out of the river valley. The city itself is stacked in three tiers against the Yangtze and the Jialing, so the interesting ground is above and below: a karst country west of the city with three natural bridges and two thousand metres of snow line, a cliff of Buddhist sculpture an hour to the west, and, four hours upriver, a fortress at the mouth of one of the Three Gorges where an emperor handed over his son.

The Three Natural Bridges at Wulong (天生三桥)

The Three Natural Bridges stand at Xianvushan in Wulong district, part of the Wulong karst national geopark and a national 5A site. On 7 June 2007 they were entered as part of the South China Karst on the UNESCO World Natural Heritage list. The protected core covers about 22 square kilometres around the Yangshui river, a tributary of the Wujiang, and includes the three bridges themselves, the Qinglong and Shenying sinkholes, the Longshui Gorge, the Chishiyuan and Xishi yuan sinkholes, the cave system of the Seventy-two Forks and the Longquan Cave, and the karst canyon of the river. The scale of the formation is in the numbers: from the upper end of the Tianlong Bridge to the lower end of the Heilong Bridge the straight-line distance is only 1,500 metres, which the sources describe as the only such case in the world, and the two sinkholes between the bridges measure 276 to 285 metres in depth and 300 to 522 metres across.

How the day runs: This is the longest drive of the six and it has to start early. Leave Chongqing at 06:30 for the 180-kilometre run to Wulong and be at the visitor centre by 09:30. The walking route descends through one of the sinkholes, crosses the three bridges in sequence and climbs out through the other, and it takes two and a half hours on the steps. The lift at the exit saves the last climb for anyone who has walked the first two bridges, which is the sensible way to do it. Allow another hour for the bus between the two park areas, have lunch in Xianvushan, and leave by 15:30 for the return, which is a three-and-a-half-hour run.

180 km from Chongqing · about 210 min by car from Chongqing · full-day trip · best in October to April; the karst holds water and ice in winter and the paths can be slick.

  • The three bridges are crossed in order and the surface changes completely between them, so the order matters for the photographs.
  • The steps between the sinkhole floor and the bridge deck are steep and can be wet, so shoes with grip matter more here than anywhere else on this list.
  • The exit lift is worth taking, because the alternative is a climb out after three hours of walking.

Dazu Rock Carvings (大足石刻)

The Dazu carvings are cliff sculpture cut into the rock of the Baodingshan and Beishan hills, begun in 650 in the first year of the Yonghui era of the early Tang, carried through the late Tang and the Five Dynasties, at their height in the two Song dynasties between 960 and 1278, and added to again between the fourteenth and nineteenth centuries. The whole group numbers more than 70 sites and more than 100,000 statues, with five clusters the most important: Baodingshan, Beishan, Nanshan, Shizuanshan and Shimenshan. The Baodingshan and Beishan carvings were listed as national key cultural relics in 1961, and in 1999 the Dazu carvings were inscribed as a World Heritage site. The programme is Buddhist in subject but the composition is a fusion of the three teachings, which is what makes it the leading example of late Chinese cave art.

How the day runs: Leave Chongqing at 08:00 for the 90-kilometre run and reach Baodingshan at 09:30. The Baodingshan cliff is a single walk of about 2 kilometres along the rock face with the major groups in sequence, and two hours is the right allowance; the figures are lit by the overhangs rather than by the sun, which is why the mid-morning light does most of the work. Lunch in the town, then spend the afternoon on the Beishan carvings, which are on a separate hillside and are more formal and less crowded. Leave by 16:00 for the return to the city.

90 km from Chongqing · about 120 min by car from Chongqing · full-day trip · best in All year; the carvings are under rock overhangs and are best in low, angled light.

  • Baodingshan and Beishan are different sites on different hills, so allow a drive between them rather than looking for a walk.
  • The carvings sit in recesses under rock overhangs, so raking daylight from the side reaches deeper into them than a wide view does.
  • The Thousand-Armed Avalokitesvara at Baodingshan is the figure people come for; the thirty or so numbered groups around it are the reason to stay.

Simian Mountain and its Waterfalls (四面山)

Simian Shan lies in Simian Mountain town in Jiangjin district, on the northern spur of the Dalou mountains of the Yunnan-Guizhou plateau, 130 kilometres from the central city of Chongqing and 90 kilometres from the seat of Jiangjin. The scenic area covers 213.37 square kilometres at altitudes between 1,000 and 1,500 metres, with the highest point, Wugongling, at 1,709.4 metres and the lowest at 560 metres. It was listed as the third batch of national key scenic areas in 1994 and rated a national 5A site in October 2015. The core scenic points number 128, and the waterfalls are the defining feature, with more than 100 of them and eight of around a hundred metres, the largest being the Wangxiangtai fall at 152 metres high and 40 metres wide, which is the highest in China. The primitive forest is what the area is valued for, and it is described as a natural gene bank of wild plant resources.

How the day runs: Leave Chongqing at 08:00 for the 130-kilometre run and arrive at the visitor centre around 10:40. The area is large and the transport between the five core scenic sections runs on internal buses, so the day is a loop rather than a single walk: take the bus to the upper section at Wangxiangtai first, walk down through the forest, and have lunch in the Honghai or Longtan Lake section. Allow five hours in the area and leave by 15:30, which puts you back in the city at 18:30.

130 km from Chongqing · about 160 min by car from Chongqing · full-day trip · best in May to September for the waterfalls at full volume; October to December for the colour in the forest.

  • The internal transport is the only practical way between the sections, and the loop is what the layout is designed for.
  • The Wangxiangtai fall is 152 metres, so it needs a wide frame and a low viewpoint to hold in the picture.
  • The forest is the point as much as the water, and October to December is the better season for the colour.

Jinfo Mountain (金佛山)

Jinfo Shan stands in Nanchuan district in the south of the municipality, on the Dalu mountain range, and is built from three peaks, Jinfo, Qingba and Baizhi. Its main summit, Fengchui Ling, reaches 2,251 metres and carries snow for four months of the year, which makes it the highest point of the Dalu range. It was inscribed as a World Natural Heritage site for its biodiversity and its karst landforms, and it holds eight national designations at once, including a national key scenic area, a national forest park and a national nature reserve. The name comes from the sight itself: on a clear evening in late summer the low sun turns the layered cliffs gold, and the range looks like a row of golden Buddhas.

How the day runs: Leave Chongqing at 07:00 for the 180-kilometre run to Nanchuan and arrive at the scenic gate by 10:00. The main summit section is reached by the internal transport, and the day splits between the cliff walk on the upper mountain and the lower sections of ancient temple sites and bamboo forest. The sunset on the cliffs is the reason the area is named, so if the weather holds, stay for it and drive back after dark, which is a two-hour run on a lit highway. Otherwise leave at 15:00 and be back before dark.

180 km from Chongqing · about 210 min by car from Chongqing · full-day trip · best in December to March for the snow; September to October for the sunset light on the cliffs.

  • The four-month snow season is the reason for the altitude, and it is the least crowded time on the mountain.
  • The golden cliff light happens on clear summer evenings, not in the snow season, so the two best photographs need different days.
  • The internal transport saves an hour of climbing, and the summit section is the part that needs it.

Geleshan and the Prison Museum (歌乐山与渣滓洞白公馆)

Geleshan is a 693-metre peak in Shapingba district, part of the Zhongliang mountains, with karst limestone and old coal workings under it. The hill carries three sites that are the reason to go. The first is the former site of the Sino-American Special Technical Cooperation Office, the secret intelligence headquarters of the war years. The second is Baigongguan and Zhazidong, the prison complexes of the second civil war. The third is the Geleshan Martyrs' Cemetery, built by the government of the People's Republic, where the martyrs of both the anti-Japanese war and the liberation struggle are buried. The local dish called Geleshan chilli chicken takes its name from the hill.

How the day runs: Allow half a day and take a car or the metro to the foot of the hill. The cemetery and the prison sites are within a short walk of each other and the road up the hill is short, so the whole sequence takes three hours. Go early, because both the prison museums and the cemetery are quiet places and the midday buses bring the school groups. Finish with the chilli chicken at one of the restaurants at the foot of the hill, which is where the dish belongs.

12 km from Chongqing · about 35 min by car from Chongqing · half-day trip · best in All year; it is indoors and within the city, and works in any weather.

  • The three sites are separate but close, and a half-day is enough for all of them.
  • Both prison museums are preserved in their original state, which is a different kind of photograph from a restored monument.
  • The hill is limestone and karst, so the paths are stepped; the museum buildings are the easy part of the visit.

Baidi City at the Mouth of Qutang Gorge (白帝城)

Baidi Cheng stands on the north bank of the Yangtze, 8 kilometres east of the county seat of Fengjie, at the mouth of the Qutang Gorge, with the mountain behind it and water on three sides. It was announced as a sixth-batch national key cultural relic on 25 May 2006. The name comes from Gongsun Shu, the warlord of Shu, who built a fortress here and took the title of Bai Di when a well in the town gave off white vapour like a white dragon; after his death the local people built the White Emperor Temple for him. In the second month of 223 Liu Bei left his son and his kingdom in the hands of Zhuge Liang from this place, which is the event the later shrines commemorate, and the Ming and Qing buildings include a Zhuge Liang shrine and a Liu Bei shrine as well. The gate carries the three characters written by Guo Moruo and a couplet from Du Fu's poems on the region. Since the Three Gorges project raised the water level, the site now stands surrounded by water and excursion boats reach it directly.

How the day runs: This is the longest of the six day trips on this page and it needs an early start. Leave Chongqing at 05:30 for the 280-kilometre run up the Yangtze, allowing for the mountain roads after Fengjie, and reach the town by 10:00. The climb from the river gate to the fortress is a long flight of steep stone steps, and it is worth taking slowly. Two hours covers the temples and the terraces with the gorge in view, and the poet's line about the place being the strongest of the Three Gorges and the Qutang pass the most dangerous of the passes is the one written on the gate. Lunch in Fengjie and leave by 14:00, which is a four-hour return.

280 km from Chongqing · about 240 min by car from Chongqing · full-day trip · best in October to March; the gorge haze is thickest in summer and the river level changes with the Three Gorges project.

  • The steps from the landing to the gate are the steepest part, so the timing has to allow for them at both ends of the day.
  • The gorge view is the subject and it is best from the terraces at the top, not from the water.
  • With the Three Gorges project raising the level, the site is now an island approach by boat, which is part of the visit rather than a detail.

Planning day trips from Chongqing

The city is stacked vertically and the trips are not, so the rule for Chongqing is to spend the morning going up. The central city sits on the Yangtze and the Jialing in three tiers, and the karst country to the west and the north is 1,000 to 2,250 metres high, which turns the temperature around in an hour and a half. Four of the six destinations here are over ninety minutes away, so the departures are early and the days are long. The two that are not, the hill at Geleshan and the city's own riverfront, are the answer for a half-day and for bad weather.

Distance is also a poor guide in this municipality, because Chongqing covers about 80,000 square kilometres and the roads follow the rivers and the ridges rather than the straight line. The Wulong karst at 180 kilometres and Jinfo Mountain at 180 kilometres are on opposite sides of the range and share the same distance, while Baidi City at 280 kilometres up the Yangtze is a full day that feels like a different province. If there is a choice to make, make it in the morning and take the long run before the traffic builds on the western expressway.

Planning a day trip from Chongqing

Geleshan and the Prison Museum, Baidi City at the Mouth of Qutang Gorge and the 4 stops in between set the two ends of this Chongqing day trip route — Geleshan and the Prison Museum sits closest at 12 km, roughly 35 minutes by car, while Baidi City at the Mouth of Qutang Gorge reaches furthest at 280 km, about 240 minutes on the road — and a private car keeps the whole loop moving at your own pace rather than a timetable's.

Simian Mountain and its Waterfalls, Jinfo Mountain, Geleshan and the Prison Museum each carry a seasonal note of their own, so check those against the dates you actually have before you fix the Chongqing itinerary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which day trip is the essential one?

The Three Natural Bridges at Wulong, entered as part of the South China Karst on the UNESCO World Natural Heritage list on 7 June 2007. Three natural bridges span the same 1,500 metres of the Yangshui river, a combination the sources describe as unique in the world, the two sinkholes between them measure 276 to 285 metres deep and up to 522 metres across, and the protected core covers about 22 square kilometres of caves, sinkholes and karst canyon.

What is at Dazu?

Cliff sculpture of more than 100,000 statues in more than 70 sites, begun in 650 and at its height in the Song, with five main clusters at Baodingshan, Beishan, Nanshan, Shizuanshan and Shimenshan. Baodingshan and Beishan were listed as national key cultural relics in 1961 and the whole group was inscribed as a World Heritage site in 1999. The subject is Buddhist but the composition unites the three teachings, which is what makes it the leading example of late Chinese cave art.

Can you get real waterfalls near Chongqing?

Simian Mountain has more than 100 of them, eight of around a hundred metres, and the Wangxiangtai fall at 152 metres high and 40 metres wide is the highest in China. The area covers 213.37 square kilometres between 1,000 and 1,500 metres, with the highest point at 1,709.4 metres, and it has been a national key scenic area since 1994 and a 5A site since October 2015. It is 130 kilometres from the city.

Is there anything to do inside the city?

Geleshan, 12 kilometres out, with 693 metres of karst hill over Shapingba district. It carries the former Sino-American Special Technical Cooperation Office, the prisons of Baigongguan and Zhazidong from the second civil war, and the Geleshan Martyrs' Cemetery. It takes three hours, works in any weather, and the chilli chicken at the foot of the hill is the local dish named after it.

How far upriver is Baidi City?

280 kilometres, at the mouth of the Qutang Gorge and 8 kilometres east of Fengjie. The fortress was built by Gongsun Shu, the warlord of Shu, who took the title of Bai Di when a well gave off white vapour, and in the second month of 223 Liu Bei entrusted his son and his kingdom to Zhuge Liang there. It became a sixth-batch national key cultural relic on 25 May 2006, and since the Three Gorges project raised the water level the site is reached by boat.

What is the best season for the mountains?

They are three different answers. Simian Mountain for its waterfalls is May to September, and for the forest colour October to December. Jinfo Mountain, whose summit reaches 2,251 metres and carries snow for four months, is a winter trip in December to March and also the place for the golden sunset on the cliffs on clear late summer evenings. Wulong is best from October to April, when the karst paths are drier, and the two peaks need different days.
