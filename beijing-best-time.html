Seasons Overview

The climate record of Beijing, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 13.3 °C; annual rainfall averages 528 mm; July is the hottest month at 27.2 °C; January the coldest at -2.7 °C; the climate pattern includes continental influence, the monsoon, dust storms.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 7.5–21.3 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 66 mm.

Highlights: Forbidden City, Great Wall at Badaling, Great Wall at Mutianyu

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 25.3–27.2 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 356 mm.

Highlights: Forbidden City, Great Wall at Badaling, Great Wall at Mutianyu

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 5.2–21.2 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 96 mm.

Highlights: Forbidden City, Great Wall at Badaling, Great Wall at Mutianyu

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run -2.7–0.6 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 10 mm.

Highlights: Forbidden City, Great Wall at Badaling, Great Wall at Mutianyu
