China Visa Types

Choose the right visa based on your purpose of travel to China.

📋

Tourist Visa (L)

18-26°C · Warm & Humid

For leisure travel, sightseeing, visiting friends or family. Available as single, double, or multiple entry. Usually valid for 30-90 days per stay.

💼

Business Visa (M)

10-20°C · Cool & Crisp

For commercial activities, trade shows, business meetings. Requires an invitation letter from a registered Chinese company. Multiple entry options available.

🚆

Transit Visa (G)

Varies by season

For passing through China to a third destination. Available at most major international airports. Can be obtained on arrival at designated ports.

How to Apply for a China Visa

A step-by-step guide to the China visa application process.

01

Check Requirements

Determine your visa type based on your purpose: tourism (L), business (M), transit (G), or student (X). Check if your passport nationality qualifies for visa-free or 144-hour transit options.

02

Prepare Documents

Passport (6+ months validity, 2+ blank pages), completed Visa Application Form (V.2013), passport photo (48×33mm white background), round-trip flight itinerary, hotel confirmation, invitation letter (business).

03

Submit Application

Apply at the Chinese embassy, consulate, or China Visa Application Service Center (CVASC) in your country. Some countries allow postal submissions. Schedule an appointment online first.

04

Collect Passport

Standard processing: 4-10 business days. Express service: 1-2 business days (additional fee). Collect in person or by prepaid courier. Check your visa details carefully before leaving the counter.

Required Documents

The standard documents needed for a China visa application.

🛂

Valid Passport

Original passport with at least 6 months validity beyond your planned entry date, and at least 2 blank visa pages. Damaged passports may not be accepted.

📝

Visa Application Form

Completed and signed Visa Application Form (Form V.2013). One form per person. Can be completed online at cova.mfa.gov.cn and then printed.

📷

Passport Photo

One recent colour photo (48mm × 33mm) on white background. No glasses, no headwear (unless religious), no smiling showing teeth.

✈️

Flight Itinerary

Round-trip flight confirmation showing entry and exit dates from China. Can be a booking rather than a purchased ticket for initial application.

🏨

Hotel Confirmation

Hotel booking confirmation for your first night(s) in China, or a letter of invitation from a Chinese host with their address and contact details.

💼

Business Invitation (M Visa)

Invitation letter from a registered Chinese company on company letterhead, including the inviter's details, your details, purpose of visit, and dates.

144-Hour Transit Visa-Free Entry

If you are transiting through China to a third country, you may qualify for visa-free entry for up to 144 hours (6 days).

⚠️ Key Conditions:
  • Your nationality must be from an eligible country (54 countries including USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea, France, Germany, and most EU nations)
  • You must hold a valid passport with at least 3 months validity
  • You must have a confirmed onward ticket to a third country/region (not your country of origin for most ports)
  • You must enter and exit through the same port or designated area
  • No pre-application needed — just present your documents at immigration

Pro Tips for Your China Visa

⏰

Apply Early

Apply at least 4-6 weeks before your trip. Consulates can experience delays during Chinese public holidays. During peak seasons (Chinese New Year, Golden Week), allow extra time.

📋

Check Reciprocal Agreements

Some countries offer bilateral visa-free access to Chinese citizens — this may mean smoother visa processing for you. Check if your country has a reciprocal agreement with China.

🌐

Use the Official Portal

Complete your application at cova.mfa.gov.cn (China Online Visa Application). Only use official visa centers or the embassy directly. Beware of third-party sites that overcharge.
