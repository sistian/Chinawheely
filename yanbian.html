Name Origin

Yanbian means "edge of the Yan" in Chinese. It is a Korean Autonomous Prefecture in eastern Jilin on the border with North Korea and Russia.

Education & Universities

Yanbian University is the main higher-education institution in the prefecture.

Getting Around

Yanji Chaoyangchuan International Airport (YNJ) has domestic and international flights. Yanji Railway Station connects to Changchun and other cities. Buses and taxis serve the prefecture.

Natural Resources & Environment

Yanbian lies in the Changbai Mountains and the Tumen River basin with a continental climate. It has forests, mountains, and the border with North Korea and Russia.

Yanbian's Best Photo Spots

Capture the city from the most iconic and photogenic angles.

⛰️ 🌅 Jingshan Park Pavilion

Best at sunrise

Climb to the Wanchun Pavilion for the classic panoramic view over the golden rooftops of the Tianchi Lake. On clear days, you can see the CCTV tower and the western mountains in the distance. Arrive early to beat the crowds.

🏯 Tianchi Lake Corner Tower

Best at dusk

The northwest corner tower reflected in the moat is one of Yanbian's most photographed scenes. The sunset glow turns the water gold and the tower silhouette dramatic. Your driver can drop you at the northeast or northwest corner for the perfect shot.

🛶 the old quarter old-quarter lanes

Best in late afternoon

The lakeside old-quarter lanes around the lakeside lanes offer a charming mix of old Yanbian life, willow trees, and red lanterns. Take a rickshaw ride or wander the narrow alleys, then capture the golden hour light on the water.

🦅 🏙️ CCTV Headquarters

Best at blue hour

Also known as the "Big Pants," this modern architectural icon in the CBD looks best from the plaza below or from across the Third Ring Road. At night, the building is illuminated and reflected in the surrounding towers.

⛰️ 🧱 Yanbian local attraction

Best in early morning mist

For photographers seeking a dramatic, less-crowded Tianchi Lake shot, Tianchi Lake is unmatched. The wild, unrestored sections and watchtowers winding over mountain ridges create unforgettable sunrise and fog images.

🏯 🎨 798 Art District

Best on a sunny afternoon

Colorful murals, industrial brick buildings, and quirky sculptures make 798 a favorite for creative portraits. Every corner offers a different backdrop, from Soviet-era slogans to contemporary street art.

Yanbian Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Yanbian like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

Tianchi Lake is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Best Time to Visit Yanbian

Each season shows a different side of Yanbian.

🌸

Spring

Mar–May · 10–22°C

Mild and pleasant with occasional dust storms. Cherry blossoms at the parkland around Tianchi Lake. Moderate crowds.

Highlights: Tianchi Lake gardens, Tianchi Lake park

☀️

Summer

Jun–Aug · 22–32°C

Hot and humid with occasional heavy rain. Air conditioning is essential. Peak domestic tourist season.

Highlights: Tianchi Lake lotus, evening old-quarter walks

🍂

Autumn

Sep–Nov · 8–20°C

Clear skies, crisp air, and golden ginkgo trees. The most popular and comfortable season to visit.

Highlights: Tianchi Lake hiking, Ditan Park ginkgo

❄️

Winter

Dec–Feb · -6–5°C

Cold and dry with occasional snow. Fewer tourists and lower hotel prices. Indoor heating is excellent.

Highlights: Frozen the old quarter skating, hot pot dinners

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Yanbian

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Yanbian

🍵 Tea Houses of Yanbian

Tea culture runs through the day in Yanbian: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
