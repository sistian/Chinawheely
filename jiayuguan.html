Name Origin

Jiayuguan is named after the westernmost end of the Great Wall of China, a strategic fortress city in Gansu.

Education & Universities

Gansu University of Chinese Medicine (Jiayuguan campus) and Jiayuguan Vocational College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Jiayuguan Airport (JGN) has domestic flights. Jiayuguan Railway Station and South Station connect to Lanzhou and Urumqi on the Lanzhou–Xinjiang railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Jiayuguan lies at the Hexi Corridor's western end with arid continental climate, desert, mountains, and the Qilian Mountains to the south.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Jiayuguan

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Jiayuguan

🍵 Tea Houses of Jiayuguan

Tea culture runs through the day in Jiayuguan: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
