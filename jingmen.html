Name Origin

Jingmen means "Jing Mountain gate," named for its location at the entrance to the Jingshan Mountains in central Hubei.

Education & Universities

Jingchu University of Technology and Jingmen Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Jingmen has no commercial airport; Yichang Sanxia Airport is the nearest. Jingmen Railway Station and Jingmen South Station connect to Wuhan and Xiangyang on the Jiaozuo–Liuzhou railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Jingmen lies between the Dabie and Jingshan mountains with humid subtropical climate, rivers, lakes, and fertile farmland.

Jingmen Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Jingmen like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

Mawangdui-related Chu culture is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Jingmen

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Jingmen

🍵 Tea Houses of Jingmen

Tea culture runs through the day in Jingmen: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
