Name Origin of Xinxiang

Xinxiang means "new prosperity" in Chinese. It is located in northern Henan and is an important industrial city near Zhengzhou.

Xinxiang Education & Universities

Henan Normal University, Xinxiang Medical University, and Xinxiang University are main institutions.

Getting Around Xinxiang

Xinxiang has no commercial airport; Zhengzhou Xinzheng Airport is used. Xinxiang Railway Station and Xinxiang East Station connect to Zhengzhou and Beijing on the Beijing–Guangzhou high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Xinxiang Natural Resources & Environment

Xinxiang lies on the North China Plain with the Yellow River to the south. It has a temperate continental climate and is an important agricultural and industrial city.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Xinxiang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Xinxiang

🍵 Tea Houses of Xinxiang

Tea culture runs through the day in Xinxiang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
