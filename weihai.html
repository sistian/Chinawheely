Name Origin

Weihai means "awe-inspiring sea" in Chinese. It is a coastal city in eastern Shandong at the tip of the Shandong Peninsula and was a former British leased territory.

Education & Universities

Harbin Institute of Technology (Weihai campus), Shandong University (Weihai campus), and Weihai Vocational College are main institutions.

Getting Around

Weihai Dashuibo International Airport (WEH) has domestic flights. Weihai Railway Station and Weihai North Station connect to Qingdao and Yantai on the Qingdao–Rongcheng intercity railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Weihai lies on the Yellow Sea coast with a humid continental climate. It has beaches, islands, and is known as one of China's cleanest and most livable coastal cities.

Food & Shopping in Weihai

What to eat and what to bring home from Weihai

🍽️ Must-Try Weihai Cuisine

Weihai is famous for fresh seafood, especially sea cucumber, abalone, scallops, and grilled squid. Try local seafood BBQ.

🛍️ Shopping in Weihai

Buy dried seafood, pearl products, local snacks, and Korean goods (Weihai has a large Korean community). Main shopping is on Xinwei Road.

🍵 Tea Culture

Traditional Weihai teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

Weihai Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Weihai like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

First Sino-Japanese War museum is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Weihai

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Weihai

🍵 Tea Houses of Weihai

Tea culture runs through the day in Weihai: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
