Overview: Food & Shopping in Guangzhou

What to eat in Guangzhou and where the shopping actually happens.

The 1 dishes below are the ones Guangzhou is known for, each of them listed in the city's own encyclopaedia entry. Stopping at a market or a food street is easier with a car and a driver who knows where the stalls are, because the good ones are rarely on the main road.

What to Eat in Guangzhou

🍜 morning tea and dim sum

Local dish

morning tea and dim sum is on the local menu in Guangzhou. Ask for it by name — it is the dish regulars order, and the one worth stopping for while you are in town.

Where to Shop in Guangzhou

The shopping in Guangzhou is centred on 上下九步行街, where the local specialties are sold and the food stalls are set up around the same streets.
