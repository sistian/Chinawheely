Name Origin

Jining means "peaceful and prosperous" in Chinese. It is located in southwestern Shandong and is famous as the birthplace of Confucius and Mencius.

Education & Universities

Qufu Normal University and Jining Medical University are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Jining has no commercial airport; Jining Qufu Airport (JNG) serves some flights. Jining Railway Station and Jining North Station connect to Beijing and Shanghai on the Beijing–Shanghai high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Jining lies on the North China Plain with the Grand Canal running through it. It has a temperate continental climate and is known for its coal resources.

Jining Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Jining like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

UNESCO is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Jining

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Jining

🍵 Tea Houses of Jining

Tea culture runs through the day in Jining: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
