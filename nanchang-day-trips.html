Overview: Day Trips from Nanchang

Nanchang stands in the hinterland of the Poyang Lake plain in north-central Jiangxi, with the Jiuling Mountains closing the western side of the valley and the Gan River running past the city wall before it enters the lake. That geography produces three different kinds of outing: riverfront monuments inside the ring road, revolutionary memorial sites at 140 kilometres to the south-west, and a mountain resort 150 kilometres to the north-east. The distances are short, the roads are straightforward, and every destination below fits inside a single working day with a car.

Tengwang Pavilion (滕王阁)

Tengwang Pavilion stands on the east bank of the Gan River in north-west Nanchang, one of the three great towers of the Jiangnan region, with Yueyang and Yellow Crane Tower as the other two. It was founded in 653, the fourth year of the Yonghui era, by Li Yuanbing, Prince of Teng and second son of the Tang founder Li Yuan, then governor of Hongzhou. It was repaired 28 times in the centuries after: five under the Tang, one under the Song, two under the Yuan, seven under the Ming and thirteen under the Qing. The structure standing today was rebuilt on the original site in 1989 to a design based on sketches by Liang Sicheng, and has nine storeys.

How the day runs: Allow three hours. Drive to the pavilion at the north-west end of the city and reach the entrance by 08:40, before the coach groups. The building runs from the ground floor to the ninth storey, and the climb is the point of the visit, so take the stairs rather than the lift and stop at the upper levels where the river bend and the city roof line are laid out on the western side. The upper levels also carry the tablets and the reconstructed painted beams that record the building history. Finish with an hour along the river bank under the wall, then return to the centre by 12:30, which leaves the afternoon free for the uprising memorial.

6 km from Nanchang · about 20 min by car from Nanchang · half-day trip · best in All year; the pavilion and the river frontage are at their quietest between November and February.

  • The ninth storey is the highest public level and the narrowest, so the queue for it is longest between 10:00 and 11:30.
  • The river side of the pavilion faces west, so the late afternoon light falls across the painted beams rather than the front elevation.
  • The memorial museum and this pavilion are on opposite sides of the city centre, which makes them a natural half-day pair.

August 1st Uprising Memorial Museum (八一起义纪念馆)

The museum is built on the site of the former headquarters of the August 1st Uprising, in the Guangrunmen quarter of the Xihu district, and was founded in 1956 and opened on 1 October 1959. In 1961 the group of old sites, including the headquarters, the He Long, Ye Ting and Zhu De command posts and the officers school, was entered in the first national register of key cultural relics. The main building was the Jiangxi Grand Hotel, built in 1923 as a five-storey brick and timber structure with a courtyard plan, 96 rooms, a frontage of 26 metres and a depth of 42 metres. A major repair was completed in 2007 and the site has been free since 2008.

How the day runs: Go early, with two hours for the museum and the surrounding old buildings. The exhibit runs through the ground and upper floors of the reconstructed headquarters, with the 1923 hotel as the anchor and the other command post sites spread around the block near the Wanshou Palace precinct. The site's own scale is the exhibit: 96 rooms across five storeys is the number that explains how a headquarters and several offices functioned inside one building. In 2008 the museum recorded 1.528 million visitors, rising to 2.035 million in 2009, which is a measure of the interest in the site. Return to the hotel by 12:00.

4 km from Nanchang · about 15 min by car from Nanchang · half-day trip · best in All year; the museum is an indoor site and the surrounding old quarter is comfortable from October to April.

  • The command post sites are separate buildings in the surrounding streets, so allow time to walk between them rather than expecting everything under one roof.
  • Admission has been free since 2008, but identity documents are still checked at the entrance.
  • The 1923 hotel building is the oldest fabric on the site; the exhibition halls around it are modern and well cooled.

Jinggangshan Mountain Resort (井冈山)

Jinggangshan lies in the middle section of the Luoxiao Mountains on the Jiangxi and Hunan boundary, 140 kilometres south-west of Nanchang, and the city covers 1,308.58 square kilometres with a population of about 150,000 across 21 townships and subdistricts. Its average elevation is 381.5 metres and its highest point, Jiangxi'ao, reaches 1,841 metres, while the range's highest peak rises to 2,120 metres. The scenic area covers 213.5 square kilometres and is divided into eight sections: Ciping, Huangyangjie, Longtan, the main peak, Tongmuling, Xiangzhou, Bijia Mountain and Xiankou. It was listed as a national key scenic area in 1982 and designated a national patriotic education base and a national garden city in 1994. The city flower is the rhododendron.

How the day runs: This is a full day and the drive is half of it. Leave Nanchang at 06:30 for the 140-kilometre run and arrive at the Ciping base around 09:00. The Huangyangjie section, with the narrow pass road and the viewpoint platforms, is the classic first stop and takes ninety minutes including the transfer road. Continue to the main peak area and the Longtan caves, both of which sit higher and cooler than the valley, and allow two hours there before the run back. The return to Nanchang takes two and a half hours, so leave by 15:00 and arrive by 17:30 rather than planning an evening in the city.

140 km from Nanchang · about 150 min by car from Nanchang · full-day trip · best in April to October, with the rhododendron flowering in late April and May.

  • The mountain average elevation is 381.5 metres and the summit sections reach 1,841 metres, so temperatures on the upper sections sit below those in the valley.
  • The scenic area is 213.5 square kilometres across eight sections, and the internal road network takes longer to use than the map suggests.
  • April and May carry the rhododendron flowering, and both are also the months with the highest visitor numbers on the winding access roads.

Lushan Mountain (庐山)

Lushan rises on the southern bank of the Yangtze in the southern outskirts of Jiujiang, 150 kilometres from Nanchang, and is a block fault mountain formed during the fourth glacial period. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site, a UNESCO Global Geopark and a national 5A rated scenic area. The highest peak, Hanyang Peak, reaches 1,474 metres, and the summer temperature on the mountain runs about 10 degrees below the plain below, which is why it became one of the country's best-known summer resorts. It is also a cultural site of the first rank: poems and fu in praise of Lushan number more than 4,000, and the West Sea village of Guling grew from building work begun in 1895.

How the day runs: Leave Nanchang at 06:45 and be at the mountain foot by 09:00, then take the road or the cableway up to the Guling plateau. The town is the collection point for the western cliff path, the Sandiequan spring and the Hanyang Peak section, and a full circuit on the plateau takes four hours. The lower alternative is the east slope, where the Donglin and Longshuiyu waterfalls are reached by a separate road and the walking is shorter. Choose one side rather than trying to cross the mountain between them. Descend by 15:00 and be back in Nanchang at 17:30, allowing for the road down through the cloud layer, which is often the slowest part of the day.

150 km from Nanchang · about 150 min by car from Nanchang · full-day trip · best in May to October; the mountain averages about 10 degrees cooler than the plain below.

  • The mountain is a block fault structure with an uplifted plateau, so the drive from the foot to Guling is long even though the horizontal distance is short.
  • Hanyang Peak at 1,474 metres is the summit; the plateau settlement sits far below it, which is why the summit walk is worth doing in the morning.
  • More than 4,000 poems and fu have been written about the mountain, and the cliff inscriptions along the western path are collected in sequence as you walk.

Meiling National Forest Park (梅岭国家森林公园)

Meiling lies in the north-west of the Xinjian district, on the higher ground above Nanchang, and was declared a national forest park by the Ministry of Forestry in May 1993, three months after the application was filed in March of that year. The mountain was originally called Feihong Mountain, and the present name dates from the early Han dynasty, when the county magistrate Mei Fu withdrew from public life there and was later honoured with a Mei Xian altar on the ridge and a Mei Xian temple below it. The forest park holds more than 600 plant species and over 2,000 kinds in total. Its named features include the Hongya Cliff Well, the Eighth Spring under Heaven and Xiyao Lake.

How the day runs: A short run of 25 kilometres north-west of the city, which makes it the easiest of the mountain outings to combine with a half-day in the city itself. Leave at 08:30 and reach the park road at 09:10. The Hongya Cliff Well section is reached by a short walk from the car park, and the Xiyao Lake area sits higher along the ridge road. Allow two hours for the ridge and the historic features, then return to Nanchang by 12:30. The park is large enough to absorb a wet afternoon if the weather closes in, and it pairs well with the pavilion or the memorial in the city.

25 km from Nanchang · about 40 min by car from Nanchang · half-day trip · best in March to November, when the ridge roads are open and the flowering season runs from March.

  • The park was formally named in May 1993 after an application in March of the same year, and much of the site infrastructure dates from the mid-1980s development period.
  • The ridge road climbs quickly from the city, so the temperature drop is noticeable within twenty minutes of leaving the plain.
  • Hongya Cliff Well, the Eighth Spring under Heaven and Xiyao Lake are the three named features worth locating before setting out.

Junshan Lake (军山湖)

Junshan Lake lies in the northern part of Jinxian county, 50 kilometres north of Nanchang, and measures 25 kilometres from north to south and 5 kilometres from east to west, widening to 16 kilometres at its widest point, with a water surface of 320,000 mu. It was formerly an arm of Poyang Lake and became an independent body only after the Junshan Lake embankment was built in 1958. The name refers to the campaign fought there between Zhu Yuanzhang and Chen Youliang in the late Yuan period. The lake is now known for its crab production, and Jinxian was designated a pollution-free aquaculture base and given the title of China's crab village by the Chinese Fisheries Association crab branch.

How the day runs: The lake is reached in about an hour, which makes it usable as a full-day outing including travel. Leave Nanchang at 08:00 and be at the lakeside town by 09:00. The embankment runs around the water and most of the day is spent on the causeway, the fishery area and the wetland margins at the southern end, with the harvest season concentrated on the eastern shore. Take lunch in the county town on the return rather than on the causeway, which is thin on facilities. Leave by 15:30 and be back in Nanchang by 17:00, which is comfortably inside a working day.

50 km from Nanchang · about 60 min by car from Nanchang · full-day trip · best in September to December, which is the crab harvest season; the lake itself is usable from March to November.

  • The lake was part of Poyang Lake until the embankment of 1958, so the shoreline paths are on reclaimed ground rather than natural banks.
  • The crab harvest is the reason for the September to December window, and the fishery areas are reached on the eastern side of the water.
  • The causeway is long and the distances between points of interest are measured in kilometres, so a bicycle or a long walk is easier than short hops by car.

Planning day trips from Nanchang

The distances here are shorter than they look on a map, and that changes how the days should be planned. Jinggangshan is 140 kilometres away and Lushan 150, both around two and a half hours of expressway, but their internal networks are large: Jinggangshan's scenic area covers 213.5 square kilometres across eight sections, and Lushan is a block mountain with the road from the foot to the plateau taking as long as the walk at the top. Fix the departure time first, then choose the section that fits the hours rather than attempting the whole area.

The other point is altitude. The plain around Nanchang sits low and hot in July and August, while Meiling rises immediately above the city, Jinggangshan averages 381.5 metres across the whole area and reaches 2,120 metres at its highest peak, and Lushan at 1,474 metres runs about 10 degrees cooler than the plain. A single extra layer covers most of the year, and in midsummer it is the difference between a workable day and a difficult one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which day trip is the essential one from Nanchang?

Tengwang Pavilion, which is 6 kilometres from the centre and takes 20 minutes by car, and which carries more history than its position suggests. It was founded in 653, repaired 28 times across the Tang, Song, Yuan, Ming and Qing, and rebuilt on the same site in 1989 from a design based on Liang Sicheng's sketches, with nine main storeys in a Song dynasty structural idiom. Jinggangshan at 140 kilometres is the deeper journey, but it needs a full day and 150 minutes of driving each way.

How much of the August 1st Uprising memorial can be seen in one visit?

The core is the former headquarters, which was the Jiangxi Grand Hotel, built in 1923 with 96 rooms across five storeys, 26 metres of frontage and about 4,800 square metres of floor space. The wider site includes the He Long, Ye Ting and Zhu De command posts, the Zhu De officers school and the Zhu De former residence, all listed among the first batch of national key cultural relics in 1961. The museum was founded in 1956, opened in October 1959, and has been free since January 2008, so two hours is enough for the headquarters and another hour for the surrounding sites.

Is Lushan a realistic day trip or better as an overnight stay?

A day trip works if the aim is the plateau. The mountain is 150 kilometres from Nanchang, the drive is about two and a half hours, and the road from the foot to the Guling plateau is long because the massif is a raised block rather than a single peak. Give the plateau four hours for the western cliff path and the Hanyang Peak section, which reaches 1,474 metres, and leave by 15:00 to beat the descent through the cloud layer. A second day is needed if both the eastern waterfall road and the plateau are on the list.

What can be done close to the city in half a day?

Three options sit within 25 kilometres. Tengwang Pavilion is 6 kilometres out, with nine storeys and a river frontage. The uprising memorial is 4 kilometres away in the Xihu district, with a 1923 hotel building and a group of command post sites around it. Meiling National Forest Park is 25 kilometres north-west, a national forest park named by the Ministry of Forestry in May 1993, with more than 600 plant species and the Hongya Cliff Well and Xiyao Lake among its features. Two of the three make a comfortable half-day with a car.

Which trip suits a family with children rather than a serious walker?

Junshan Lake and Meiling. The lake is 50 kilometres north with an hour of driving, a 320,000 mu water surface, causeway paths around the embankment and fishery areas that work well for a slow day out. Meiling is 25 kilometres out with short walks to the Hongya Cliff Well and the Xiyao Lake area. The pavilion and the museum work as well, being building-based, while Jinggangshan and Lushan both involve real climbing and long internal road networks.

What is the best season for these trips?

April to October for the mountains, since April and May carry the rhododendron flowering at Jinggangshan and the temperature difference of about 10 degrees makes Lushan comfortable in summer. Meiling runs from March to November. The city sites, the pavilion and the uprising memorial, work all year, and their quietest period is from November to February. Junshan Lake's harvest season runs from September to December, and the lake itself is usable from March to November.
