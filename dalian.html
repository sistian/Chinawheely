About Dalian

Name Origin

Dalian (大连) was named in 1899 during the Russian lease of the Liaodong Peninsula. The name means 'Great Link' and reflects the city's strategic location on the Yellow Sea. It was also known as Dairen during the Japanese period.

Education & Universities

Dalian is a significant education center in northeast China. Dalian University of Technology is one of China's top engineering universities, and Dalian Maritime University is the country's leading maritime university.

Metro & Transportation

Dalian Metro has 5 lines covering the city and connecting to the airport and railway stations. The hilly coastal roads and scenic Binhai Road make a private car the best way to enjoy the views and reach distant beaches.

Natural Resources & Environment

Dalian is located on the southern tip of the Liaodong Peninsula, surrounded by the Yellow Sea and Bohai Sea. The city is known for its clean environment, mild coastal climate, green hills, and numerous beaches and bays.

Intangible Cultural Heritage & Living Crafts of Dalian

Meet the traditional arts that have shaped Dalian's identity for centuries.

🎭 Dalian Yangge Folk Dance

A lively northeastern folk dance performed during festivals with drums and colorful costumes.

Where to find it: Spring festivals and public celebrations in parks and squares.

🎨 Lüshun Paper Cutting

A traditional folk art in the Lüshun area with intricate designs depicting local life and naval history.

Where to find it: Lüshunkou folk art shops and Dalian cultural markets.

🧵 Dalian Shell Carving

Artisans carve intricate designs into seashells to create decorative items and jewelry.

Where to find it: Souvenir shops along the Binhai Road and Xinghai Square.

🎵 Northeastern Errenzhuan

A comedic song-and-dance performance style popular in northeast China.

Where to find it: Local theaters and cultural performances in Dalian.

🏺 Dalian Glass Art

Dalian has a tradition of glassblowing and glass sculpture, producing decorative items and art pieces.

Where to find it: Art galleries and craft shops in the city center.

🪁 Seaside Kite Making

Traditional kite making with coastal themes like seagulls, ships, and fish.

Where to find it: Beach festivals and spring kite events.

6 Things to Do in Dalian

Make the most of your visit to Dalian with these handpicked experiences — all easily accessible with a private car and English-speaking driver.

🌊

Walk or Bike Along Xinghai Bay's Coastal Road

One of China's most scenic coastal drives, with views of Xinghai Square, the International Conference Centre, and the open sea. Rent a bike to explore the full 20km coastal path.

🏖️

Relax at Fujiazhai Beach

Dalian's most popular beach, with golden sand, clear water, and surrounding hills covered in pine forest. A private car from the city centre makes the 40-minute drive comfortable, with your driver waiting.

🏯

Ride the Dalian Metro to Heishijiao (Black Rock Reef)

Take the scenic metro line to the coast at Heishijiao, where you can walk the rocky shoreline, explore the fishermen's harbour, and eat fresh seafood at the waterfront restaurants overlooking the Yellow Sea.

🌸

Stroll through Dalian's Russian and Japanese Concessions

Dalian's colonial heritage is remarkably well-preserved. The former Russian and Japanese districts feature European-style villas, tree-lined streets, and the historic Dalian Railway Station building.

🦌

Visit the Dalian Forest Zoo

One of China's best zoos, set on a hillside with natural habitats for both local and exotic species. The polar bear exhibit and the open-air aviary are particularly popular with visitors.

🍜

Eat Fresh Seafood at Dalian's Fisherman's Wharf

The renovated Fisherman's Wharf area combines historic fishing village atmosphere with excellent seafood restaurants. Try fresh crab, scallops, and the famous Dalian sea cucumber dish.

Best season to travel in Dalian

🌸 Best Season May – June / September – October — May–June brings comfortable temperatures and blooming azaleas across the city's hillsides. September–October offers warm seas and spectacular autumn foliage along Xinghai Bay.

🌸
Spring April – June 10°C–24°C 🌸 Best

The best time to visit. Azaleas bloom across Dalian's hillsides in April–May. Comfortable temperatures for walking the Xinghai Bay coastal path.

☀️
Summer July – August 22°C–30°C OK

Warm and pleasant with the best beach weather at Fujiazhai and Diamond Cove. The coastal path is perfect for evening walks. Peak season for water sports.

🍂
Autumn September – October 10°C–24°C 🍂 Best

The second-best season. The coastal road is spectacular in autumn light, the Russian Concession is at its most atmospheric, and seafood is at its freshest.

❄️
Winter November – March -5°C–5°C OK

Cold with strong sea winds. The coastal path is dramatic but bracing. Indoor attractions like the Dalian Forest Zoo are warm.

🏆 Major Sports Events in Dalian

Dalian is a legendary football city in northeast China -- home to Dalian Pro FC and one of China's newest world-class stadiums. Dalian has produced many of China's greatest footballers.

🏃

Dalian Marathon (大连马拉松)

2026: May (annual, World Athletics Gold Label)

The Dalian Marathon (大连马拉松) is a World Athletics Gold Label event. Course passes the Dalian coastline, Xinghai Square (星海广场), and the modern Jinzhou District. 30,000 runners. One of China's oldest marathons -- first held in 1987.

Venue: Start: Xinghai Square (星海广场); Finish: Jinzhou District. Metro: Xinghai Square (Line 1).

⚽

Chinese Super League Football (中超联赛 – 大连英博)

2026 season: March to November

Dalian Pro (大连英博) competes in the CSL at the Dalian Youth Stadium (大连梭鱼湾足球场) -- one of China's newest stadiums with 60,000 capacity. Dalian's football tradition runs deep -- the city has produced many of China's greatest footballers.

Venue: Dalian Youth Stadium (大连梭鱼湾足球场), Ganjingzi District. Metro: Dalianwan (Line 4).

🏀

Dalian CBA Basketball (大连鲲城)

2026-2027 season: October 2026 to May 2027

Dalian's CBA team (大连鲲城/大连华谊) plays at Dalian's modern sports venues. The 2026-2027 season marks an exciting new chapter for basketball in this football-mad city.

Venue: Dalian Sports Centre or Dalian Bay Sports Centre.

⚽

Dalian Derby – vs Qingdao (连青德比)

2026 season: select games

The Lian-Qing Derby (连青德比) between Dalian and Qingdao clubs is one of northern China's most passionate football rivalries. Both cities are historic football powerhouses with deep fan cultures.

Venue: Dalian Youth Stadium or away at Qingdao Youth Football Stadium.
