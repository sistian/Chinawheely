Name Origin

Chongzuo (Chinese: 崇左; pinyin: Chóngzuǒ; Zhuang: Cungzcoj) is a prefecture-level city in the south of Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region near the Sino-Vietnamese border.

Education & Universities

Higher education in Chongzuo is provided by the city's local colleges, vocational schools and teacher-training institutions rather than by large research universities.

Getting Around

Getting around Chongzuo is mostly by road, with buses and taxis in the urban area and long-distance coaches to the surrounding counties.

Natural Resources & Environment

Chongzuo sits in its provincial hinterland, where the surrounding hills, farmland and rivers shape the local landscape, weather and food.

Chongzuo Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Chongzuo like a local

Detian Waterfall is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Distances inside Chongzuo look short on a map but stretch out in traffic, so build in buffer time between sights.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Chongzuo

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Chongzuo

🍵 Tea Houses of Chongzuo

Tea culture runs through the day in Chongzuo: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
