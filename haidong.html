Haidong (Chinese: 海东市; pinyin: Hǎidōng shì) is a prefecture-level city of Qinghai province in Western China.

Education & Universities

Higher education in Haidong is provided by the city's local colleges, vocational schools and teacher-training institutions rather than by large research universities.

Getting Around

Getting around Haidong is mostly by road, with buses and taxis in the urban area and long-distance coaches to the surrounding counties.

Natural Resources & Environment

Its name literally means "east of the (Qinghai) Lake." On 8 February 2013 Haidong was upgraded from a prefecture (海东地区) into a prefecture-level city.

Haidong Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Haidong like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

太子坡草原 is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Distances inside Haidong look short on a map but stretch out in traffic, so build in buffer time between sights.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Haidong

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Haidong

🍵 Tea Houses of Haidong

Tea culture runs through the day in Haidong: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
