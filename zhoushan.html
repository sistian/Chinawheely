Food & Shopping in Zhoushan

🍽️ Must-Try Zhoushan Cuisine

Zhoushan Roast Duck (舟山)

The city's most famous dish. Crispy skin, tender meat, wrapped in thin pancakes with sweet bean sauce. Quanjude and Da Dong are the most renowned restaurants. Your driver can recommend the best duck restaurant based on your location and budget.

Zhajiangmian (Noodles with Soybean Paste)

A beloved Zhoushan street food. Thick wheat noodles topped with a savory fermented soybean paste, cucumber, and bean sprouts. Try it at a local hutong eatery for an authentic experience.

Jianbing (Zhoushan Crepes)

The quintessential Zhoushan breakfast — a crispy egg crepe with scallions, cilantro, and a crunchy fried cracker. Sold at street carts across the city. Ask your driver for the best morning spot near your hotel.

Hotpot (Shuan Yang Rou)

Mongolian-style hotpot with thinly sliced lamb, fresh vegetables, and sesame dipping sauce. Zhoushan's winters are perfect for this communal dining experience. Ask your driver for a local favorite away from tourist traps.

Wangfujing Street

Zhoushan's most famous shopping street, featuring international brands, traditional Chinese crafts, and the famous Wangfujing Snack Street. Your driver can drop you at the pedestrian entrance and arrange a pickup time.

Sanlitun (Taikoo Li)

The trendy heart of Zhoushan's nightlife and fashion. Open-air malls with international designer brands, craft breweries, and rooftop bars. Perfect for an evening shopping trip with a private car waiting to take you home safely.

Pearl Market (Hongqiao Market)

A multi-story market famous for pearls, silk, electronics, and souvenirs. Bargaining is expected. Your driver can help you negotiate or recommend trustworthy vendors and ensure you don't get overcharged.

Panjiayuan Antique Market

The largest antique market in China, open on weekends. Hundreds of stalls selling ceramics, jade, calligraphy, and Mao memorabilia. A fascinating cultural experience, best visited early morning with a private car for the 30-minute drive from downtown.

Intangible Cultural Heritage & Living Crafts of Zhoushan

Meet the traditional arts that have defined Zhoushan for centuries — and the artisans keeping them alive.

🎭 舟山 (Jingju)

UNESCO-listed intangible heritage. With roots in the 18th century, 舟山 combines singing, mime, dance, acrobatics, and stylized combat. The elaborate face painting and costumes make every performance a visual feast.

Where to experience it: Liyuan Theatre, Zhengyici Theatre, or Huguang Guild Hall. Ask your driver to book tickets in advance.

🏺 Cloisonné Enamel (Jingtailan)

A Zhoushan specialty since the Yuan dynasty, cloisonné involves soldering copper wires onto a metal body, filling the compartments with enamel, and firing multiple times. The result is luminous, jewel-like tableware and art pieces.

Living master: Master Zhang Tonglu, a national-level inheritor, has devoted over 60 years to cloisonné design. His studio creates museum-quality pieces that preserve the imperial tradition while introducing contemporary forms.

✂️ Zhoushan Paper-Cutting (Jianzhi)

Red paper-cuttings of flowers, animals, and auspicious symbols are pasted on windows during Spring Festival. Zhoushan's style is bold and symmetrical, often featuring the city's landmark architecture alongside traditional motifs.

Where to find it: workshops in hutongs, Panjiayuan Market, and the Zhoushan Folk Art Museum.

🪁 Kite Making (Shayan Kite)

Traditional Zhoushan kites are framed with bamboo and painted with birds, butterflies, or characters from folklore. The Shayan kite tradition, passed down through generations of craftsmen, is known for its perfect balance and elegant designs.

Where to find it: Look for kite craftsmen at the Temple Fair during Spring Festival, or visit traditional craft shops near Shichahai.

🎨 Inside-Bottle Painting

An astonishing art form where artists paint miniature landscapes, figures, and animals on the inside surface of tiny glass bottles using a bent brush. It requires years of steady hands and deep concentration.

Where to find it: Liulichang Cultural Street and the Zhoushan Enamel Factory have shops and demonstrations.

🎤 Storytelling & Acrobatics (Pingshu & Acrobatics)

Traditional Zhoushan storytelling (pingshu) and acrobatics have entertained locals in teahouses for centuries. Acrobatics troupes perform breathtaking feats of balance, juggling, and contortion nightly.

Where to see it: Chaoyang Theatre Acrobatics Show, Laoshe Teahouse, or Tianqiao Acrobatics Theatre.

Zhoushan Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Zhoushan like a local.

Beat the Crowds at the 舟山

Enter through the Gate of Divine Prowess (Shenwu Men) at opening time and walk the opposite direction of the tour groups. The central axis is busiest, but the side halls are quieter and equally beautiful.

🍜

Eat Breakfast Like a Zhoushaner

Start with a jianbing from a street cart, then try douzhi (fermented mung bean milk) and jiaoquan (crispy fried dough rings) at a traditional hutong shop. The flavor is polarizing — but it's the real Zhoushan.

🚲

Explore the Hutongs by Bike or on Foot

Skip the crowded Nanluoguxiang and explore Beixinqiao, Guozijian, or Wudaoying Hutong instead. These alleys still have family courtyards, independent cafés, and old Zhoushan charm.
