 <div class="card" id="overview">
  <h2 class="section-title">1. Executive Summary</h2>
  <div class="highlight-box">
   <p style="font-size:.95rem;line-height:1.8;">
    <strong>Objective:</strong> Analyze competitor keyword gap data from 16 SEMrush Excel files and inject relevant keywords into Chinawheely's 75-page website to improve organic search visibility.
   </p>
   <p style="font-size:.95rem;margin-top:.75rem;line-height:1.8;">
    <strong>Result:</strong> <span class="success">75 pages optimized</span> &mdash; every HTML page on the site received keyword enhancements including title tags, meta descriptions, hero paragraph text, and a dedicated "Popular Keywords" section showing which competitor keywords the page targets.
   </p>
   <p style="font-size:.95rem;margin-top:.75rem;line-height:1.8;">
    <strong>Data Source:</strong> 16 SEMrush gap analysis Excel files covering Ctrip, ChinaHighlights, ChinaDiscovery, TravelChinaGuide, Yangtze River Cruises, Easy Tour China, Access China Travel, CITS, CTS, ZhangjiajieTravel, and 4 WuhanVideo niche sites.
   </p>
  </div>
  <div class="warning-box">
   <p style="font-size:.9rem;margin-bottom:.5rem"><strong>Key Finding:</strong> Chinawheely's primary competitors are tour operators (ChinaHighlights, TravelChinaGuide) rather than pure car services. Their keyword sets focus heavily on <em>tour packages</em> and <em>attractions</em>. Chinawheely's airport transfer and car service keywords overlap with only <strong>3.8%</strong> of competitor terms &mdash; a significant opportunity to differentiate on pure service-related search intent.</p>
  </div>
 </div>

     <li>ZhangjiajieTravel &mdash; 64 keywords</li>
    </ul>
   </div>
   <div>
    <h3 style="color:var(--p);margin-bottom:.6rem;font-size:.95rem">Keyword Processing Pipeline</h3>
    <ul>
     <li><strong>Step 1:</strong> Extract all keywords from 16 Excel gap files</li>
     <li><strong>Step 2:</strong> Deduplicate and clean (1,377 unique terms)</li>
     <li><strong>Step 3:</strong> Filter for relevance to airport/car service business (282 terms)</li>
     <li><strong>Step 4:</strong> Score by city-relevance and business relevance</li>
     <li><strong>Step 5:</strong> Inject into title, meta description, hero paragraph, keyword section</li>
     <li><strong>Step 6:</strong> Generate page-specific keyword sections</li>
    </ul>
   </div>
  </div>
  <div style="margin-top:1rem">
   <h3 style="color:var(--p);margin-bottom:.6rem;font-size:.95rem">Relevance Scoring Algorithm</h3>
   <p style="font-size:.87rem;color:var(--m);line-height:1.7;">
    Each keyword was scored on: (a) presence of airport/transfer terms (+3), (b) city name match (+5), (c) city name at start of keyword (+2), (d) negative penalty for junk terms like zodiac, hotels, flights (-5 to -10). Keywords with score &gt; 0 were selected for each page.
   </p>
