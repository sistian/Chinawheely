Seasons Overview

The climate record of Shenzhen.

The annual average temperature is 23 °C; annual rainfall averages 1,886 mm; the climate pattern includes typhoons, the monsoon, cold waves, the flood season, frost.

☀️

Summer

The warmest month is July, when the average high sits near 29 °C. Expect long days, heavier traffic and the busiest sightseeing hours.

❄️

Winter

The coldest month is January, averaging 15.7 °C. It is the quietest season for sightseeing and the easiest time to drive between sights.

🌸

Spring and autumn

Across the year the average temperature is 23 °C, with annual rainfall of 1,886 mm — the shoulder seasons are the most comfortable for walking between landmarks.

🌦️

What to expect

The climate pattern includes typhoons, the monsoon, cold waves, the flood season. Weather changes with the seasons, so a private car with a local driver is the practical way to keep a plan on track.
