Seasons Overview

The climate record of Chengdu, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 16.3 °C; annual rainfall averages 856 mm; July is the hottest month at 25.4 °C; January the coldest at 5.6 °C; the climate pattern includes The monsoon, maritime influence, highland conditions.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 11.5–21.3 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 147 mm.

Highlights: Wuhou Shrine, Jinli Street, Du Fu Thatched Cottage

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 23.8–25.4 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 520 mm.

Highlights: Wuhou Shrine, Jinli Street, Du Fu Thatched Cottage

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 12.4–21.6 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 161 mm.

Highlights: Wuhou Shrine, Jinli Street, Du Fu Thatched Cottage

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run 5.6–7.9 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 28 mm.

Highlights: Wuhou Shrine, Jinli Street, Du Fu Thatched Cottage
