Name Origin of Jingzhou

Jingzhou means "Jing prefecture" in Chinese. It was a strategic city in ancient Chu state and Three Kingdoms history on the middle Yangtze River.

Jingzhou Education & Universities

Yangtze University and Jingzhou Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around Jingzhou

Jingzhou Shashi Airport (SHS) has domestic flights. Jingzhou Railway Station connects to Wuhan and Yichang on the Wuhan–Yichang railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Jingzhou Natural Resources & Environment

Jingzhou lies on the Jianghan Plain along the Yangtze River with humid subtropical climate, lakes, and fertile farmland.

Jingzhou Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Jingzhou like a local

The main sight of Jingzhou rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Jingzhou

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Jingzhou

🍵 Tea Houses of Jingzhou

Tea culture runs through the day in Jingzhou: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
