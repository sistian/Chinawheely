Name Origin

Jixi means "west of Jilin" in Chinese, named for its location west of the Jilin area. It is a coal and grain city in southeastern Heilongjiang.

Education & Universities

Jixi University and Heilongjiang Industrial College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Jixi Xingkaihu Airport (JXA) has domestic flights. Jixi Railway Station connects to Mudanjiang and Harbin. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Jixi lies on the Muling River with a humid continental climate. It has coal reserves, farmland, and is near the Ussuri River and Russian border.

Jixi Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Jixi like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

shared with Russia is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

The annual average temperature is 4.5 °C; annual rainfall averages 542 mm; July is the hottest month at 21.7 °C; January the coldest at -16 °C; The climate pattern includes continental influence, the monsoon.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Jixi

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Jixi

🍵 Tea Houses of Jixi

Tea culture runs through the day in Jixi: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
