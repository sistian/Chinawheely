The Rich History of Tea in Hangzhou

Hangzhou has been a center of China's tea culture for centuries. As a major trading hub, the city absorbed tea traditions from every province, creating a unique local tea culture that blends influences from across China. Today, these traditions continue, offering visitors an authentic window into Chinese daily life.

Tea Drinking Etiquette in Hangzhou

Always pour tea for others before filling your own cup. Tap two fingers on the table to thank the person pouring. Never let an empty cup sit — refill when low. Accept snacks offered — refusing is considered impolite. Take time — tea houses are for lingering, not rushing.
