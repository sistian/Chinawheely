Name Origin

Leiyang (simplified Chinese: 耒阳; traditional Chinese: 耒陽; pinyin: Lěiyáng) is a county-level city and the third most populous county-level division in Hunan Province, China.

Education & Universities

Higher education in Leiyang is provided by the city's local colleges, vocational schools and teacher-training institutions rather than by large research universities.

Getting Around

Getting around Leiyang is mostly by road, with buses and taxis in the urban area and long-distance coaches to the surrounding counties. Leiyang West Railway Station is the main rail gateway.

Natural Resources & Environment

Located on the south of the province, the city is bordered to the north by Hengnan County, to the west by Changning City, to the south by Guiyang and Yongxing counties, and to the east by Anren County.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Leiyang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Leiyang

🍵 Tea Houses of Leiyang

Tea culture runs through the day in Leiyang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
