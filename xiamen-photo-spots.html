Photography Tips for Best Results

Arrive 30 minutes before sunrise or sunset for setup and to capture the full color show.

Use a tripod for sharp images during low-light conditions.

Consider using a polarizing filter to enhance sky colors and reduce glare.

Explore different angles and compositions rather than shooting from typical positions.

Photography Tips for Xiamen

Maximize your photography experience in Xiamen with these practical tips from experienced photographers who know the best times and techniques for capturing the city.

Essential Photography Tips

Visit major attractions early morning to avoid crowds and get clean shots.

Respect local customs when photographing people, always ask permission.

Check weather forecasts for optimal conditions, especially for rooftop shots.

Consider hiring a local guide who knows the best photo spots and timing.
