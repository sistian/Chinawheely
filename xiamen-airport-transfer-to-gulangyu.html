Gulangyu is a 1.88-square-kilometre island administratively part of Xiamen's Siming District, with a recorded population of 12,509 in 2023 and more than 10 million visitors a year, making it one of China's most-visited attractions. Inscribed as a UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site, it is car-free and bicycle-free, reachable only by ferry across the 600-metre Lujiang Strait. From Gaoqi Airport the road leg runs to the Dongdu cruise terminal on Xiamen Island's west shore, where the crossing to Sanqiutian Pier begins.

Gaoqi occupies the north end of Xiamen Island itself, so the run south to the ferry piers stays on the island's road grid — Success Avenue and the Huandong coastal route — rather than crossing to the mainland. The useful drop point for visitors is the Dongdu International Cruise Center terminal on the island's west side, from which the passenger ferries to Gulangyu leave. Cars cannot cross to Gulangyu; the transfer ends at the pier.

Why transfer to Gulangyu?

The island is the physical record of a treaty-port century. After the Treaty of Nanking opened Xiamen in 1842, foreign residents settled Gulangyu, and in May 1902 the Qing government approved Land Regulations creating an international settlement; eventually 13 countries, among them Britain, France, the Netherlands and Japan, held extraterritorial privileges under the Kulangsu Municipal Council. The consular residences and merchant villas of that period still line the lanes, and this ensemble is what UNESCO inscribed.

Gulangyu is also one of the rare traffic-free zones in China: no cars and no bicycles are permitted, freight moves on wheeled wooden carts, and electric tourist buggies are the only motorised concession. The island's pianos and organs give it the Piano Island name, and the museum collection in the former consular stock of buildings records the instrument trade. A day on the island therefore involves walking its lanes at first hand, which is the point of the crossing.

What to do on Gulangyu once you arrive

Ferries from the Dongdu cruise terminal land at Sanqiutian Pier on the island's north shore, with a second route serving Neicuo'ao Pier further west; both run under the Xiamen Ferry Company. From Sanqiutian the lanes climb south past the consular-era villas toward Sunlight Rock, the island's high point, and the Shuzhuang Garden with its piano museum on the southern shore. Walking the full loop takes about half a day unhurried.

Set a return ferry before the last departure, since sailings from the cruise terminal finish at 17:30, extended to 18:30 in the June-to-September season, and the Neicuo'ao route ends earlier at 17:10. Evening sailings revert to the resident-only route from Lundu ferry pier near Zhongshan Road at 8 yuan, which visitors cannot use in resident periods. The island's buggies serve fixed circuits if the lanes prove long.

  • Buy the tourist ferry ticket for the cruise-center-to-Sanqiutian route; round trips are 35 to 80 yuan by seat class
  • Sailing hours: 07:10 to 17:30 from Dongdu, or to 18:30 from June to September
  • The 8 yuan ferry from Lundu pier is reserved for Xiamen residents during daytime resident periods
  • No vehicles cross, so pack light for the lanes, which are steep in the southern half

Arriving at Xiamen Gaoqi International Airport

Gaoqi sits on the main island roughly 10 kilometres northeast of the Dongdu cruise terminal, and because both points stand on Xiamen Island the transfer avoids any bridge or tunnel toll plaza. Terminal 3 handles the bulk of international and regional flights while Terminal 4 serves domestic services; each has its own arrivals kerb, so confirm which terminal your flight uses before setting the meeting point.

Allow 20 minutes from touchdown to the kerb for bags on a domestic arrival, longer when the morning banks land together. A booked driver holding a name sign at the arrivals exit tracks the flight number, which matters here because the last Sanqiutian sailing leaves at 17:30 outside summer; landing after mid-afternoon, budget the road leg and ticket queue into your ferry timing or plan the island stay for the following morning.

What the transfer costs

A metered taxi from Gaoqi to the Dongdu cruise center prices around 30 to 45 yuan on the urban tariff for the roughly 10-kilometre run. A pre-booked sedan with Chinawheely quotes in the 100 to 150 yuan band including airport waiting and the toll-free island drive, with seven-seat classes above that for groups carrying luggage for an overnight stay on Gulangyu.

Add the ferry tariff to your budget: the visitor routes from the cruise center cost 35 to 80 yuan round trip by deck class, and the resident route from Lundu pier is 8 yuan single. The ferry ticket includes the return sailing within the day for the standard class; buggy circuits on the island are priced separately at the pier exits.

Ask the driver for the Dongdu International Cruise Center, gate of the ferry hall, not the old Lundu ferry pier across town, since visitors cannot board the resident route there in daytime. If you are staying overnight on the island, book a late ferry class explicitly, as seat classes sell out before the final sailings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get from Xiamen airport to Gulangyu?

Two legs. First a road transfer of roughly 10 kilometres from Gaoqi to the Dongdu International Cruise Center on the island's west shore, then the Xiamen Ferry Company crossing to Sanqiutian Pier on Gulangyu. The ferry costs 35 to 80 yuan by class and cars cannot be taken across, so the transfer ends at the cruise terminal's ferry hall.

Which ferry should visitors take to Gulangyu?

Visitors use the Dongdu cruise center routes: to Sanqiutian Pier from 07:10 to 17:30, extended to 18:30 in June-September, or to Neicuo'ao Pier from 08:10 to 17:10. The old Lundu ferry pier route at 8 yuan is restricted to Xiamen residents during daytime hours, so do not plan on boarding there as a visitor.

Why are there no cars on Gulangyu?

The island operates as a traffic-free zone: cars and bicycles are both prohibited, freight is hauled on wheeled wooden carts, and electric tourist buggies on fixed circuits are the only motorised transport. The rule preserves the lane scale of the 1902 international settlement, whose consular villas form the UNESCO-inscribed core that visitors walk.

How much time does a Gulangyu visit need?

Half a day covers Sanqiutian Pier, the consular lanes, Sunlight Rock and Shuzhuang Garden with the piano museum; a full day allows the southern headland and the quieter Neicuo'ao side. Check the last sailing back — 17:30 from the cruise center, 18:30 in summer — or book accommodation on the island for the evening hours.

Can I take luggage to Gulangyu?

Yes, on foot. There are no vehicle transfers on the island, so you carry or cart your bags from Sanqiutian Pier up the lanes, which climb steeply in the southern half. Hotels on the island arrange baggage porters on request; otherwise pack light for the crossing and store large cases at your Xiamen hotel.

What did the airport-to-pier transfer cost in total?

Count the road leg plus the ferry. A metered taxi runs roughly 30 to 45 yuan from Gaoqi to the Dongdu cruise center; a fixed-quote sedan transfer runs higher but includes arrivals waiting. The visitor ferry adds 35 to 80 yuan round trip, and island buggy circuits price separately at the pier.
