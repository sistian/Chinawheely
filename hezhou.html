Name Origin of Hezhou

Hezhou (simplified Chinese: 贺州; traditional Chinese: 賀州) is a prefecture-level city in the northeast of the Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, People's Republic of China.

Hezhou Education & Universities

Higher education in Hezhou is provided by the city's local colleges, vocational schools and teacher-training institutions rather than by large research universities.

Getting Around Hezhou

Getting around Hezhou is mostly by road, with buses and taxis in the urban area and long-distance coaches to the surrounding counties.

Hezhou Natural Resources & Environment

Hezhou sits in its provincial hinterland, where the surrounding hills, farmland and rivers shape the local landscape, weather and food.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Hezhou

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Hezhou

🍵 Tea Houses of Hezhou

Tea culture runs through the day in Hezhou: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
