Name Origin

Yan'an means " Yan'an " or " prolonging peace" in Chinese. It is a holy land of the Chinese Communist Revolution in northern Shaanxi and was the CCP headquarters from 1935 to 1947.

Education & Universities

Yan'an University and Yan'an Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Yan'an Nanniwan Airport (ENY) has domestic flights. Yan'an Railway Station and Yan'an North Station connect to Xi'an and other cities. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Yan'an lies in the Loess Plateau of northern Shaanxi with a continental climate. It has the Yellow River, loess hills, and is famous for red tourism and apple production.

Yan'an Local Secrets

The things a driver who works here every day would point out.

🏛️ The revolutionary sites

Pagoda Hill and the old caves

Yan'an is the place the Chinese Communist revolution was run from between 1935 and 1948. Pagoda Hill over the city, the Yangjialing site and the cave dwellings cut into the loess cliffs are all on the standard visit, and the museums explain what happened here in detail.

🍎 Loess hills and apple orchards

The orchards of northern Shaanxi

South of the city the loess plateau breaks into hills and gullies that are now planted with apple orchards. Yan'an apples are shipped all over China, and the orchards are open to visitors in the autumn.

🍜 What to eat here

Northern Shaanxi cooking

Yan'an yangrou paomo — lamb bread soup — is the local bowl, with buckwheat noodles, millet dishes and the region's own vinegar. Food here is hearty and built for the plateau's cold.

🍂 Autumn and winter

Seasonal highlights

Late September and October are the best months on the plateau: the apple harvest is on, the summit is clear, and the loess hills turn gold. Winter is dry and cold — the wrong season for long walks along the Yan River, but the right one for the museums.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Yan'an

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Yan'an

🍵 Tea Houses of Yan'an

Tea culture runs through the day in Yan'an: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
