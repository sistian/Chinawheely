Seasons Overview

The climate record of Chongqing, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 18.8 °C; annual rainfall averages 1,152 mm; 7月 is the hottest month at 28.9 °C; 1月 the coldest at 8.1 °C; the climate pattern includes 季风, 干旱, 高原.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 14.5–22.6 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 302 mm.

Highlights: 重庆古城墙, 山城啤酒, 湖广填四川

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 25.4–28.9 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 512 mm.

Highlights: 重庆古城墙, 山城啤酒, 湖广填四川

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 14.5–24.4 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 270 mm.

Highlights: 重庆古城墙, 山城啤酒, 湖广填四川

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run 8.1–10.4 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 68 mm.

Highlights: 重庆古城墙, 山城啤酒, 湖广填四川
