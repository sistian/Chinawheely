Tianjin Best Time to Visit | When to Go to Tianjin

Complete seasonal guide for planning your Tianjin trip

Seasons Overview

The climate record of Tianjin, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 13.1 °C; annual rainfall averages 521 mm; 7月 is the hottest month at 27.1 °C; 1月 the coldest at -3.2 °C; the climate pattern includes 季风.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 7.1–21.1 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 62 mm.

Highlights: 京杭大运河, 小白楼, 唐山大地震

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 25.1–27.1 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 354 mm.

Highlights: 京杭大运河, 小白楼, 唐山大地震

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 5.3–21.2 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 96 mm.

Highlights: 京杭大运河, 小白楼, 唐山大地震

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run -3.2–0.1 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 9 mm.

Highlights: 京杭大运河, 小白楼, 唐山大地震
