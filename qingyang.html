Name Origin

Qingyang means "prosperous and sunny" in Chinese. It is located in eastern Gansu on the Loess Plateau and is known as the birthplace of the Chinese agricultural culture and Du Kang wine.

Education & Universities

Qingyang Normal University and Qingyang Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Qingyang Xifeng Airport (IQN) has domestic flights. Qingyang Railway Station connects to Xi'an and Yinchuan. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Qingyang lies on the Loess Plateau with a continental climate. It has the Malian River, loess landforms, and is famous for oil and agriculture.

Qingyang Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Qingyang like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

The main sight of Qingyang rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Qingyang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Qingyang

🍵 Tea Houses of Qingyang

Tea culture runs through the day in Qingyang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
