Name Origin

Heyuan means "source of the river" in Chinese, named because it is the source of the Dong River (Dongjiang), which supplies water to Hong Kong and Shenzhen. It is in northeastern Guangdong.

Education & Universities

Heyuan Polytechnic and Heyuan Vocational and Technical College are the main local institutions.

Getting Around

Heyuan has no commercial airport; the nearest airports are in Guangzhou and Shenzhen. Heyuan Railway Station and Heyuan East Station connect to Guangzhou and Shenzhen on the Guangzhou–Meizhou–Shantou railway. Buses and taxis are available.

Natural Resources & Environment

Heyuan has a subtropical climate with mountains, forests, rivers, and the Xinfengjiang Reservoir (Wanlu Lake), one of Guangdong's largest reservoirs. It is known for good air quality.

Heyuan's Best Photo Spots

Capture the city from the most iconic and photogenic angles.

⛰️ 🌅 Jingshan Park Pavilion

Best at sunrise

Climb to the Wanchun Pavilion for the classic panoramic view over the golden rooftops of the Heyu. On clear days, you can see the CCTV tower and the western mountains in the distance. Arrive early to beat the crowds.

🏯 Heyu Corner Tower

Best at dusk

The northwest corner tower reflected in the moat is one of Heyuan's most photographed scenes. The sunset glow turns the water gold and the tower silhouette dramatic. Your driver can drop you at the northeast or northwest corner for the perfect shot.

🛶 the old quarter old-quarter lanes

Best in late afternoon

The lakeside old-quarter lanes around the lakeside lanes offer a charming mix of old Heyuan life, willow trees, and red lanterns. Take a rickshaw ride or wander the narrow alleys, then capture the golden hour light on the water.

🦅 🏙️ CCTV Headquarters

Best at blue hour

Also known as the "Big Pants," this modern architectural icon in the CBD looks best from the plaza below or from across the Third Ring Road. At night, the building is illuminated and reflected in the surrounding towers.

⛰️ 🧱 Heyu at Xinfengjiang Reservoir

Best in early morning mist

For photographers seeking a dramatic, less-crowded Heyu shot, Xinfengjiang Reservoir is unmatched. The wild, unrestored sections and watchtowers winding over mountain ridges create unforgettable sunrise and fog images.

🏯 🎨 798 Art District

Best on a sunny afternoon

Colorful murals, industrial brick buildings, and quirky sculptures make 798 a favorite for creative portraits. Every corner offers a different backdrop, from Soviet-era slogans to contemporary street art.

Heyuan Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Heyuan like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

Xinfengjiang Reservoir is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Heyuan

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Heyuan

🍵 Tea Houses of Heyuan

Tea culture runs through the day in Heyuan: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
