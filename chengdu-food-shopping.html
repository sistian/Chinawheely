Overview: Food & Shopping in Chengdu

What to eat in Chengdu and where the shopping actually happens.

The 8 dishes below are the ones Chengdu is known for, each of them listed in the city's own encyclopaedia entry. Stopping at a market or a food street is easier with a car and a driver who knows where the stalls are, because the good ones are rarely on the main road.

What to Eat in Chengdu

🍜 hot pot

Local dish

hot pot is on the local menu in Chengdu. Ask for it by name — it is the dish regulars order, and the one worth stopping for while you are in town.

Where to Shop in Chengdu

The specialties of Chengdu are sold from the market and food streets of the old commercial quarters; a driver who works here every day will take you to the stalls the locals use rather than to the tourist-facing shops near the main sights.
