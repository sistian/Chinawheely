Name Origin

The name "Xinyang" (信阳) means "South of the Xin River" (信水之阳). The city is named after the Xin River (浉河), which flows through the city center, and its position south of the river. Historically part of the ancient Chu Kingdom, Xinyang has over 2,300 years of recorded history. It was an important military and cultural center during the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods.

Education & Culture

Xinyang is a regional educational hub with Xinyang Normal University (信阳师范学院), known for its teacher training programs, and Xinyang Agricultural University, which specializes in tea science and agriculture. The city has a strong literary tradition and is known as one of the "Top 10 Literary Cities in Central China." The Xinyang Tea Research Institute is China's leading institution for tea cultivar development.

Transportation

Xinyang is a major stop on the Beijing–Guangzhou Railway and the high-speed Beijing–Guangzhou HSR, making it easily accessible from Beijing (4 hours), Wuhan (45 min), Zhengzhou (50 min), and Guangzhou (5 hours). Xinyang East Station (信阳东站) is the high-speed rail hub. Xinyang does not have its own commercial airport — the nearest major airports are Wuhan Tianhe (WUH) (~200 km) and Zhengzhou Xinzheng (CGO) (~180 km). Our private car service covers all airport pickup routes.

Natural Environment

Xinyang sits in the hilly southern region of Henan Province, an area unusually green and forested for Henan. The Dabie Mountains (大别山) rise to the south, creating a scenic landscape of rolling hills, tea gardens, and reservoirs. Nanwan Lake (南湾湖), the city's crown jewel, is a vast reservoir surrounded by forested islands. Xinyang's climate is more humid and mild than most of Henan, earning it the nickname "Henan's Backyard Garden."

Top Xinyang Attractions

From scenic lake islands to ancient tea terraces, explore Henan's most beautiful prefecture city.

🏞️

🌊 Nanwan Lake (南湾湖)

Henan's Largest Reservoir • 5A Scenic Area

Xinyang's crown jewel — a vast lake with 63 forested islands, surrounded by rolling hills and tea gardens. The water is crystal clear, and the scenery changes dramatically with the seasons. A private car is the most comfortable way to visit from the city center and explore the lakeside trails and tea plantations.

🍃

⛰️ Xinyang Tea Museum & Plantations

Xinyang Maojian Tea • Living Culture

Xinyang Maojian (信阳毛尖) is one of China's Top 10 famous teas, grown in the misty Dabie Mountain foothills. Visit the Xinyang Tea Museum to learn about the 2,300-year tea heritage, then tour the terraced plantations where the tea is picked by hand each spring. Your driver knows the best tea farms that welcome visitors.

🏯

🏞️ Feilaifeng Scenic Area (飞来石)

Mountain Peak • Taoist Heritage

A dramatic granite peak atop the Dabie Mountain foothills, with stunning views over the surrounding tea country. The area is rich in Taoist legends and ancient temples. The hiking trail to the summit takes about 90 minutes and rewards visitors with panoramic views of Xinyang's green countryside.

🌸

🛕 Jingang Temple & Ancient Ginkgo

1,400-Year-Old Tree • Tang Dynasty Temple

Jingang Temple (金刚寺) houses one of China's oldest ginkgo trees — over 1,400 years old, 30 meters tall, and 8 meters in circumference. The temple dates to the Tang Dynasty (618–907 AD). In autumn, the ginkgo turns brilliant gold and attracts photographers from across China.

🌊

🌊 Xin River Scenic Embankment

Urban Riverside • Evening Strolls

The Xin River (浉河) divides Xinyang's old and new districts. The riverside embankment is beautifully lit in the evening, with the Moon Landing Bridge (望湖桥) and Xinyang's famous "Tea Cup" Bridge creating iconic photo opportunities. Locals gather here for evening dances, tea, and strolls along the water.

🏛️

⛰️ Xinyang Museum (信阳博物馆)

Chu Kingdom Culture • Regional History

The Xinyang Museum houses over 10,000 artifacts from the ancient Chu Kingdom period, including bronze ritual vessels, jade ornaments, and pottery. The collection tells the story of Xinyang's 2,300-year history as a center of Chu culture. Admission is free.

Xinyang's Best Photo Spots

Capture Henan's greenest city from its most scenic angles.

🌅 Nanwan Lake Sunrise

Best at dawn

The mist rising off Nanwan Lake at sunrise, with forested islands emerging from the fog, is one of Henan's most breathtaking scenes. Arrive early before the tour boats start. Your driver can take you to the best lakeside viewpoint before the crowds arrive.

🍃 Tea Terraces at Spring Plucking

Best in early April

The terraced tea gardens south of Xinyang city, in the Dabie Mountain foothills, are a sea of bright green in early April during the first spring plucking. Women in straw hats picking tea leaves against the mountain backdrop make perfect travel photos.

🌸 Jingang Temple Ginkgo in Autumn

Best in November

China's oldest ginkgo tree — 1,400 years old — turns brilliant gold in November, with golden leaves carpeting the temple ground. Photographers come from across China. Arrive early on a sunny morning for the best light and fewer visitors.

🌉 Xin River "Tea Cup" Bridge at Night

Best after dark

Xinyang's most distinctive bridge, shaped like a traditional tea cup, is beautifully illuminated at night with the Xin River reflecting the lights. The riverside promenade is lively with evening strollers and local dancers.

🏔️ Feilaifeng Peak Overlooking Tea Country

Best on clear mornings

The summit of Feilaifeng offers a panoramic view of Xinyang's rolling green hills, tea gardens, and the Nanwan Lake reservoir in the distance. After rain, low clouds drift through the peaks creating a misty mountain atmosphere.

🌾 Rapeseed Flower Fields (Spring)

Best in March–April

In spring, the flat valleys around Xinyang are carpeted in brilliant yellow rapeseed flowers, creating a vivid contrast with the green hills. The fields near Gushi County and the Nanwan Lake approach roads are the most photogenic spots.

Food & Shopping in Xinyang

🍽️ Must-Try Xinyang Cuisine

Xinyang Mao Jian Noodles (信阳毛尖面)

A Xinyang specialty — wheat noodles tossed with brewed Xinyang Maojian green tea infusion, sesame paste, and pickled vegetables. The subtle tea flavor makes this unlike any other noodle dish in China. Try it at morning markets near the Xin River.

Freshwater Fish from Nanwan Lake

Nanwan Lake is rich in carp, bass, and perch. Local restaurants serve fresh fish in a variety of styles — steamed with ginger and scallion, braised in soy sauce, or in a spicy hot pot. Lakeside restaurants near the scenic area offer the freshest catch.

Xinyang Steamed Ground Chicken (信阳汽蒸鸡)

A local specialty — free-range chicken from the Dabie Mountain foothills, steamed with mushrooms, ginger, and rice wine. The slow-steaming method keeps the meat incredibly tender and juicy. Best at traditional farm-to-table restaurants outside the city.

Bamboo Shoots & Wild Mushrooms

The Dabie Mountain foothills produce exceptional bamboo shoots and wild mushrooms in spring. Stir-fried with garlic and a splash of soy sauce, they showcase Xinyang's pristine mountain ingredients. Your driver can recommend the best local restaurants.

🛍️ Shopping Destinations

Xinyang Tea Market (信阳茶叶市场)

The best place to buy authentic Xinyang Maojian. Located near the city center, the tea market has dozens of vendors selling directly to the public. Prices are reasonable if you buy in small quantities and taste before purchasing. Ask your driver to take you there.

Shangcheng Department Store

Xinyang's main department store chain, with a flagship store on Minzu Avenue (民族路). Good for local specialties, clothing, and everyday items. The basement food hall has an excellent selection of Xinyang teas and local snacks.

Xin River Night Market

The evening market along the Xin River embankment comes alive after sunset. Street food stalls sell local snacks, grilled meats, and fresh fruit. It's a great place to experience Xinyang's evening atmosphere and try local delicacies.

🎁 What to Buy in Xinyang

Xinyang Maojian Tea (信阳毛尖)

One of China's Top 10 famous teas. Look for the green, slender leaves with a distinctive fresh, chestnut-like aroma. The best quality is from the spring first-flush harvest (明前茶, pre-Qingming). Available at the Xinyang Tea Market or the Tea Research Institute gift shop.

Xinyang Glutinous Rice Cakes (信阳糍粑)

Traditional rice cakes made from glutinous rice, often stuffed with sweet red bean paste. A Xinyang specialty available at wet markets and food shops. They can be sliced and fried with eggs for breakfast or eaten plain as a snack.

Chu Kingdom Style Lacquerware

Henan has a long tradition of lacquerware inspired by the ancient Chu Kingdom art found in Xinyang Museum. Look for lacquered boxes, bowls, and ornaments featuring phoenix and dragon motifs. Available at the museum gift shop and select craft stores.

Intangible Cultural Heritage & Living Crafts of Xinyang

Meet the traditional arts that have defined Xinyang for centuries — and the artisans keeping them alive.

🍵 Xinyang Tea Ceremony & Culture

Xinyang Maojian tea culture is a registered intangible cultural heritage of Henan Province. The traditional tea ceremony involves brewing Maojian in a glass cup, appreciating the green color and fresh aroma, and serving guests with the phrase "请喝茶" (Please drink tea).

Where to experience it: Xinyang Tea Museum, tea plantations during spring harvest, or traditional teahouses on Minzu Avenue.

🎭 Xinyang Lantern Making

Paper lantern making has been a Xinyang craft for centuries. Traditional Xinyang lanterns are made from bamboo frames covered with silk or paper, often featuring hand-painted landscapes and calligraphy. They are displayed during Spring Festival and Mid-Autumn Festival.

Where to find them: Craft shops near Xinyang Museum and the annual Spring Festival temple fair.

🪡 Xinyang Embroidery (Xinyang Xiu)

Henan embroidery is known for its fine needlework and vivid colors. Xinyang-style embroidery often features local flora — tea leaves, plum blossoms, and lotus — and Chu Kingdom motifs inspired by the ancient artifacts in the Xinyang Museum.

Where to find it: Xinyang Museum gift shop, folk art markets, and select craft boutiques.

🎋 Bamboo Weaving (Xinyang Zhizhu)

The Dabie Mountain foothills around Xinyang produce fine bamboo used for weaving baskets, mats, and decorative items. Traditional bamboo weavers create everything from tea caddies to large decorative screens featuring intricate landscape patterns.

Where to find it: Rural craft markets and bamboo plantation workshops near Feilaifeng.

🥁 Xinyang Flower Drum Lantern Dance

A traditional folk performance combining flower-drum lantern dances with drumming and folk singing. The dance is performed during Spring Festival and at local celebrations, with performers carrying flower lanterns and dancing to traditional percussion music.

Where to see it: Spring Festival temple fairs, Mid-Autumn Festival celebrations, and cultural performances at Nanwan Lake.

🏺 Chu Kingdom Bronze-Inspired Crafts

The ancient Chu Kingdom artifacts in the Xinyang Museum inspire modern craftwork. Contemporary artisans create replicas of bronze ritual vessels, jade ornaments, and lacquered items using traditional techniques. These pieces make meaningful souvenirs of Xinyang's deep history.

Where to find it: Xinyang Museum gift shop is the most reliable source for authentic craft pieces.

Xinyang Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Xinyang like a local.

🍃

Buy Tea Directly from Tea Farmers

Skip the tourist shops and visit the tea plantations south of the city in the Dabie foothills. In spring, tea farmers sell directly to visitors at the plantation gates. Prices are significantly lower, and you can taste before buying. Ask your driver to introduce you to a trusted farm.

🌅

Visit Nanwan Lake Before 8 AM

Nanwan Lake gets crowded with tour groups by mid-morning. Arrive at 7 AM when the mist is still on the water and you may have the lake to yourself. The first boat departs at 8 AM. Your driver can pick you up at your hotel early.

🍜

Eat Breakfast at the Xin River Morning Market

Start with mao jian noodles (信阳毛尖面) at a riverside stall, then walk the morning market near Minzu Avenue. Xinyang's morning markets are authentic and lively — locals doing their daily shopping, not tourists. The Xin River embankment makes a great post-breakfast stroll.

🌸

Seasonal Highlights

Spring: Tea picking in the Dabie foothills (March–April). Summer: Lotus flowers at Nanwan Lake. Autumn: Golden ginkgo at Jingang Temple. Winter: Warm fish hot pot and Xinyang tea by the Xin River.

🏯

Ask Your Driver for the "Lao Xiaochi" Spots

Xinyang's best food isn't in restaurants — it's at the small "lao xiaochi" (老小吃) holes-in-the-wall run by families for decades. Ask your driver for his personal favorite spots. Freshwater fish from Nanwan Lake at a family-run lakeside restaurant is an unforgettable experience.

🚗

Use a Private Car for Nanwan Lake

Nanwan Lake is spread over a large area with limited public transit. A private car lets you visit the tea museum, lakeside viewpoints, and a farm-to-table restaurant without multiple taxi haggling sessions. The scenic drive along the lake's southern shore is particularly beautiful.

Best Time to Visit Xinyang

Each season offers a different side of Henan's greenest city.

🍃

Spring

Mar–May · 10–25°C

The best time to visit. Tea picking season in March–April brings fresh Maojian tea. The Dabie Mountain foothills bloom with rapeseed flowers. Pleasant temperatures for Nanwan Lake walks.

Highlights: Tea plucking, Nanwan Lake cherry blossoms, rapeseed flowers

☀️

Summer

Jun–Aug · 22–35°C

Warm and humid with occasional rain. Nanwan Lake's lotus flowers are in full bloom. Peak season for lakeside retreats and boat trips. Afternoon thunderstorms are common — indoor tea house visits are a great backup plan.

Highlights: Lotus flowers at Nanwan Lake, evening Xin River strolls

🍂

Autumn

Sep–Nov · 10–24°C

The second-best season. Cooler temperatures and clear skies. The ancient ginkgo at Jingang Temple turns golden in November. The tea gardens are lush and green. Perfect weather for scenic drives and hiking at Feilaifeng.

Highlights: Golden ginkgo at Jingang Temple, tea harvest season

❄️

Winter

Dec–Feb · -2–8°C

Cold but mild for northern Henan. Indoor tea house culture is at its best — warm up with a pot of Xinyang Maojian. Fewer tourists and lower hotel prices. Snow occasionally dusts the Dabie Mountain peaks.

Highlights: Tea house culture, warm fish hot pot, snow-dusted peaks
