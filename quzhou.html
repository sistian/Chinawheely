Name Origin of Quzhou

Quzhou means "Qu Prefecture" in Chinese, named after the ancient Qu River. It is located in western Zhejiang at the junction of Zhejiang, Fujian, Jiangxi, and Anhui.

Quzhou Education & Universities

Quzhou University and Quzhou Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around Quzhou

Quzhou Airport (JUZ) has domestic flights. Quzhou Railway Station and Quzhou West Station connect to Hangzhou and other cities on the Shanghai–Kunming high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Quzhou Natural Resources & Environment

Quzhou lies in the hilly western Zhejiang with a humid subtropical climate. It has the Qiantang River tributaries, forests, and is famous for its ecological environment.

Quzhou Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Quzhou like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

Nanzong Confucius Temple is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Quzhou

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Quzhou

🍵 Tea Houses of Quzhou

Tea culture runs through the day in Quzhou: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
