Name Origin

Siping means "four directions peaceful" in Chinese. It is located in central Jilin and is an important transportation hub in Northeast China.

Education & Universities

Jilin Normal University and Siping Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Siping has no commercial airport; Changchun Longjia Airport is the nearest. Siping Railway Station and Siping East Station connect to Changchun and Shenyang on the Harbin–Dalian high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Siping lies on the Songnen Plain with a humid continental climate. It has fertile farmland and is an important grain production base.

Getting to Siping: Airports & Railway Stations

How to reach Siping by air, rail and road

✈️ Airports

✈️ Airports

No commercial airport in Siping

Siping has no commercial passenger airport, so most visitors arrive by rail or road and transfer to a private car for the last leg. Your driver collects you at the station or hotel and takes you straight to your destination in Siping.

🚄 Railway Stations

🚄 Siping Railway Station

Main rail station

Siping Railway Station is the principal rail station for Siping, with high-speed services on the national network. Taxis and buses meet arrivals at the station forecourt; a pre-booked private car is the easiest way to reach hotels and sights beyond the city centre.

🛣️ By road

Highways and expressways

Siping is reached by expressway from the provincial capital and neighbouring prefecture cities. Driving distances are usually 2–5 hours, and a private car is often the most comfortable way to cover several sites in one day.

Siping Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Siping like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

Manchu history is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Siping

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Siping

🍵 Tea Houses of Siping

Tea culture runs through the day in Siping: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
