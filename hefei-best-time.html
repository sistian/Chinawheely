Seasons Overview

The climate record of Hefei, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 16.2 °C; annual rainfall averages 1,001 mm; July is the hottest month at 28.3 °C; January the coldest at 2.8 °C; the climate pattern includes The monsoon, muggy heat.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 9.8–21.8 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 249 mm.

Highlights: Bao Gong Temple, Xiaoyaojin Park, Li Hongzhang Former Residence

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 25.3–28.3 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 442 mm.

Highlights: Bao Gong Temple, Xiaoyaojin Park, Li Hongzhang Former Residence

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 11–23.3 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 184 mm.

Highlights: Bao Gong Temple, Xiaoyaojin Park, Li Hongzhang Former Residence

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run 2.8–5.2 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 126 mm.

Highlights: Bao Gong Temple, Xiaoyaojin Park, Li Hongzhang Former Residence
