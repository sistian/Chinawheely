Name Origin

The name "Yichun" (伊春) means "Yichun River in Spring." The city is named after the Yichun River which flows through it. Known as the Forest Capital of China (林都), Yichun has 85% forest coverage — among the highest in China. The vast forests of the Lesser Khingan have been carefully preserved as a natural treasure.

Education & Culture

Yichun is a cultural center for northeastern China ethnic minorities. Yichun University specializes in forestry, ecology, and minority studies. The city has a rich Manchu and indigenous culture, with traditional woodcarving and birch bark crafts reflecting the forest culture of the region.

Transportation

Yichun does not have its own commercial airport. The nearest major airport is Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB), 230 km away — approximately 3 hours via the G1011 Harbin-Tongjiang Expressway. Yichun is served by Yichun Railway Station with connections to Harbin (4.5 hours). Our private car is the most comfortable way to reach Yichun from Harbin Airport.

Natural Environment

Yichun sits at the southern foot of the Lesser Khingan Mountains (小兴安岭) and the northern edge of the Sanjiang Plain. The city is blanketed in mixed coniferous and broadleaved forests — larch, Korean pine, birch, and oak. The Zhalong Nature Reserve nearby is one of China most important wetlands, home to the endangered red-crowned crane.

Top Yichun Attractions

From volcanic crater lakes to red-crowned crane sanctuaries — discover China Forest Capital.

🌊 Wudalianchi Volcanic Lakes (五大连池)

UNESCO Global Geopark - Five Connected Lakes

One of China most spectacular volcanic landscapes — five deep blue crater lakes connected by rivers, surrounded by volcanic rock, black sand beaches, and mineral hot springs. About 100 km from Yichun city center.

🏯 Zhalong Nature Reserve (扎龙保护区)

Red-Crowned Cranes - Wetland Paradise

One of China most important wetlands and the worlds most important habitat for the endangered red-crowned crane. In summer, hundreds of cranes perform their iconic breeding dances at dawn. About 180 km from Yichun.

🛕 Lesser Khingan Forest (小兴安岭)

Primeval Forests - Autumn Foliage Heaven

The Lesser Khingan Mountains north of Yichun are covered in ancient primeval forests. In late September, larch and birch forests turn gold and crimson — one of China most spectacular autumn displays.

🛕 Yichun Forest Museum

Forest Culture - Interactive Exhibits

A modern museum showcasing Yichun forest ecosystem, logging history, and conservation efforts. Features a 1:1 scale model of the primeval forest canopy and displays of local wildlife. Free admission.

🛕 Jianfeng National Forest Park

Ancient Trees - Canopy Walkway

A vast forest park with ancient Korean pine trees over 300 years old, canopy walkway, and scenic trails. Changes dramatically with seasons — green in summer, golden in autumn, snow-covered in winter.

🎭 Hongxinglong Farm

Rice Fields - Rural Landscape

A vast agricultural area known for pristine organic rice paddies and rural northeastern landscape. The flat farmland against forested mountains creates a striking contrast. Best in summer or autumn.

Getting to Yichun: Airports & Railway Stations

Yichun has no airport. Here is how to reach Yichun from the nearest major hubs.

Nearest Airports

🗼 Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB)

230 km to Yichun - 3 hours by road

HRB is Heilongjiang Province main aviation hub. It has two terminals and serves flights to most major Chinese cities and international destinations in Russia, Japan, and Korea. The drive to Yichun via the G1011 Tongjiang Expressway takes approximately 3 hours.

🌊 No Local Airport in Yichun

Yichun city itself has no commercial airport

The closest airport is Harbin Taiping (HRB). Mudanjiang Airport (MDG) is another option but further away. Our driver can arrange transfers from either — just let us know your arrival details when booking.

Railway Stations

🌊 Yichun Railway Station (伊春站)

Main station - Near city center

The main railway station serving Yichun, with conventional train connections to Harbin (4.5 hours) and other northeastern cities. High-speed rail is limited in this direction. Private car pickup recommended.

🌊 Yichun West Station (伊春西站)

Regional and freight trains

Primarily serves regional and freight train operations. Less important for tourist arrivals but may be useful for specific routes within Heilongjiang Province.

Yichun Best Photo Spots

Capture China Forest Capital from its most scenic angles.

⛰️ Lesser Khingan Autumn Foliage

Best in late September

The Lesser Khingan Mountains north of Yichun put on one of China most spectacular autumn shows — larch forests turning gold, birch turning yellow, and Korean pines staying deep green.

🌊 Wudalianchi Volcanic Lakes

Best in summer

The five volcanic crater lakes glow an extraordinary deep blue against the black volcanic rock. The mineral hot springs create steam in cold weather. Sunrise over the lakes is particularly striking.

🦅 Zhalong Cranes at Dawn

Best at sunrise in summer

Hundreds of red-crowned cranes performing their elaborate courtship dances at dawn, with mist rising from the wetland. One of China most iconic wildlife photography opportunities.

🛕 Jianfeng Forest Canopy Walkway

Best in autumn and winter

The canopy walkway in Jianfeng National Forest Park gives a unique perspective above the forest floor. In autumn, looking down through the canopy at the golden and crimson leaves is breathtaking.

🌊 Wudalianchi Black Sand Beach

Best in summer

Wudalianchi volcanic black sand beach (火山石滩) is unique in China — black volcanic pebbles along a lake shore. The contrast between the dark sand, blue water, and green surrounding hills is striking.

🛕 Yichun Riverside at Dusk

Best at sunset

The Yichun River waterfront near the city center is beautifully lit at dusk. Willow trees along the river, the historic wooden bridge, and city lights reflecting on the water create a peaceful urban landscape.

Food & Shopping in Yichun

Must-Try Yichun & Northeastern Cuisine

Wild Mushroom Hotpot (山野菜火锅)

The forests around Yichun produce exceptional wild mushrooms — matsutake, bolete, and chanterelle — used in a hearty hotpot. A warming dish perfect for Yichun cool climate. Your driver can take you to a forest village restaurant for an authentic wild mushroom feast.

Siberian Pine Nut Porridge (松子粥)

Yichun is one of China main pine nut producing regions. Pine nut porridge with honey and dried fruit is a traditional local breakfast — nourishing, warming, and unique to the forest region.

Steamed Fish from Wudalianchi Lakes

Fish from the volcanic crater lakes of Wudalianchi are prized for their fresh, clean flavor. Steamed with ginger and scallion, or braised in soy sauce. Best at lakeside restaurants in Wudalianchi.

Northeastern Dumplings (东北饺子)

Northeastern Chinese dumplings (jiaozi) are legendary — larger than southern varieties, with thick chewy skins and generous pork and cabbage fillings. Made fresh at family-run dumpling restaurants throughout Yichun.

Medical History & TCM Heritage of Yichun

Discover the traditional medicine and natural healing heritage of China Forest Capital.

Lesser Khingan Medicinal Herbs

Ancient forest pharmacy. The vast forests of the Lesser Khingan Mountains are home to over 1,500 species of medicinal plants. Ginseng (人参, Panax ginseng) from the Yichun forests was historically prized as one of the finest in China. Other important medicinal herbs include astragalus (黄芪), licorice root (甘草), and cordyceps (冬虫夏草).

Where to find it: Yichun Forest Products Market, TCM pharmacies, and specialty herb shops throughout the city.

Wudalianchi Mineral Springs

Volcanic mineral healing waters. Wudalianchi volcanic area produces natural mineral water with a unique composition from the volcanic geology. The mineral water is believed to aid digestion, metabolism, and skin conditions. The Wudalianchi spa resorts offer therapeutic bathing in the mineral-rich waters.

Where to find it: Wudalianchi Geopark spa resorts, about 100 km from Yichun city center.

Manchu Birch Bark Medicine Bundles

Traditional Manchu forest medicine. Indigenous peoples of the Lesser Khingan — including the Manchu and Daur ethnic groups — developed unique medicinal traditions using forest plants, bark, and fungi. Birch bark preparations (桦树皮药) were used for wound healing and skin conditions.

Where to learn more: Yichun Forest Museum has displays on traditional forest medicine and indigenous healing practices.

Zhalong Crane Habitat & Wetland Medicine

Wetland healing environment. The Zhalong wetland ecosystem has been used traditionally for its therapeutic air quality and medicinal plants. The lotus and reed communities have been used in TCM for their cooling and blood-circulation properties. Modern wellness retreats incorporate Zhalong wetland environments.

Where to experience: Zhalong Nature Reserve has a wetland ecology center with information on the medicinal plants of the wetland.

Yichun Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Yichun like a local.

Autumn Foliage Timing is Everything

The Lesser Khingan autumn foliage peaks around September 20 to October 5. The larch turns golden first, then the birch, then the maples turn crimson. Timing your visit within this window is essential — follow Yichun tourism WeChat accounts for real-time foliage reports.

Zhalong Cranes: Go at Dawn

The red-crowned cranes are most active at dawn, performing their elaborate courtship dances. The first feeding session starts around 6 AM. Your driver can pick you up at 4:30 AM for the 180 km drive — it is worth every early minute.

Seasonal Highlights

Spring: Wildflowers bloom in the forest. Summer: Cool forest retreat, crane watching. Autumn: Spectacular Lesser Khingan foliage (late Sep-Oct). Winter: Snow-covered forests, ice activities, Zhalong frozen wetland.

The best food in Yichun is at small family-run restaurants in the forest villages near Wudalianchi and along the Lesser Khingan mountain roads. Your driver knows the best spots — wild mushroom hotpot at a forest village is unforgettable.

Summer Escape: Yichun is Much Cooler Than Harbin

Yichun forest setting means summer temperatures average just 20-25C — a welcome relief from Harbin 30+C summer heat. Many Harbin residents have summer homes in Yichun for this reason.

Best Time to Visit Yichun

Each season offers a different side of China Forest Capital.

Spring

Apr-May - 5-18C

Wildflowers bloom in the forests and the Zhalong wetland comes alive with migrating birds. The weather is warming but still cool.

Highlights: Zhalong crane migration, wildflowers, forest hiking

Summer

Jun-Aug - 18-28C

Yichun is at its most vibrant. The forests are lush and green, Zhalong cranes are breeding. Summer temperatures make it a perfect escape from city heat.

Highlights: Cool forest retreat, Zhalong cranes, Wudalianchi swimming

Autumn

Sep-Nov - 2-18C

The most spectacular season. Lesser Khingan forests turn gold and crimson in one of China best autumn foliage displays. Late September to early October is peak.

Highlights: Lesser Khingan foliage, photography, Wudalianchi autumn colors

Winter

Dec-Mar - -20 to -5C

Yichun transforms into a snow-covered wonderland. Ice fishing, winter forest walks, and the frozen Zhalong wetland create a dramatic landscape.

Highlights: Snow-covered forests, ice activities, frozen Zhalong
