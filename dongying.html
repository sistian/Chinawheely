Name Origin

Dongying means "eastern camp" in Chinese. It was established as a city in 1983 on the Yellow River delta and is home to China's second-largest oilfield, Shengli Oilfield.

Education & Universities

China University of Petroleum (East China) is the main higher-education institution in Dongying.

Getting Around

Dongying Shengli Airport (DOY) offers domestic flights. Dongying Railway Station and Dongying South Station connect to Jinan and other cities. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Dongying lies where the Yellow River empties into the Bohai Sea. It has vast wetlands, the youngest land in China (new delta), and rich oil reserves.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Dongying

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Dongying

🍵 Tea Houses of Dongying

Tea culture runs through the day in Dongying: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
