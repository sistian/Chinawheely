Traditional Tea Houses in Harbin

Harbin is a city where tea is drunk through the day rather than in a single sitting. The tea houses and tea counters below are the places worth stopping at, and a car with a waiting driver is what makes it practical to visit more than one. The tea counters in Harbin sit on the commercial streets, so a tea stop and a sightseeing stop are usually the same stop.

Tea houses around the old quarter

Most of Harbin's tea trade happens in the streets around the old quarter, where the tea shops pour a pot for you at the counter rather than selling by the tin. Ask for a longjing, a shou pu-erh or a jasmine blend and they will let you taste before you buy.

Morning tea and the market stalls

Tea drinking in Harbin starts early: the markets open with the first kettle of the day, and the stalls around the old commercial streets are where the city's own blend is put together.

Tea Gardens & Plantations Near Harbin

The tea served in Harbin comes from the producing areas around the city — the green teas of the lower Yangtze and the darker fermented teas of the south-west — brought in by the usual supply routes.

No signature tea grown in Harbin

Tea Shopping Guide in Harbin

In Harbin, buy tea where it is poured rather than where it is packaged. The counters around the old commercial streets sell loose leaf by the jin, and most will let you taste before you decide.

Ask for the tea by name

Name the tea rather than the region: longjing, biluochun, tieguanyin, shou pu-erh. The shop will then bring the right grade, and the tasting is the point.

Buy loose, not only tins

Loose leaf is cheaper per gram and lets you buy the amount you will actually drink. Ask whether it is this season's picking.

Check the leaves, not the packaging

Look at the leaf: it should be whole, tight and evenly rolled for green tea, and dark and glossy for fermented tea.

Tea Ceremony Experience

What to expect

A pot is normally shared between two to four people, and the host refills it without asking. In Harbin this usually happens over a few hours rather than a single sitting.

Your driver can wait while the pot is brewed — a first round usually takes a few minutes to settle.
