Seasons Overview

The climate record of Hangzhou, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 17.5 °C; annual rainfall averages 1,483 mm; 7月 is the hottest month at 29.3 °C; 1月 the coldest at 5.3 °C; the climate pattern includes the midsummer drought, typhoons, the monsoon, cold waves, the plum-rain season.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 11.1–22 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 376 mm.

Highlights: West Lake, Lingyin Temple, Leifeng Pagoda

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 25–29.3 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 601 mm.

Highlights: West Lake, Lingyin Temple, Leifeng Pagoda

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 13.3–24.5 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 263 mm.

Highlights: West Lake, Lingyin Temple, Leifeng Pagoda

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run 5.3–7.4 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 243 mm.

Highlights: West Lake, Lingyin Temple, Leifeng Pagoda
