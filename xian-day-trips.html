Day Trip Tips

Two things make a day trip out of Xian work: agree the pick-up time and the return time before you leave, and keep the last stop within an hour of the city so the drive back stays easy. A private car with a waiting driver handles both — no schedule to keep and no luggage to move between vehicles.
