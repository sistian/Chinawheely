Name Origin

Yangzhou means "rising prefecture" in Chinese. It was one of the wealthiest cities in ancient China during the Tang and Song dynasties, famous for the Grand Canal and gardens.

Education & Universities

Yangzhou University and Yangzhou Vocational and Technical College are main institutions.

Getting Around

Yangzhou has no commercial airport; Nanjing Lukou and Yangzhou Taizhou airports are used. Yangzhou Railway Station and Yangzhou East Station connect to Nanjing and Shanghai on the Lianyungang–Zhenjiang high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Yangzhou lies on the Grand Canal and the Yangtze River plain with a humid subtropical climate. It has rivers, lakes, and famous classical gardens.

Food & Shopping in Yangzhou

What to eat and what to bring home from Yangzhou

🍽️ Must-Try Yangzhou Cuisine

Yangzhou is famous for Huaiyang cuisine: Yangzhou fried rice, steamed buns, braised meatballs, and diced chicken with rice. Try morning tea (zaocha).

🛍️ Shopping in Yangzhou

Buy Yangzhou lacquerware, paper-cutting, jade carvings, and local snacks. Main shopping is on Wenchang Road and Dongguan Street.

🍵 Tea Culture

Traditional Yangzhou teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

🚗 Private Car for a Food Crawl

A private car and driver lets you start at the morning market, cross the city for lunch, and finish at an evening teahouse without worrying about parking or the last bus back to your hotel.

Yangzhou Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Yangzhou like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

Shouxi Hu is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Yangzhou

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Yangzhou

🍵 Tea Houses of Yangzhou

Tea culture runs through the day in Yangzhou: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
