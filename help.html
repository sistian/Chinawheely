Booking Questions

How do I book a ride?

Visit our booking page , select your service type, enter pickup/dropoff details, choose your vehicle, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation within 1 hour.

Can I modify my booking?

Yes, contact us via WhatsApp at +86-155-2777-1775 at least 2 hours before pickup. Changes are subject to availability.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight automatically. Your driver will wait up to 60 minutes after landing at no extra charge.

Payment Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal and Alipay. All payments are processed securely - no card details are collected on this site.

Is the price fixed?

Yes, the price you see is the price you pay. No hidden fees, no surge pricing.

How do refunds work?

See our cancellation policy . Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days to your original payment method.

Service Questions

Do your drivers speak English?

Our drivers are local professionals and most of them do not speak English; they use translation apps on their phone to follow your instructions. More importantly, every booking includes our English-speaking online customer service team \u2014 real people, available 7 days a week (8AM-12AM Beijing time) on WhatsApp and WeChat — you can reach our bilingual team on WhatsApp or WeChat in real time during your ride, and we will translate your needs to the driver immediately. If you want a fluent fluent English companion on-site, you can also add an Escort or Expert Guide during booking.

What if I need help communicating during the ride?

Every booking includes our English-speaking online customer service team - real people, available 7 days a week (8AM-12AM Beijing time) on WhatsApp and WeChat. Message our bilingual team on WhatsApp or WeChat during the ride, and we will translate between you and your driver instantly.

What vehicles do you offer?

Economy sedans (up to 3 passengers), Business sedans (up to 3 passengers), and MPVs (up to 6 passengers). All vehicles are clean, air-conditioned, and well-maintained.

Can I request a child seat?

Yes, child seats can be arranged at an extra charge - please tell us your child's age and weight and we will confirm the price before you pay. Please mention this in your booking notes.

Cancellation & Changes

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before your pickup time. Cancellations 24-48 hours ahead receive a 50% refund; under 24 hours there is no refund, but you can reschedule free of charge if you contact us at least 4 hours before pickup.

Can I change my pickup time after booking?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp or by email as soon as possible. We will accommodate changes free of charge if we have availability.

Vehicle & Service Questions

What vehicle types do you offer?

We offer Economy sedan (3 passengers), Business car (3 passengers), and MPV van (6 passengers). All vehicles are clean, air-conditioned, and well-maintained.

Do drivers speak English?

Yes. We specifically select drivers who can communicate in English for airport pickups, city tours, and business trips.
