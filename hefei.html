Key Fact

Name Origin

Hefei (合肥) means 'Confluence of Two Rivers' or 'Lake Fei,' referring to the Fei and Shi Rivers that once joined here. The city has a history of over 2,200 years and was an important center in the feudal states of Chu and Wu before becoming the capital of Anhui province.

Education & Universities

Hefei is one of China's rising science and education cities. University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) is one of the country's top universities, especially for physics and technology. Hefei University of Technology and Anhui University are also major institutions.

Metro & Transportation

Hefei Metro has 5 lines covering the city center, Hefei South Railway Station, universities, and the western high-tech zone. The system is modern and expanding, but many scenic and business areas are best reached by car.

Natural Resources & Environment

Hefei lies between Chaohu Lake, one of China's largest freshwater lakes, and the low hills of central Anhui. The city has many parks and green corridors, with a humid subtropical climate and distinct seasons. It is known as a science city with a clean, modern environment.

6 Things to Do in Hefei

Make the most of your visit to Hefei with these handpicked experiences — all easily accessible with a private car and professional licensed driver.

🏯

Visit the Xiaoyao Tower (逍遥津)

One of China's most famous historical parks, featuring an ancient pagoda, a large lake, and traditional pavilions. The park has been a centre of Hefei life for over 1,000 years and is particularly beautiful in spring.

🏛️

Explore the Anhui Museum

One of China's finest provincial museums, with a comprehensive collection of ancient Huizhou culture, Anhui ink paintings, and the famous Ming and Qing Dynasty Huizhou Ancient Architecture exhibition.

🌳

Stroll through Baohe Park and Chengzhi Pavilion

A pleasant park along the city's moat system with historic pavilions, a museum of Anhui's revolutionary history, and one of the best-preserved Ming dynasty halls outside of Beijing.

🍜

Eat Hefei's Famous Duck Blood Soup (莲花血鸭)

The signature dish of Anhui cuisine — crispy duck cooked with chillies and served with blood soup — is a bold, spicy flavour adventure. The most authentic versions are found in the old street restaurants near Shushan.

🏯

Visit Lord Liu's Residence (刘铭传故居)

The former home of Liu Yongfu, the famous Qing dynasty general who defended Taiwan against French invasion. The restored Ming-Qing compound provides a fascinating window into 19th-century elite life in Anhui.

🌸

Walk the University City Area near Anhui University

The tree-lined streets around Anhui University and the nearby Hefei University are surrounded by lively cafés, bookshops, and small restaurants that make for a pleasant afternoon wander.

Best season to travel in Hefei

🌸 Best Season March – May / October – November — Spring (March–May) offers the most comfortable weather for sightseeing, with blooming flowers across the city's parks. Autumn (October–November) brings cool, clear days — the best time for walking the Xiaoyao Tower gardens.

🌸
Spring March – May 10°C–26°C 🌸 Best

The best time to visit. Comfortable temperatures, blooming flowers across the city's parks, and pleasant conditions for walking the Xiaoyao Tower gardens.

☀️
Summer June – August 24°C–36°C Avoid

Hot and humid. The city's lakes provide natural cooling. Early morning visits to Xiaoyao Tower are recommended. Indoor attractions like the Anhui Museum are preferable in afternoon heat.

🍂
Autumn October – November 10°C–24°C 🍂 Best

The second-best season. Cool, clear days with beautiful autumn foliage. The Chengzhi Pavilion area is particularly attractive. Ideal for outdoor walks along the city's moat system.

❄️
Winter December – February 1°C–9°C OK

Cold and damp. The city is quiet with fewer tourists. The Anhui Museum is an excellent refuge from the cold.

Hefei Huangshan Maofeng (黄山毛峰) - Anhuis Most Famous Tea

Venue: Hefei major tea markets, Hefei tea houses   Hours: Year-round, best in spring

While Huangshan Maofeng (黄山毛峰) is named after Huangshan (Yellow Mountain), Hefei as the capital of Anhui province is the best place to taste and purchase this famous green tea. One of Chinas top ten teas, known for its white hairs (毫) on the leaves and delicate, sweet flavor.

Hefei Huangshan Maofeng (黄山毛峰) - Anhuis Most Famous Tea

Venue: Hefei major tea markets, Hefei tea houses   Hours: Year-round, best in spring

While Huangshan Maofeng (黄山毛峰) is named after Huangshan (Yellow Mountain), Hefei as the capital of Anhui province is the best place to taste and purchase this famous green tea. One of Chinas top ten teas, known for its white hairs (毫) on the leaves and delicate, sweet flavor.

Hefei Taiping Houkui (太平猴魁) Appreciation

Venue: Hefei upscale teahouses, Shushan District   Hours: Year-round

Taiping Houkui (太平猴魁) is one of Anhuis most prized teas, known for its unusually large, flat leaves and distinctive bitter-sweet flavor. Hefei teahouses with tea sommeliers specialize in serving this premium tea with proper gongfu brewing techniques.
