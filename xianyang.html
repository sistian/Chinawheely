Name Origin

Xianyang means "all sun, all yang" in Chinese. It was the capital of the Qin dynasty and is located near Xi'an in Shaanxi Province.

Education & Universities

Northwest A&F University (Xianyang branch) and Xianyang Normal University are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Xianyang has no commercial airport; Xi'an Xianyang International Airport is the main gateway. Xianyang Railway Station and Xianyang West Station connect to Xi'an and other cities. Xianyang is connected to Xi'an metro Line 1.

Natural Resources & Environment

Xianyang lies on the Guanzhong Plain with a temperate continental climate. It is on the Wei River and is famous for its historical and archaeological sites.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Xianyang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Xianyang

🍵 Tea Houses of Xianyang

Tea culture runs through the day in Xianyang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
