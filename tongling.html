Tongling at a Glance

A historic copper-mining city on the south bank of the Yangtze River in Southern Anhui - known as "China Copper Capital," surrounded by mountains and lakes, and gateway to Huangshan (Yellow Mountain).

Name Origin

The name "Tongling" (铜陵) literally means "Copper Mound" or "Copper Hill" - named for the copper deposits and copper mining that has sustained the city for over 3,000 years. According to historical records, the city supplied copper for the legendary Jiufeng Nine Tripod Cauldrons (九鼎) of the Xia Dynasty over 4,000 years ago. The city is called "China Copper Capital" (中国铜都) today - one of the largest copper production and processing centers in China.

Culture & Heritage

Tongling has a rich heritage in bronze casting and metalworking. The city has been producing bronze artifacts since the Shang and Zhou dynasties - some of the most sophisticated bronze work in Chinese history came from this region. The modern city is also known for its copper art and sculpture - large bronze sculptures of workers and industrial scenes dot the city. Tongling is also famous for its Tianjing (天井) town - a beautiful historic water town with canals, bridges, and traditional architecture.

Transportation

Tongling does not have its own airport. The nearest major airport is Nanjing Lukou International Airport (NKG), about 130 km away - approximately 1.5 hours by road. Hefei Xinqiao International Airport (HFE) is about 180 km away (2 hours). Tongling is served by the Nanjing-Jiujiang Railway (宁九铁路) with frequent trains to Nanjing (45 minutes), Hefei (1.5 hours), Shanghai (3 hours), and Jiujiang (1.5 hours). The G3 Beijing-Taipei Expressway and G50 Shanghai-Chongqing Expressway pass through Tongling.

Geography & Environment

Tongling is located in Southern Anhui Province, on the south bank of the Yangtze River where it is joined by the Qingyi River (青弋江). The city is surrounded by mountains and lakes - the landscape is hilly and green, unlike the plains of Northern Anhui. The Yangtze River forms the northern boundary of the city. The climate is subtropical monsoon - hot humid summers and cool winters. The city is about 100 km from Huangshan (Yellow Mountain) - a 1.5-hour drive making Tongling a popular stop for Huangshan visitors.

Top Tongling Attractions

Bronze casting heritage, mountain and lake scenery, and historic water town canals - Southern Anhui experiences.

🌲 Tongling Copper Museum

China Copper Industry Heritage

The Tongling Copper Museum (铜陵博物馆) is one of the few museums in China dedicated to a single industry. Exhibits include ancient bronze artifacts from the Shang and Zhou dynasties, historical mining equipment, and modern copper production technology. The museum is essential for understanding why Tongling has been called "Copper Capital" for 3,000 years.

🏘️ Tianjing Water Town (天井小镇)

Canals - Historic Architecture

Tianjing (天井) is a historic water town near Tongling known for its network of canals, traditional bridges, and well-preserved Ming and Qing dynasty architecture. Unlike the more famous tourist water towns, Tianjing remains an authentic working town with local residents, small workshops, and traditional street life. Beautiful at dawn when mist rises from the canals.

🌊 Shikong Lake (石榴湖)

Urban Lake - Copper Sculptures

Shikong Lake is a scenic urban lake in the center of Tongling, surrounded by parks and bronze sculptures of industrial workers. The lakeside promenade is popular for evening walks and the lake is illuminated at night with colorful lights reflecting off the water. The bronze worker sculptures are iconic of Tongling industrial heritage.

⛰️ Jiangnan Change Daily Scenic Area

Yangtze River Scenic

The Jiangnan Change Daily (江南改变日) scenic area along the Yangtze River bank near Tongling offers views of the broad river, cargo shipping traffic, and the distant mountains on the north bank. The area has walking paths, pavilions, and is particularly scenic at sunset when the river is gilded with golden light.

🌊 Daying River Scenic Area (大通河)

River & Mountain Views

The Daying River flows through Tongling area creating scenic mountain and water vistas. The river valley has hiking paths, small temples, and villages that offer a glimpse of traditional rural life in Southern Anhui. The area is particularly beautiful in autumn when the hillsides show autumn colors.

⛰️ Huangshan Day Trip (黄山)

Yellow Mountain - 100 km from Tongling

Tongling is one of the most convenient base cities for visiting Huangshan (Yellow Mountain) - one of China most famous scenic areas and UNESCO World Heritage Site. The drive from Tongling to Huangshan takes about 1.5 hours. Your driver can take you to Huangshan and wait while you explore, or arrange a multi-day trip combining Tongling and Huangshan.

Tongling Best Photo Spots

Capture bronze heritage, water town canals, and Yangtze River scenery.

🌊 Shikong Lake Bronze Sculptures at Night

Best after dark

The bronze sculptures of industrial workers around Shikong Lake are dramatically lit at night with colored lights. The reflections of the sculptures in the lake water create compelling photographs. The lakeside promenade is most atmospheric after 8 PM when the city quiets down.

🏘️ Tianjing Water Town Dawn Mist

Best at dawn

Tianjing Water Town at dawn, when mist rises from the canals and the morning light filters through the traditional architecture, is one of the most atmospheric water town scenes in Anhui. Arrive at opening time before the day-trippers to have the canals largely to yourself.

⛰️ Yangtze River Sunset

Best at golden hour

The Yangtze River near Tongling is broad and busy with cargo traffic. The riverside promenade at the Jiangnan Change Daily scenic area offers views of the river at sunset. The contrast of the river, distant mountains, and cargo vessels creates dynamic compositions.

🌲 Tongling Copper Museum Bronze Collection

Best with museum opening

The Tongling Copper Museum has a collection of ancient bronze artifacts including vessels, weapons, and ritual objects from the Shang and Zhou dynasties. The exhibits are beautifully displayed and the museum architecture is itself striking. Photography is permitted in most areas.

🌊 Daying River Valley in Autumn

Best October-November

The Daying River valley near Tongling shows vibrant autumn colors in October and November - the hillsides turn orange, red, and gold. The hiking paths along the river offer opportunities for landscape photography with the river and traditional village backdrop.

⛰️ Jiangnan Change Daily at Blue Hour

Best at dusk

The Yangtze riverfront at the Jiangnan Change Daily scenic area has a distinctive industrial-meets-natural character. The blue hour after sunset, when the sky turns deep blue and the city lights start to appear, creates atmospheric urban landscape photographs with the river and distant mountains.

Food & Shopping in Tongling

Must-Try Tongling & Southern Anhui Cuisine

Tongling Copper Duck (铜陵酱鸭)

A specialty of Tongling - duck braised in soy sauce and spices until the meat is tender and flavorful. The dish has a distinctive coppery-red color from the braising liquid. Best at local restaurants in the old city area near the lake.

Southern Anhui Steamed Fish

Fresh fish from the Yangtze River or local lakes steamed with ginger, scallion, and light soy sauce. Southern Anhui cuisine favors fresh ingredients and delicate cooking. The fish is often served live from the tank at waterside restaurants.

Tianjing Water Town Tofu

Tianjing is known for its hand-made tofu - fresh tofu made using traditional methods from local soybeans. The tofu has a delicate texture and fresh flavor. Try it at breakfast shops in the water town or at local restaurants.

Wenzhou Steamed Pork Dumplings

Though associated with Wenzhou, steamed pork dumplings (小笼包) are popular throughout Anhui and the Tongling area has excellent versions. The thin-skinned dumplings are filled with juicy pork and served in a small bamboo basket. A beloved breakfast item.

Huangshan Tea (黄山毛峰)

Huangshan (Yellow Mountain) produces one of China most famous green teas - Huangshan Maofeng (黄山毛峰). The tea is grown in the misty mountain environment and has a distinctive fresh, slightly sweet flavor. Available at tea shops throughout Tongling.

Shopping Destinations

Tongling Pedestrian Street

The main pedestrian shopping street near Shikong Lake has a mix of local brands, chain stores, and regional products. Good for everyday shopping and a glimpse of Tongling urban life. Evening is the most lively time.

Tianjing Water Town Souvenir Shops

The small souvenir shops in Tianjing Water Town sell local crafts, hand-made paper products, and traditional snacks. The shops are more authentic and less tourist-oriented than those in the famous water towns. Good for finding unique regional items.

What to Buy in Tongling

Tongling Copper Products

As China Copper Capital, Tongling has excellent quality copper products - tea sets, incense burners, bowls, and decorative items. Available at specialty copper shops in the city and at the Copper Museum gift shop. Prices are reasonable compared to copper products elsewhere.

Huangshan Green Tea

Huangshan Maofeng green tea from the Huangshan mountain area. Available at tea shops throughout Tongling. For the best quality, ask your driver to take you to a tea shop rather than a tourist store.

Bronze Replicas

Small replicas of ancient bronze artifacts - ritual vessels, bells, and weapons - based on the pieces in the Tongling Copper Museum. Sold at the museum gift shop and specialty copper shops. Authentic and well-made souvenirs of Tongling copper heritage.

Medical History & TCM Heritage of Tongling

Traditional medicine and wellness traditions of the Southern Anhui mountains and Yangtze River basin.

Southern Anhui Mountain Herbs

The mountainous region around Tongling and near Huangshan is a source of medicinal herbs. Huangshan (Yellow Mountain) in particular is known for medicinal herbs collected from its forests and meadows. Bai Zhu (白术, atractylodes) and Fu Ling (茯苓, poria) from the area are used in TCM for digestion and immune support. Local TCM pharmacies carry these regional herbs alongside standard preparations.

Available at Tongling TCM pharmacies and the Tianjing Water Town area.

Yangtze River Fish as Health Food

The clean waters of the Yangtze River and surrounding lakes produce high-quality freshwater fish that are valued in TCM nutritional therapy. Fish from the Yangtze is considered easily digestible and beneficial for kidney and spleen health. The local diet of fresh river fish, vegetables, and rice aligns with TCM dietary principles.

Best experienced at waterside restaurants near the Jiangnan Change Daily scenic area.

Bronze and Metal in Traditional Medicine

Copper and bronze have a historical role in traditional Chinese medicine. Bronze mirrors were used in ancient times for their believed therapeutic properties when reflecting sunlight. The long tradition of copper and bronze working in Tongling connects the city to these historical medical practices. Bronze scraping tools (bronze cupping) are still used in some TCM treatments.

Some Tongling TCM practitioners still use traditional bronze scraping tools.

Tianjing Water Town Wellness Environment

The water town environment of Tianjing - with its canals, clean air, and traditional architecture - has a recognized restorative quality. The water surface creates a cooling effect in summer, and the traditional buildings provide natural ventilation. The pace of life in the water town is slow and the environment is calming - well-suited for rest and recovery.

The water town is most peaceful in the early morning before tourist groups arrive.

Tongling Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Tongling like a local.

Book NKG Airport Transfer 24 Hours Ahead

Tongling has no airport. The 130 km drive from Nanjing (NKG) to Tongling takes 1.5 hours. Booking 24 hours ahead ensures your driver is ready at the airport when you land. Last-minute bookings may not have availability for the Tongling route.

Visit Tianjing Water Town Before 8 AM

Tianjing Water Town is an authentic working town - most tourists arrive after 9 AM. Visit at opening time (usually 8 AM) to experience the canals without crowds, see the morning routine of local residents, and photograph the mist rising from the water. Your driver can pick you up at your hotel at 7 AM.

Eat Tongling Copper Duck at Lakeside Restaurants

The best Tongling Copper Duck (铜陵酱鸭) is served at small restaurants on the north side of Shikong Lake. Look for the restaurants with the most local customers - the duck is braised fresh each day and the portions are generous.

Buy Copper Products at the Museum Shop

The Tongling Copper Museum gift shop has the best quality copper products - well-made, properly priced, and with clear provenance. Avoid the tourist shops near the highway exits which often sell imported copper items at inflated prices.

Go to Huangshan Early in the Morning

If you are visiting Huangshan as a day trip from Tongling, leave by 6 AM so you arrive at the mountain around 7:30 AM - before the tour groups. This gives you several hours to see the key sights before starting the drive back. The summit views are clearest in the morning.

Walk Shikong Lake at Night

The Shikong Lake promenade is most atmospheric at night when the bronze sculptures are dramatically lit and the city quiets down. An evening walk around the lake (about 30 minutes) is a pleasant way to end the day and offers excellent night photography opportunities.

Best Time to Visit Tongling

Subtropical monsoon climate with distinct seasons.

Spring

Mar-Apr - 10-22C

A pleasant season to visit Tongling. The weather is warming and the surrounding hills show fresh spring growth. The Daying River valley is particularly beautiful with spring wildflowers. Good for all outdoor activities and photography.

Highlights: Spring flowers, comfortable weather, river valley hikes

Summer

May-Aug - 22-34C

Hot and humid with frequent afternoon thunderstorms. Summer is a good time for the water town atmosphere - the canals and lakes are refreshing and the evening lakeside promenade is pleasant. Huangshan is green and at its most lush. Mosquitoes are present near water - bring repellent.

Highlights: Green mountain scenery, water activities, evening lake walks

Autumn

Sep-Nov - 10-25C

The best overall season to visit Tongling. The weather is comfortable, the air is clear, and the Daying River valley shows beautiful autumn colors (October-November). Huangshan is at its most photogenic with clear skies. Peak travel season but not crowded at Tongling itself.

Highlights: Autumn colors, clearest Huangshan views, comfortable temperatures

Winter

Dec-Feb - 2-10C

Cold but manageable. The city is quiet and indoor heated restaurants serve rich winter dishes. The bronze sculptures at Shikong Lake have a different atmosphere in winter mist. Fewer tourists and lower prices. Some outdoor attractions may have reduced hours.

Highlights: Quiet atmosphere, winter lake views, lowest prices
