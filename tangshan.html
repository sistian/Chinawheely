Name Origin

Tangshan means "Tang Mountain" in Chinese, named after a nearby hill. It is an industrial city in eastern Hebei famous for its 1976 earthquake and rebirth.

Education & Universities

Tangshan Normal University, North China University of Science and Technology, and Tangshan University are main institutions.

Getting Around

Tangshan Sannühe Airport (TVS) has domestic flights. Tangshan Railway Station and Tangshan West Station connect to Beijing and Tianjin on the Beijing–Tianjin–Tangshan railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Tangshan lies on the Bohai Sea coast with a temperate continental climate. It has coal, iron, and is a major steel and port city.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Tangshan

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Tangshan

🍵 Tea Houses of Tangshan

Tea culture runs through the day in Tangshan: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
