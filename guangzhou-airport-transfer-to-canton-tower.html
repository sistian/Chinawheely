Canton Tower stands 600 metres tall on the south bank of the Pearl River, with a 450-metre main body and a 146-metre antenna mast on top. It opened to the public on 1 October 2010 in time for the 16th Asian Games, and the twisted hyperboloid shape has been the city's skyline marker ever since. From Baiyun Airport it is about 30 kilometres south across the river, which is a straightforward run given clear traffic.

Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN)

30 km from the Guangzhou terminal, about 45 minutes by car.

Baiyun opened in August 2004 and sits 28 kilometres north of downtown Guangzhou in Baiyun and Huadu districts. The run south on the Airport Expressway and the north ring road crosses the Pearl River before dropping you in Haizhu District, a few minutes from the tower's base at the foot of Chigang Pagoda.

Why transfer to Canton Tower?

The structure is the point. Designed by the Dutch practice IBA with Ove Arup as engineering consultant, the tower is a hyperboloid generated from two ellipses, and it uses three elements — the vertical cylinder, the horizontal ring and the diagonal — in a way that has no close comparison. The whole thing weighs 98,450 tonnes and was built by the Shanghai Construction Group, with construction starting in 2005 and the structure topped out in September 2009. It was the second tallest tower in the world after Tokyo's Skytree and the second tallest tower-type structure in China.

There is more to do inside than the view alone suggests. The main body carries a 450-metre platform and observation capsules, and just above 452 metres there is a large open-air observation deck. A rooftop terrace at 488 metres opened in December 2011 and was, at the time, the highest and largest outdoor observation deck in the world, taking the title from the Burj Khalifa's deck at 452 metres. The highest and longest open-air staircase anywhere, the Skywalk, starts 170 metres up and spirals almost another 170 metres through the waist of the structure.

What to do at Canton Tower once you arrive

Start at ground level and climb on foot, because the fastest part of the visit is the vertical one. The Skywalk is the reason most people come: it begins 170 metres above the plaza and spirals almost 170 metres further through the waist of the tower, open on one side the whole way, so you can see the Pearl River, Haixinsha island and the Zhujiang New Town skyline rotating around you as you climb. The tower stands 125 metres back from the south bank of the Pearl River, so the view from the upper stairs looks straight across the water.

At the top, the observation decks sit just above 452 metres and the rooftop terrace is at 488 metres, which means a clear evening gives you the tower's own lighting plus the view across the river in one place. The horizontal Ferris wheel at the 450 to 454 metre level carries 16 capsules and takes around 20 minutes for a circuit. Below ground level, the tower's own entrance hall has the usual ticket desks and a large-scale model of the structure, which is a good place to pick up the layout before you ride up.

  • Canton Tower station on the Guangzhou Metro is served by Line 3 and the APM line, both of which run under the tower
  • The open-air Skywalk is exposed, so check the weather before committing to the climb
  • Evening is the better time, when the tower's lighting is on and the river reflects it
  • Book the upper decks and the Ferris wheel separately if you want both, and allow at least two hours in total

Arriving at Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport

Baiyun is a large airport with a Terminal 1 that handles regional and short-haul traffic and a Terminal 2 that Skytrax rates a five-star terminal, and it served 63 million passengers in 2023, ranking twelfth in the world by traffic. The road run south to the Haizhu District crosses the Pearl River, and at 28 kilometres from downtown the drive takes about 45 minutes when it is clear and considerably longer in the evening peak. Terminal 1 handles regional and short-haul services while Terminal 2 takes the main domestic and international traffic.

Allow twenty minutes for checked bags and immigration before you meet your driver in the arrivals hall. Every booked driver carries a name board and monitors your flight number, so a delayed arrival does not need a phone call. The old Baiyun airport opened in 1932 and the replacement came online on 5 August 2004, built at a cost of 8.8 billion yuan on a site nearly five times the size of its predecessor. If you want to be at the Skywalk for sunset, factor the river crossing into your timing rather than planning to arrive exactly at sunset.

What the transfer costs

A private car from CAN to Canton Tower is typically priced at 140 to 200 yuan for a sedan, with the quote covering fuel, tolls on the airport expressway and the driver's waiting time at the drop-off. A sedan suits a couple travelling with hold luggage. Groups travelling together tend to pick the MPV class at roughly 180 to 250 yuan for the run from Baiyun.

Past the six-hour mark, pricing switches to a flat day package instead of metre distance; the standard waiting allowance of two hours covers a full trip up the Canton Tower and back to the kerb. A sedan handles two people with hold luggage comfortably, while a van or seven-seat MPV suits a family or a group of visitors heading for the same deck, and the quote reflects the distance element plus the vehicle class.

Ask for the drop-off at the tower's main entrance on Yizhou Road rather than at the roadside, so you walk in through the plaza. If you want the driver to wait, agree a pick-up point at the same entrance, because the drop-off area is a loop and cannot be used as a standing car park.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is it from Guangzhou Baiyun Airport to Canton Tower?

Baiyun Airport lies 28 kilometres north of downtown Guangzhou, and Canton Tower stands across the Pearl River in the Haizhu District to the south of the centre, so the road distance is roughly 30 km. Expect 45 minutes with clear traffic and closer to an hour during the evening peak when the river crossings slow down.

How much does it cost to go up Canton Tower?

Prices have changed several times since the tower opened, so treat published rates as a starting point rather than a fixed figure. Reporting from the opening period put the observation deck ticket at 150 yuan, and the rooftop Ferris wheel opened to the public on 1 September 2011 at 130 yuan per ride. Check the current tariff before you travel, and book online if you want to avoid the entrance queue.

Which metro line goes to Canton Tower?

The Guangzhou Metro has a Canton Tower station that serves two lines: Line 3 on its south-east run through Kecun and Zhujiang New Town, and the APM line, an automated people mover that connects the tower with the Haixinsha island in the north. Line 3 opened in December 2005, and the APM service runs directly beneath the tower, so the station is inside the complex.

Is the Skywalk worth it and how long does the visit take?

The Skywalk is the distinguishing feature of the visit. It is the highest and longest open-air staircase in the world, starting at 170 metres and spiralling almost 170 metres further through the waist of the tower, and the fact that it is open-air is the whole experience. Allow two hours for the upper decks and the Ferris wheel, and check the weather first because there is no shelter on the stairs.

Should I visit in the day or in the evening?

The evening is stronger. The tower's lighting comes on after dark and reflects in the Pearl River, and from the 488-metre rooftop terrace you get the tower's own illumination and the Zhujiang New Town skyline in the same view. In the daytime you see the structure itself and the river traffic. If you want both, arrive in the late afternoon and stay through sunset.
