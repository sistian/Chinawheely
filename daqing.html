Name Origin

Daqing means "great celebration" in Chinese, named when oil was discovered in 1959. It is China's most famous oil city and the birthplace of the Daqing Oilfield.

Education & Universities

Northeast Petroleum University and Heilongjiang Bayi Agricultural University (Daqing) are the main higher-education institutions.

Getting Around

Daqing Sartu Airport (DQA) offers domestic flights. Daqing Railway Station, Daqing East Station, and Daqing West Station connect to Harbin and other Northeast cities. Buses and taxis are available.

Natural Resources & Environment

Daqing is located on the Songnen Plain with vast oil reserves. The region has wetlands, grasslands, and a cold continental climate. It is famous for the Daqing Oilfield.

Daqing Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Daqing like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

石油城博物馆 is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Daqing

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Daqing

🍵 Tea Houses of Daqing

Tea culture runs through the day in Daqing: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
