Name Origin of Lufeng

Lufeng (禄丰) was a county in Chuxiong Yi Autonomous Prefecture until April 2021, when it was reorganised as a county-level city. The name comes from 禄 meaning prosperity and 丰 meaning abundance — a county that was historically rich in grain and known across Yunnan for its salt wells, its bronze castings and, from the 1930s, its dinosaur fossils.

Lufeng Education & Universities

Higher education in Lufeng is provided by the city's local colleges, vocational schools and teacher-training institutions rather than by large research universities. The best-known of these are the teacher-training and vocational schools that feed the local mining, metallurgy and construction trades.

Getting Around Lufeng

Lufeng has no airport of its own. The nearest is Kunming Changshui International Airport (KMG), and most travellers continue to Lufeng by rail or road. Lufeng South Station and Guangtong North Station on the Kunming–Chuxiong railway, and Heijing Station on the old Chengdu–Kunming line, serve the city; buses and taxis cover the urban area.

Lufeng Natural Resources & Environment

Lufeng sits in the dry-hot valley country of central Yunnan, on the eastern edge of the Yunnan-Guizhou Plateau. It is one of the world's richest dinosaur sites — the Lufeng basin has yielded a remarkable sequence of Jurassic and Cretaceous fossils — and it is also where the Ramapithecus hominid fossils, among the oldest hominin remains ever found, were unearthed in the 1950s.

🚄 Lufeng Railway Stations

🚄 Rail connections

Rail access to Lufeng

Lufeng South Station and Guangtong North Station on the Kunming–Chuxiong high-speed railway, and Heijing Station on the Chengdu–Kunming line, connect the city to Kunming, Chuxiong and Dali. Pre-booking a private car for that final leg avoids a bus transfer; your driver waits on the platform.

🛣️ By road

Highways and expressways

Lufeng is reached by expressway from Chuxiong and the neighbouring county cities, and the G56 Hangzhou–Ruili expressway passes the county seat. Driving distances are usually 1–2 hours, and a private car is often the most comfortable way to cover the fossil sites and the salt towns in one day.

Food & Shopping in Lufeng

What to eat and what to bring home from Lufeng

🍽️ Must-Try Lufeng Cuisine

The kitchen follows the local agricultural belt: the mushrooms, wild vegetables and cured meat of the dry-hot valleys, plus the salt-cured ham and preserved vegetables that the old salt towns built their trade on.

🛍️ Shopping in Lufeng

Shopping centres on the everyday commercial streets around the centre, where you will find local tea, snacks and crafts, plus a mall or two for anything you could not find locally.

🍵 Tea Culture in Lufeng

Traditional Lufeng teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

🚗 Private Car for a Food Crawl

🏛️ Heritage & Landmarks

Heijing, the old salt town, carries most of Lufeng's heritage: the wellhead towers, the guild halls of the salt merchants and the old stilt-house streets above the still-working salt pans. The county seat's bronze casting tradition and the Lufeng dinosaur sites are the other two things the town is known for.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Lufeng

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Lufeng

🍵 Tea Houses of Lufeng

Tea culture runs through the day in Lufeng: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
