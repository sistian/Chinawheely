Seasons Overview

The climate record of Nanchang, month by month.

The annual average temperature is 18 °C; annual rainfall averages 1,614 mm; July is the hottest month at 29.5 °C; January the coldest at 5.5 °C; the climate pattern includes The monsoon, flooding, muggy heat, the rainy season.

🌸

Spring

Monthly means from March to May run 11.4–22.8 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 618 mm.

Highlights: Tengwang Pavilion, Bayi Uprising Memorial Tower, Mount Lu (Lushan)

☀️

Summer

Monthly means from June to August run 25.9–29.5 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 566 mm.

Highlights: Tengwang Pavilion, Bayi Uprising Memorial Tower, Mount Lu (Lushan)

🍂

Autumn

Monthly means from September to November run 13.7–25.1 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 204 mm.

Highlights: Tengwang Pavilion, Bayi Uprising Memorial Tower, Mount Lu (Lushan)

❄️

Winter

Monthly means from December to February run 5.5–7.9 °C. Rainfall over those three months totals about 226 mm.

Highlights: Tengwang Pavilion, Bayi Uprising Memorial Tower, Mount Lu (Lushan)
