Name Origin

Langfang means "lang's house" or "official post house" in Chinese. It is located midway between Beijing and Tianjin in Hebei Province.

Education & Universities

Langfang Normal University and North China Institute of Aerospace Engineering are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Langfang has no commercial airport; Beijing and Tianjin airports are used. Langfang Railway Station and Langfang North Station connect to Beijing and Tianjin on the Beijing–Shanghai high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Langfang lies on the North China Plain with a temperate continental climate. It is between the Yongding River and Chaobai River basins.

Getting to Langfang: Airports & Railway Stations

How to reach Langfang by air, rail and road

✈️ Airports

✈️ Airports

No commercial airport in Langfang

Langfang has no commercial passenger airport, so most visitors arrive by rail or road and transfer to a private car for the last leg. Your driver collects you at the station or hotel and takes you straight to your destination in Langfang.

🚄 Railway Stations

🚄 Langfang Railway Station

Main rail station

Langfang Railway Station is the principal rail station for Langfang, with high-speed services on the national network. Taxis and buses meet arrivals at the station forecourt; a pre-booked private car is the easiest way to reach hotels and sights beyond the city centre.

🛣️ By road

Highways and expressways

Langfang is reached by expressway from the provincial capital and neighbouring prefecture cities. Driving distances are usually 2–5 hours, and a private car is often the most comfortable way to cover several sites in one day.

What to eat and what to bring home from Langfang

Traditional Langfang teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

Intangible Cultural Heritage & Living Crafts in Langfang

The tea houses, workshops and old-quarter crafts of Langfang

🍵 Tea Culture

Tea culture runs through the day in Langfang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses are where the city's social life happens.

🏺 Local Crafts & Products

Grand Canal souvenirs, local snacks, and Hebei crafts are sold in Guangyang District.

🏛️ Heritage & Landmarks

大红门 is the heritage anchor of Langfang, and the old town streets around it are where the city's traditional crafts, small workshops and family businesses are still concentrated.

Langfang Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Langfang like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

大红门 is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.
