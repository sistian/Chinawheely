Overview: Photography in Xiamen

Xiamen is a city of two shorelines. On the island itself, a road of arcaded shophouses runs for a kilometre from the ferry waterfront, and a bridge of white steel sits so low over the water that the tide almost reaches the deck. Across the channel, an island of 1.88 square kilometres holds a settlement of Victorian villas and consulates, and above them a 92-metre rock. Seven locations cover both sides of the strait.

Gulangyu Island (鼓浪屿)

Gulangyu lies about 1,000 metres off the Xiamen waterfront in Siming district, covering 1.88 square kilometres with a recorded population of 12,509 in 2023, and it was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in July 2017. The island was the only international settlement on Chinese soil apart from Shanghai's, and its land regulations were approved in May 1902; thirteen countries, among them Britain, France, the Netherlands and Japan, held extraterritorial rights and sat on the municipal council, which explains the predominantly Victorian street architecture. Motor vehicles and bicycles are banned.

best light: The hour after sunrise, when the lanes between the villas are still empty and the tower on the far hill has not yet picked up its own shadow. · island, UNESCO world heritage site

Getting there: Reached by ferry from the Xiamen island waterfronts, with the island administered as Gulangyu Subdistrict within Siming district; the whole area is a pedestrian zone after the ferry landing.

  • With cars banned and 1.88 square kilometres to cover, the ferry landing and the southern slopes are a long walk apart; plan the route as a loop.
  • The Victoria-era villas stand in rows with narrow gaps, so a long lens compresses the facades into a repeating pattern.
  • The island's highest point is 92.7 metres, which means the sun clears the rooflines quickly and the shadows are short by mid-morning.

Sunlight Rock (日光岩)

Sunlight Rock, also called the Swaying Rock, is a granite boulder more than 40 metres high standing on the summit of Longtou Shan in the central southern part of Gulangyu, at an elevation of 92.68 metres, the highest point on the island. A round viewing platform has been built on the top, from which the whole of Gulangyu and the strait are visible in one arc. The rock belongs to the wider group of island sights that include the Yuyuan garden built for the doctor Lin Qiaozhi and the Shuzhuang garden of more than 20,000 square metres built from 1913 and donated to the city in 1951.

best light: Late afternoon, when the sun drops toward the mainland and the rock's west face, the harbour and the island roofs all separate into layers. · summit rock and viewing platform

Getting there: At the top of Longtou Shan in the middle and south of Gulangyu island, reached on foot from the ferry landing past the island's main street, with a stepped climb to the platform on the rock.

  • The climb is stepped and enclosed, so the summit views are the reward rather than the subject; work the platform edge rather than the steps.
  • At 92.68 metres the rock is above most of the island's rooflines, which makes it the natural high vantage for the harbour and the mainland skyline.
  • The Haoyue garden statue is 15.7 metres tall, a scale that only reads from a distance, so it needs a separate wide frame from the harbour side.

Nanputuo Temple (南普陀寺)

Nanputuo Temple stands at the foot of the Wulao peaks on the south-east of Xiamen island, at 515 Siming South Road in Siming district, and is the second largest Buddhist temple in the city. It was founded in the final years of the Tang dynasty as the Sizhou Temple, rebuilt by the monk Wenci in the Northern Song as Wujinyan, abolished in the Yuan and rebuilt in the Ming as Puzhao Si, moved to its present site. In 1684 the admiral Shi Lang donated money for a repair that included the Great Compassion Pavilion, and the temple remains influential across Minnan and Southeast Asia. It sits directly beside Xiamen University.

best light: Early morning, when the halls are lit from the east side and the ridge of Wulao behind the temple is still in shadow. · Buddhist temple

Getting there: On the south-east side of Xiamen island in Siming district, reached by bus from the city centre and on the approach road between Xiamen University and the Wulao ridge.

  • The main halls face the sea and the ridge behind, so the rooflines stack in a way that a raised position handles better than a ground-level frame.
  • The temple is adjacent to the university campus, so the two can be worked in the same walk with the light running from morning on the campus to afternoon on the ridge.
  • The Great Compassion Pavilion is the largest built element and was added in the 1684 rebuilding, which is the point where the construction history is visible in the fabric.

Yanwu Bridge (演武大桥)

Yanwu Bridge crosses between the naval pier at the south-west corner of the ring road and Baicheng at Xiamen University. It is 2.2 kilometres long, with a main span, the Chenggong Avenue interchange, the Yanwu interchange and the Baicheng interchange, and it opened on 6 September 2003 at a total construction cost of 560 million yuan. The structure uses a fish-belly girder with elliptical piers and the whole body is painted white, and to keep the sea view clear the lighting is fixed to the parapets with no lamp standards. The low span has a deck only 5.5 metres above sea level.

best light: Late afternoon, when the white steelwork takes the low sun and the wet tidal flats under the low span fill with reflected sky. · coastal bridge

Getting there: At the south-west corner of the ring road in Siming district, between the naval pier and the university side at Baicheng, reached on foot from the Baicheng beach and by bus along the ring road.

  • The deck is only 5.5 metres above sea level at the low span, so a long lens from the beach compresses the bridge against the far shore.
  • The parapet-mounted lighting means there are no vertical elements to break the horizontal line at night, which suits a low viewpoint.
  • The piers are elliptical rather than round, which shows in the reflections at high water and is invisible from the deck itself.

Zhongshan Road Arcade Street (中山路)

Zhongshan Road runs east to west across the south-west of Xiamen island in the Zhonghua area of Siming district, about 1 kilometre long and 15 metres wide, starting at Lujiang Dao and ending at the junction of Xinhua Road and Gongyuan South Road. It was laid out in 1925 to link Wanshi Shan with Gulangyu, and the frontage is continuous shophouse arcades. Since 2006 the western section has been protected as the Old City Zhongshan Road Historic Style District under the Xiamen Purple Line Control Plan, which bars large-scale demolition; the western section became a full pedestrian street in September 2006 and the eastern section followed in September 2010.

best light: Blue hour, when the continuous shopfronts are lit along the footbridge while the upper storeys keep some daylight. · historic commercial street

Getting there: In the Zhonghua area of Siming district in the old town of Xiamen island, one block inland from the Lujiang Dao waterfront, with the pedestrian section running west from the Gucheng crossroads.

  • The arcade runs the full length of the frontage, so a low camera under the covered walkway keeps the line of columns unbroken.
  • The road is only about 15 metres wide, which means the shopfronts on both sides dominate any wide frame and a longer lens is needed for a full elevation.
  • The west section has been pedestrian-only since 2006 and the east since 2010, so traffic-free frames have to be timed to the evening restrictions rather than to the day.

Huli Mountain Fort (胡里山炮台)

Huli Mountain Fort on the south side of Xiamen island holds a 280 millimetre coastal gun cast by the German Krupp works in 1894, which is identified as the largest surviving nineteenth century coastal gun in the world. The fort was entered in the fourth group of national key cultural relics in 1996, recorded in the Guinness Book of Records in 2000 and rated a national 4A tourist attraction in 2009. A battery had existed on the site since the Ming period, and in 1841 a granite sea wall was built from Baishitou to Shapowei, 1,667 metres long, 3.3 metres high and 2.6 metres thick, mounting 100 guns.

best light: Late afternoon, when the sea gun's long barrel throws a hard shadow across the platform and the sea behind it holds a bright band. · coastal fortification and museum

Getting there: On the south coast of Xiamen island near the Shapowei waterfront in Siming district, close to the east end of the ring road and the Yanwu Bridge approach.

  • The 280 millimetre gun is the largest surviving nineteenth century coastal gun, and its scale only reads if it is framed with the platform and the sea behind it.
  • The 1841 granite wall is 1,667 metres long and 3.3 metres high, so the low section along the shore is the subject rather than the gun alone.
  • The fort was handed over from the military to local administration in 1984 and is now run as a managed attraction, so the platform and the wall are both accessible on foot.

Huandao Road (环岛路)

Huandao Road is the metropolitan road that circles Xiamen island, crossing the Siming and Huli districts for a total length of 43 kilometres and carrying the local road number 401. Construction began in 1991 and ran in three phases at a total cost of close to 3.8 billion yuan, and the route is a four or six lane arterial throughout. It takes in the Yanwu Bridge, the southern and eastern ring sections, Huyu Road, the northern ring, Chang'an Road and Lujiang Dao, starting at the Shigu interchange in the north of the island.

best light: Evening, when the road surface holds the last sky and the bay on the seaward side goes dark before the hillside does. · coastal highway

Getting there: The ring road of Xiamen island, from the Shigu interchange in the north through the eastern and southern arcs down to Lujiang Dao at the harbour, with the Yanwu Bridge and Baicheng sections the most accessible from the city centre.

  • At 43 kilometres the road is too long to cover in one session, so pick a two or three kilometre arc rather than trying to drive the whole ring.
  • The seaward side is a continuous bay with the city behind it, which is the strongest single composition in the city.
  • The road links directly to Yanwu Bridge at the south-west corner, so the two can be worked together without an extra transfer.

Camera gear for Xiamen

A weather seal and a microfibre cloth matter more here than a second body. Xiamen is a maritime city with a summer monsoon and 43 kilometres of open sea road, so salt spray, humidity and sudden rain are routine, and a damp front element ruins more frames than any filter. A circular polariser is still worth carrying, mainly for the tidal flats under the low span of Yanwu Bridge, the wet sand at Baicheng and the glazed roofs of the temples, all of which flare without one.

On the lens side, the island rewards a long focal length rather than a wide one. Gulangyu's Victorian villas stand shoulder to shoulder with narrow gaps between them, and the same compression makes the 40-metre boulder of Sunlight Rock separate cleanly from the roofs below it. The only genuinely wide subjects in the city are the bridge deck from the water and the island skyline from the ring road. Exposure on the white-painted steelwork of Yanwu Bridge and on pale stonework elsewhere both clip easily, so reading off the highlights and giving back two thirds of a stop holds the detail in the paint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can you not drive onto Gulangyu?

The island bans motor vehicles and bicycles. Only small electric buggies and electric government service vehicles are permitted, so everything on the island is walked or ridden on one of those. It covers 1.88 square kilometres and had a recorded population of 12,509 in 2023, so the ferry landing and the southern sights are a long walk apart.

What makes the Gulangyu buildings look European?

The island was the only international settlement on Chinese soil apart from the one at Shanghai. Its land regulations were approved by the Qing government in May 1902, and thirteen countries, among them Britain, France, the Netherlands and Japan, held extraterritorial rights and served on the Kulangsu Municipal Council. The consulates, churches, hospitals, schools and police stations they built are why the architecture reads as predominantly Victorian.

How close does Yanwu Bridge really sit to the water?

The deck at the low span is 5.5 metres above sea level, which is generally counted as the closest any bridge sits to the ocean surface. The bridge is 2.2 kilometres long, uses a fish-belly girder with elliptical piers, is painted white throughout, and opened on 6 September 2003 at a construction cost of 560 million yuan.

Is the Zhongshan Road pedestrian street the whole road?

No. The western section became a full pedestrian street in September 2006 and the eastern section followed in September 2010, with the western night-time pedestrian hours fixed by season at 18:30 to 22:00 in summer and autumn and 18:00 to 21:30 in spring and winter. The route itself has also been shortened at the eastern end twice, first to the Gucheng crossroads in 2012 and then to the Xiaxi junction in 2020.

What is at the fort with the big gun?

The 280 millimetre coastal gun at Huli Mountain Fort was cast by the German Krupp works in 1894 and is identified as the largest surviving nineteenth century coastal gun in the world, confirmed by appraisal by the national cultural heritage authority together with the Krupp historical archive. The fort entered the fourth group of national key cultural relics in 1996 and the Guinness Book of Records in 2000. The 1841 granite wall nearby runs 1,667 metres, 3.3 metres high and 2.6 metres thick, and had 100 guns mounted on it.

Can the university campus be photographed?

Xiamen University was founded on 6 April 1921 by the overseas Chinese businessman Tan Kah Kee and was the first university in modern Chinese history founded by overseas Chinese; it became a national university in July 1937 and is a Double First Class, former Project 985 and former Project 211 institution, with over 45,000 students enrolled as of April 2023. It sits directly beside Nanputuo Temple and runs down to the Baicheng beach at the foot of Yanwu Bridge, so the campus, the temple and the bridge form a single walk.
