Name Origin

Luoyang means "north of the Luo River" in Chinese. It was the capital of 13 dynasties and one of the cradles of Chinese civilization.

Education & Universities

Henan University of Science and Technology, Luoyang Normal University, and Luoyang Institute of Science and Technology are the main institutions.

Getting Around

Luoyang Beijiao Airport (LYA) has domestic flights. Luoyang Railway Station and Luoyang Longmen Station connect to Beijing, Xi'an, and Zhengzhou on the Zhengzhou–Xi'an high-speed railway. The city has two metro lines (Lines 1 and 2).

Natural Resources & Environment

Luoyang lies in the western Henan plain surrounded by mountains. It has a temperate continental climate, the Yi and Luo rivers, and the nearby Yellow River.

Top Luoyang Attractions

Visit the Longmen Grottoes (UNESCO), Shaolin Temple (near Dengfeng), White Horse Temple, Luoyang Old City, and the Peony Festival in spring.

Food & Shopping in Luoyang

What to eat and what to bring home from Luoyang

🍽️ Must-Try Luoyang Cuisine

Try Luoyang water banquet (shuixi), beef soup, peony cake, Luoyang pancakes, and Henan-style noodles.

🛍️ Shopping in Luoyang

Buy peony-themed souvenirs, Tang dynasty replicas, Tang Sancai pottery, and local snacks. Main shopping is on Xigong District.

🍵 Tea Culture

Traditional Luoyang teahouses serve loose-leaf tea in covered bowls, and the regional green teas are the usual house pour. Your driver can point you to a teahouse that specialists use.

🚗 Private Car for a Food Crawl

Luoyang Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Luoyang like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

UNESCO is the sight everyone should build the day around. Ask your driver to arrive early, when the coaches have not arrived yet, and to wait nearby rather than circling the car park.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Luoyang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Luoyang

🍵 Tea Houses of Luoyang

Tea culture runs through the day in Luoyang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
