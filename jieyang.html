Name Origin of Jieyang

Jieyang means "sunrise in the east." It is the cultural center of the Chaoshan (Teochew) region in eastern Guangdong.

Jieyang Education & Universities

Jieyang University and Jieyang Vocational and Technical College are the main institutions.

Getting Around Jieyang

Jieyang Chaoshan International Airport (SWA) serves flights. Jieyang Railway Station connects to Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and Xiamen on the Xiamen–Shenzhen railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Jieyang Natural Resources & Environment

Jieyang lies in the Chaoshan Plain with subtropical monsoon climate, rivers, wetlands, and South China Sea coastline.

Jieyang Local Secrets

Insider tips to help you experience Jieyang like a local

🎭 Start at the landmark

The main sight of Jieyang rewards an early start, before the tour coaches arrive. Ask your driver to wait close by so you can return to the car at your own pace.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Eat where the queues are shortest: the morning market and the small noodle shops a block back from the main square usually feed locals at a fraction of the tourist-restaurant price.

⏱️ Plan the driving time

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Jieyang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Jieyang

🍵 Tea Houses of Jieyang

Tea culture runs through the day in Jieyang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
