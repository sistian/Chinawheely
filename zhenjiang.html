Name Origin

Zhenjiang means "guarding the river" in Chinese. It is located in southern Jiangsu on the Yangtze River and was historically a strategic military location.

Education & Universities

Jiangsu University and Jiangsu University of Science and Technology are main institutions.

Getting Around

Zhenjiang has no commercial airport; Nanjing Lukou Airport is the nearest. Zhenjiang Railway Station and Zhenjiang South Station connect to Nanjing and Shanghai on the Shanghai–Nanjing high-speed railway. Buses and taxis serve the city.

Natural Resources & Environment

Zhenjiang lies on the southern bank of the Yangtze River with a humid subtropical climate. It has mountains, rivers, and is famous for its vinegar.

🥢 Skip the tourist menu

Allow extra time between the airport or main station and the old town — the city spreads out, and the distance that looks short on a map can take half an hour in traffic.

Tea Culture & Teahouse Guide in Zhenjiang

Where to sit down for a bowl of tea in Zhenjiang

🍵 Tea Houses of Zhenjiang

Tea culture runs through the day in Zhenjiang: loose-leaf houses serve the region's own greens in covered bowls, and the older teahouses around the old town are where the city's social life happens.

🫖 A Gongfu Tasting

Most visitors try a gongfu-style tasting session — a host brews one tea at a time and explains how the leaves open — and pair it with the local snacks sold in the same establishments.

💡 Tea Etiquette Tips

Ask your driver for the teahouse that locals favour rather than the loudest one on the tourist route; the best rooms are usually a short walk from the market, and a quiet hour there is the cheapest and most rewarding way to understand the city's pace.
