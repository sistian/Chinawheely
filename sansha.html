Name Origin

The name "Sansha" (三沙) literally means "Three Sandy Islands" - referring to the three island groups that comprise the city: Xisha Islands (西沙群岛, Paracel Islands), Nansha Islands (南沙群岛, Spratly Islands), and Zhongsha Islands (中沙群岛, Zhongsha Islands). Established as a prefecture-level city in 2012, Sansha is China southernmost municipal administration. The administrative headquarters is on Yongxing Island (永兴岛) in the Xisha Islands - the largest of the Paracel Islands.

Culture & Heritage

Sansha represents China maritime frontier in the South China Sea. The Xisha Islands have been inhabited by Chinese fishermen for centuries - traditional Beikeng (南海耕海) fishing culture runs deep in the communities. Yongxing Island has a naval garrison atmosphere with a small civilian population of administrative and support staff. The islands have excellent coral reef ecosystems and are of significant strategic and geopolitical importance in the South China Sea disputes.

Transportation

Sansha is only accessible by boat from Hainan Island. There is no commercial airport on the islands. The regular passenger boat (琼沙3号) departs from Sanya and Haikou ports to Yongxing Island - the journey takes about 15-18 hours from Sanya and 18-20 hours from Haikou. Government and charter vessels also operate from Wenchang (文昌) on Hainan east coast. Our private car service covers transfers from Sanya Airport (SYX) or Haikou Meilan Airport (HKK) to the boat ports.

Geography & Environment

Sansha spans the entire South China Sea jurisdiction - an area of approximately 2 million km2 including hundreds of islands, reefs, and shoals. The islands are tropical - hot and humid year-round. Yongxing Island (永兴岛) is the largest, about 3.2 km long with coconut palms,蔬菜种植, and a small settlement. The surrounding waters have world-class coral reefs, marine biodiversity, and diving sites. The climate is tropical oceanic with typhoon risks (May-November).

Top Sansha Attractions

Tropical South China Sea islands - coral reefs, marine life, and China maritime frontier at its most remote and beautiful.

🏝️ Yongxing Island (永兴岛)

Administrative Center - Tropical Island Capital

Yongxing Island (永兴岛) is Sansha administrative headquarters - the largest of the Paracel Islands at 3.2 km long. The island has coconut palms, a small town with administrative offices, a hospital, school, and fishing port. The island is a military-civilian settlement with a unique frontier atmosphere. The surrounding lagoon has excellent coral and is popular for snorkeling.

🏝️ Xisha Islands Coral Reefs

World-Class Diving - Pristine Reefs

The Xisha Islands (西沙群岛) have some of the most pristine coral reefs in the South China Sea. The water is exceptionally clear (visibility 30+ meters) and the reefs support extraordinary marine biodiversity - coral gardens, tropical fish, sea turtles, and rays. Diving trips to the Xisha Islands depart from Sanya and Wenchang on Hainan Island.

🏯 North Reef (七连屿)

Seven Islands - Bird Sanctuary

North Reef (七连屿) is a string of seven coral islands and reefs north of Yongxing Island, known for its bird sanctuary. The islands are covered in sea birds and have pristine white sand beaches surrounded by turquoise lagoon water. Access is by boat from Yongxing Island. The reef is considered one of the most beautiful spots in the Paracel Islands.

🍜 Yongxing Port Fishing Market

Frontier Port - Fresh South China Sea Catch

The Yongxing Island fishing port (永兴岛渔港) is where the South China Sea fishing fleet unloads its catch. The market sells the freshest possible seafood - tropical fish, lobster, crab, sea cucumber, and abalone - all from the rich waters of the South China Sea. The port area also has small restaurants serving grilled fresh fish.

🏝️ Phoenix Island (琛航岛)

Military Memorial - Paracel War Heritage

Phoenix Island (琛航岛) hosts the Paracel Islands Martyrs Cemetery (西沙海战烈士陵园), commemorating the 1974 Xisha (Paracel) Islands battle between China and South Vietnam. The memorial honors the Chinese naval personnel who died in the conflict that secured Chinese control of the Paracel Islands. A place of significant historical and patriotic significance.

🌊 South China Sea Museum

Maritime History & Marine Environment

The South China Sea Museum (南海博物馆) on Yongxing Island displays the history of Chinese activity in the South China Sea, including artifacts from ancient shipwrecks, marine biology exhibits, and historical documents relating to Chinese sovereignty claims. The museum provides important context for understanding the significance of the Sansha jurisdiction.

Getting to Sansha: How to Reach the South China Sea Islands

Sansha is only accessible by boat from Hainan Island - no airport on the islands.

How to Reach Sansha (Yongxing Island)

🌊 Step 1: Fly to Sanya (SYX) or Haikou (HKK)

Fly to Hainan Island first

Book a flight to either Sanya Phoenix International Airport (SYX) or Haikou Meilan International Airport (HKK). Sanya is closer to the boat departure port - the drive to the Sanya ferry terminal is only 30 minutes. Haikou has more flight options and a 1.5-hour drive to Wenchang port.

🏝️ Step 2: Take the Ferry to Yongxing Island

Boat journey: Sanya 15-18h / Wenchang 12-14h

The passenger ferry (琼沙3号) operates from Sanya and Wenchang ports to Yongxing Island. Tickets must be booked in advance through official channels - the boat is popular and often fully booked weeks ahead. The journey takes 15-18 hours from Sanya and 12-14 hours from Wenchang. Cabin class is recommended for the overnight journey.

Nearest Airports (Hainan Island)

🏯 Sanya Phoenix Airport (SYX)

15 km to Sanya ferry port - 30 min by road

SYX serves domestic flights and some international routes. Closer to the Sanya ferry terminal for boats to Yongxing Island. Less flight options than Haikou but convenient. Our driver meets you at the airport and transfers you directly to the ferry port.

🌊 Haikou Meilan Airport (HKK)

90 km to Wenchang port - 1.5 hours by road

HKK is Hainan main aviation hub with more flight options. Drive to Wenchang port for boats to Yongxing Island. Wenchang is famous for Wenchang chicken - a good excuse for a lunch stop on the way to the port.

Best Photo Spots in Sansha

Crystal-clear South China Sea waters, coral reefs, and tropical island frontier at China southernmost edge.

🏝️ Yongxing Island Lagoon

Best with underwater camera

The lagoon surrounding Yongxing Island has exceptionally clear water with coral formations visible from the surface. On calm days the turquoise lagoon is most beautiful. Aerial photography (if permitted) captures the full island lagoon system best. Snorkeling provides the closest view of the coral gardens.

🏯 North Reef at Low Tide

Best at low tide

North Reef (七连屿) has vast areas of reef exposed at low tide - creating extraordinary tidal pools filled with sea life. The white sand bars between the reef sections are most visible at low tide. The bird sanctuary islands are covered in sea birds - visible from the boat approach.

🌊 Sunset over the South China Sea

Best at golden hour

The South China Sea sunset from Yongxing Island is dramatic - the sky and sea turn orange and gold with no land in any direction to interrupt the view. The palm trees on the island make excellent silhouettes. Best viewed from the north side of the island facing the open sea.

🎭 Yongxing Port Fishing Catch

Best early morning

The fishing port at Yongxing Island is most active in the early morning when the overnight fishing boats unload their catch. The display of tropical fish, lobster, and sea cucumber is colorful and unique to this frontier port. The surrounding fishing boats and port activity make good subjects.

🌲 Coral Reef Snorkeling

Best in the morning

The Xisha Islands coral reefs are best snorkeled in the morning when the water is calmest and visibility is highest (30+ meters). The coral gardens near Yongxing Island have brain coral, table coral, and anemones with clownfish. Bring an underwater camera - the marine life is extraordinary.

🏯 Paracel Martyrs Cemetery

Best in quiet morning

The Paracel Islands Martyrs Cemetery (琛航岛) is a solemn and photogenic site. The neat rows of graves against the tropical island background create a striking image. The site is best visited early in the morning when it is quiet and the light is soft.

Food & Shopping in Sansha

Must-Try South China Sea Cuisine

South China Sea Grouper (南海石斑)

Fresh grouper from the South China Sea - cooked simply with ginger, scallion, and soy sauce to highlight the natural sweetness of the fish. Available at the Yongxing Island port restaurants. The fish is so fresh it is often still twitching when served.

Steamed Lobster (清蒸龙虾)

Fresh lobster from the South China Sea - steamed with garlic and scallion. The clear waters around the Xisha Islands produce excellent lobster with firm, sweet meat. Available at port restaurants and some accommodation on Yongxing Island.

Sea Cucumber (海参)

South China Sea sea cucumber - a prized delicacy. Usually braised or stir-fried with vegetables. High in protein and considered a luxury ingredient. Available at port restaurants and a popular souvenir item for those with the means to transport it.

Wenchang Chicken (文昌鸡)

If traveling via Wenchang before boarding the boat to Sansha, Wenchang chicken is essential. The free-range chicken raised on coconut and grain has delicate, flavorful meat. Many consider it the best chicken in China. Best at the chicken restaurants in Wenchang town.

Fresh Abalone (鲜鲍鱼)

South China Sea abalone - tender and sweet when stir-fried or steamed. Available at Yongxing Island port market and restaurants. The abalone here is considered premium quality due to the clean ocean environment of the Xisha Islands.

What to Buy in Sansha

Dried Seafood from the South China Sea

Dried fish, dried shrimp, and dried squid from the South China Sea are popular souvenirs. Available at the Yongxing Island port market and Hainan markets. Lightweight and keep well for travel. The flavor of sea-dried fish from these clean waters is exceptional.

Coral and Shell Crafts

Shell and coral crafts are available at Hainan markets (including in Sanya and Haikou) for those who have visited the Sansha area. Be aware of CITES regulations if purchasing coral - some species are protected. Look for shell-based items rather than coral.

Hainanese Tea (兴隆咖啡)

Hainan produces excellent coffee at the Xinglong (兴隆) plantations near Haikou. The local Arabica coffee grown on volcanic soil is smooth and low-acid. Available at supermarkets and specialty shops throughout Hainan - a practical and delicious souvenir.

Medical History & Wellness Heritage of Sansha

South China Sea marine medicine, tropical island wellness, and frontier garrison health traditions.

South China Sea Marine Medicine

The South China Sea waters around the Xisha Islands have been a source of traditional marine medicine for centuries. 海参 (sea cucumber, haishen) from these waters is used in TCM for its exceptional nutritional and medicinal properties - associated with joint health, immune support, and longevity. 海马 (seahorse) from the South China Sea is used in TCM for kidney and respiratory health. The clean, unpolluted waters of the Paracel Islands produce high-quality marine medicinal products.

Available at the Yongxing Island port market and Hainan TCM shops.

Tropical Island Climate Therapy

The tropical maritime climate of the Xisha Islands - warm year-round, humid, with abundant sunshine and sea air - has therapeutic properties. The negative ions from the ocean spray, the consistent warmth, and the high UV levels (moderate sun exposure) are associated with skin health and mood improvement. The coral reef environment produces oxygen-rich air. The island environment is generally beneficial for respiratory and skin conditions.

The island is most pleasant in the morning and evening - avoid midday sun.

Traditional Fishing Health Culture

The traditional Beikeng (南海耕海) fishing culture of the South China Sea involves physical labor, sea air, and a fish-rich diet. Fishermen on these waters traditionally had strong physiques and long working lives despite demanding conditions. The diet - rich in omega-3 fatty acids from fresh fish, combined with physical activity - is associated with cardiovascular health. This traditional lifestyle is referenced in discussions of longevity in island fishing communities.

Fresh South China Sea fish is best for health at Yongxing Island port restaurants.

Coral Reef Therapeutic Environment

The coral reef environment of the Xisha Islands has been studied for its therapeutic properties. Coral reefs produce certain bioactive compounds with potential pharmaceutical applications. The reef ecosystem, with its high biodiversity and clean water, creates an environment associated with mental wellness - the "blue mind" effect of being near marine environments. Snorkeling and diving in the crystal-clear reef waters are considered beneficial for stress reduction.

Best experienced in the morning when water clarity is highest.

Sansha Local Secrets

Insider tips for visiting China southernmost frontier city.

Book Boat Tickets Weeks in Advance

The passenger ferry to Yongxing Island (琼沙3号) is very popular and often fully booked. Book your boat ticket at least 2-3 weeks in advance through the official Sansha government ticketing system. Our driver can help with airport to port transfer once your boat schedule is confirmed.

Choose a Cabin Class Ticket

The ferry journey to Yongxing Island takes 12-18 hours overnight. Cabin class (舱位) tickets provide air-conditioned cabins with bunks - much more comfortable than the open deck. Book cabin class if available. Bring motion sickness medication if susceptible to seasickness - the South China Sea can be rough.

Stop for Wenchang Chicken on the Way

If departing from Haikou to Wenchang port for the boat, stop in Wenchang town for the famous Wenchang chicken (文昌鸡). The chicken is considered the best in China - tender, flavorful, and served with dipping sauce. Best at the chicken restaurants near Wenchang old town.

Bring Reef-Safe Sunscreen

The tropical sun at the Xisha Islands is intense year-round. Use reef-safe sunscreen (无氧苯甲酸酯) to protect both your skin and the coral reef ecosystem. Regular sunscreen chemicals are harmful to coral - reef-safe formulas are strongly encouraged. Wear a hat and UV-protective clothing.

Visit in Winter (November-March)

The best time to visit Sansha is November through March when the weather is drier, the sea is calmer (better for boat travel), and there is no typhoon risk. Summer (June-September) has rough seas, higher humidity, and typhoon risk. The winter months offer the most reliable access and most comfortable conditions.

Eat Fresh Fish at the Port Market

The best meal in Sansha is at the Yongxing Island fishing port market. Fresh fish, lobster, and abalone are grilled to order by small stalls near the port. The seafood does not get any fresher - the fish may have been swimming hours earlier. Go for lunch when the morning catch has just arrived.

Best Time to Visit Sansha

Tropical oceanic climate - hot and humid year-round with distinct dry and wet seasons and typhoon risks.

Winter

Nov-Mar - 22-28C - Best Season

The best time to visit Sansha. Dry season with calm seas, lower humidity, and no typhoon risk. The boat journey is most reliable and comfortable. Clear skies and excellent visibility for diving and snorkeling. Peak season for visits - book boat tickets well in advance.

Highlights: Best weather, calm seas, clearest water, diving peak

Spring

Apr-May - 25-30C

Warm and increasingly humid. The water temperature is comfortable for swimming and diving. Boat services are still reliable. Late May marks the beginning of the typhoon season - monitor weather conditions. Shoulder season with moderate tourist numbers.

Highlights: Warm water, fewer crowds, diving still good

Summer

Jun-Oct - 28-34C - Typhoon Season

Hot, humid, with typhoon risk (May-November). The South China Sea can be very rough in summer - boat services are occasionally suspended during typhoons. This is low season - fewer visitors and lower prices. Not recommended for those with limited time due to weather uncertainty.

Highlights: Lowest prices, fewer tourists, risk of rough seas

Autumn

Sep-Nov - 25-30C - Transition

September still has typhoon risk. Conditions improve in October and November as the typhoon season ends. November marks the start of the best season. Shoulder month - boat services becoming more reliable and weather improving. A good compromise between summer prices and winter reliability.

Highlights: Improving weather, transitional prices
