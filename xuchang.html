Name Origin

The name "Xuchang" (许昌) means "Xu Prefecture." The city served as the Eastern Han capital from 196 AD when the warlord Cao Cao welcomed the child Emperor Xian here, making Xuchang one of the most important cities in Chinese Three Kingdoms history.

Education & Culture

Xuchang is home to Henan University of Science and Technology and vocational colleges specializing in Jun porcelain craftsmanship. The city is also known as Jun City (钧都) for its world-famous Jun porcelain.

Transportation

Xuchang does not have its own commercial airport. The nearest major airport is Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport (CGO), about 60 km away — approximately 1 hour by road. Xuchang is served by Xuchang East Railway Station on the Zhengzhou-Wuhan HSR corridor.

Natural Environment

Xuchang is located in central Henan Province on the North China Plain, with fertile farmland surrounding the city. The region has a temperate continental climate with four distinct seasons — hot summers and cold winters.

Top Xuchang Attractions

From Three Kingdoms palaces to thousand-year-old porcelain kilns.

Cao Cao Government Office (丞相府)

Three Kingdoms - Cao Cao Historic Site

The best-preserved Three Kingdoms site in China — the government office where Cao Cao administered the Eastern Han Empire from 196-220 AD. Features reconstructed halls, historical exhibits, and the famous "Three Visits to the Thatched Cottage" story. Located in downtown Xuchang.

Jun Porcelain Industrial Park

Jun Kiln - 1000-Year Porcelain Tradition

Xuchang is Jun City (钧都) — birthplace of Jun porcelain (钧瓷), one of China five famous porcelain types. Watch throwing, glazing, and reduction firing. Open to visitors for factory tours and direct purchase.

Wei Emperor Palace (文帝宫)

Three Kingdoms - Cao Pi Shrine

The shrine to Cao Pi, son of Cao Cao, who established the Wei State (220-265 AD) as China first of the Three Kingdoms. The palace complex includes ancient halls, stone inscriptions, and a museum of Three Kingdoms artifacts.

Xuchang Old City Walls

Ming Dynasty - Historic City Walls

Preserved Ming Dynasty city walls originally built in 1370 and partially restored. About 5 km in circumference with historic gates. Walk the walls at sunset for views over the city.

Zhou Enlai Drama Troupe Site

Modern History - Cultural Heritage

A well-preserved compound where the young Zhou Enlai (later China first Premier) worked with a drama troupe in 1938 during the Anti-Japanese War. Preserves the rehearsal halls and living quarters.

Tianning Temple (天宁寺)

Tang Dynasty - Buddhist Heritage

One of the oldest Buddhist temples in Henan, originally built during the Tang Dynasty (618-907 AD). Features a nine-story pagoda (九层塔) that was once the tallest structure in Xuchang.

Getting to Xuchang: Airports & Railway Stations

Xuchang has no airport. Here is how to reach Xuchang from the nearest major hubs.

Nearest Airports

Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport (CGO)

60 km to Xuchang - 1 hour by road

CGO is Henan Province main aviation hub. It has two terminals and serves flights to most major Chinese cities and international destinations. The drive to Xuchang via the S1 Airport Expressway and G4 takes approximately 1 hour.

No Local Airport in Xuchang

Xuchang city itself has no commercial airport

The closest is Zhengzhou Xinzheng (CGO) at 60 km. High-speed rail from Zhengzhou to Xuchang takes just 20 minutes, but involves luggage and city transfers — our door-to-door car is more convenient.

High-speed hub - Eastern district

The main high-speed rail station on the Zhengzhou-Wuhan corridor. From here, Zhengzhou is 20 minutes by HSR, Luoyang is 45 minutes, and Wuhan is 2.5 hours. The station is about 5 km from the city center.

Serves conventional (non-high-speed) trains within Henan and to surrounding provinces. Located near the city center, convenient for central Xuchang destinations.

Xuchang Best Photo Spots

Capture Three Kingdoms capital and Jun City from its most scenic angles.

Cao Cao Government Office at Dusk

Best at sunset

The historic government office complex is beautifully lit at dusk, with traditional eaves silhouetted against the orange sky. The reconstructed Han Dynasty architecture creates dramatic photo opportunities.

Jun Porcelain Glaze Patterns

Best with natural light

The iridescent ruyi cloud patterns and mangxiao crystalline glaze effects of Jun porcelain are best photographed with natural light. The factory workshops with their wood-fired kilns create atmospheric shots.

Old City Walls Panorama

Best in morning

The eastern section of Xuchang old city walls offers a panoramic view of the old city layout — traditional grid of streets, tiled rooftops, and the Tianning Temple pagoda in the background.

Wei Emperor Palace Interior

Best with museum lighting

The stone inscriptions, ancient stone carvings, and reconstructed halls of the Wei Emperor Palace are best photographed with the museum interior lighting.

Jun Porcelain Market

Best in morning

The Jun porcelain wholesale market in Xuchang has rows of shops displaying thousands of pieces — the colorful glazes create a vibrant photo opportunity.

Tianning Temple Pagoda

Best in morning or evening

The nine-story pagoda of Tianning Temple is Xuchang most iconic structure. The temple grounds with incense smoke and prayer flags create a spiritual atmosphere for photography.

Food & Shopping in Xuchang

Must-Try Xuchang & Henan Cuisine

Roast Chicken

Xuchang has its own distinctive roast chicken tradition — whole chicken roasted until the skin is crispy and golden, served chopped with a savory dipping sauce. Look for small family-run shops near the old city walls.

Steamed Soup Buns

Henan is famous for its steamed buns. The Xuchang style has particularly thin skins and rich pork broth inside. Best at morning dim sum restaurants near the old city market.

Spicy Flat Noodles

Wide hand-pulled noodles in a savory spicy broth with lamb, bean sprouts, and chili oil. A warming Henan specialty — the wide noodles are chewy and the broth has gentle heat.

Walnut Crisps

A Xuchang specialty — crisp pastry filled with crushed walnuts and sugar. Originally made for the imperial court, now a popular local snack sold at traditional bakeries near the old city.

Shopping Destinations

Jun Porcelain Industrial Park

The primary destination for buying authentic Jun porcelain. Dozens of workshops and showrooms — prices range from a few dollars for small pieces to thousands for museum-quality works.

Sanpianxia Old City Market

Xuchang old city traditional market near the Three Kingdoms district. Sells local snacks, dried foods, small porcelain pieces, and local crafts.

What to Buy in Xuchang

Jun Porcelain (钧瓷)

One of China five famous porcelain types. Look for pieces with the characteristic iridescent ruyi cloud glazes — blues, purples, and reds created through copper-reduction firing. Small pieces start from ten dollars; museum-quality works cost thousands.

Three Kingdoms Memorabilia

Small porcelain figures of Cao Cao, Liu Bei, and Zhuge Liang, as well as Three Kingdoms-themed calligraphy scrolls. Available at the Cao Cao Government Office gift shop and the Sanpianxia market.

Jun Kiln Replica Vases

Smaller replica pieces of classic Jun porcelain vase shapes — meiping, guande, and lu — make elegant souvenirs. Available at factory shops at the Jun Porcelain Industrial Park.

Medical History & TCM Heritage of Xuchang

Discover the traditional medicine heritage of Three Kingdoms capital and central Henan.

Hua Tuo and the Origins of Surgery

China legendary surgeon. Hua Tuo (145-207 AD), one of China most famous physicians, served at the imperial court during the late Eastern Han Dynasty and is associated with the Xuchang area. Hua Tuo is credited with inventing mafeisan (麻沸散), an herbal anesthetic used during surgery — making him one of the first surgeons in world history to use general anesthesia.

Hua Tuo temples and shrines throughout China honor his medical contributions.

Zhang Zhongjing and the Treatise

Henan medical master. Zhang Zhongjing (150-219 AD), another legendary physician of the late Han/Three Kingdoms period associated with Henan, wrote the Shang Han Za Bing Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage and Miscellaneous Diseases), one of the foundational texts of Traditional Chinese Medicine.

His formulas remain the basis of TCM diagnosis and treatment today.

Henan TCM Pharmacology

Central Chinese medicine traditions. Henan Province is a major production area for TCM herbs — the fertile North China Plain produces high-quality Chinese yam (山药), rehmannia (地黄), and lotus seed (莲子) used in TCM formulas throughout China.

Available at traditional TCM pharmacies along the old city streets of Xuchang.

Jun Porcelain Health Ceramics

Mineral-rich Jun glaze wellness. Jun porcelain is made from locally mined porcelain stone with unique mineral properties. Traditional belief holds that water stored in Jun porcelain vessels has beneficial health properties due to the mineral leaching into the water.

Modern wellness products incorporating Jun glaze particles are sold at the industrial park.

Best Time to Visit Xuchang

Each season offers a different side of Three Kingdoms capital.

Spring

Mar-Apr - 10-22C

Pleasant temperatures for walking the old city walls and Three Kingdoms sites. Apricot blossoms around Tianning Temple add color to photos.

Highlights: City wall walks, temple visits, comfortable weather

Summer

May-Aug - 22-35C

Hot and humid summer. Early morning visits to outdoor sites recommended. Summer is the best time for porcelain factory visits as the kilns run frequently.

Highlights: Porcelain factory visits, evening markets

Autumn

Sep-Nov - 10-25C

The best overall season. Clear skies, comfortable temperatures, and the best conditions for photography at all outdoor sites.

Highlights: Photography weather, porcelain firing season

Winter

Dec-Feb - -3 to 8C

Cold but quiet. Indoor sites — the Cao Cao Government Office museum and Jun porcelain workshops — are at their best in winter.

Highlights: Museum visits, porcelain workshops
