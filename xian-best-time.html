Seasons Overview

The climate record of Xian.

The annual average temperature is 13.7 °C; annual rainfall averages 561 mm; the climate pattern includes The monsoon, drought, dust storms, flooding.

☀️

Summer

The warmest month is July, when the average high sits near 27 °C. Expect long days, heavier traffic and the busiest sightseeing hours.

❄️

Winter

The coldest month is January, averaging 0.3 °C. It is the quietest season for sightseeing and the easiest time to drive between sights.

🌸

Spring and autumn

Across the year the average temperature is 13.7 °C, with annual rainfall of 561 mm — the shoulder seasons are the most comfortable for walking between landmarks.

🌦️

What to expect

The climate pattern includes The monsoon, drought, dust storms, flooding. Weather changes with the seasons, so a private car with a local driver is the practical way to keep a plan on track.
